## B — Fitness to drive

Audience: first-time Kansas non-commercial Class C applicants studying Fitness to drive.
The selected scope excludes motorcycle and commercial licensing; those require their own manuals [A11].
Prerequisites and sequence: Understand that vision, alertness, attention, substances, illness and emotions can each change fitness for driving. This is an editorial teaching order, with no claimed official domain weights.
