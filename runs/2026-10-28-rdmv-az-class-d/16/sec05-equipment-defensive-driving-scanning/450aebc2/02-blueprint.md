## A — Exam authority for the remaining equipment, defensive driving, distractions, steering and scanning section (Arizona Class D operator license, written test)
Issuer: the Arizona Department of Transportation Motor Vehicle Division (ADOT MVD) provides this manual as its guide to Arizona traffic laws and to obtaining a driver license or identification card [A05].
Source document: Arizona Driver License Manual and Customer Service Guide, whose contents page carries the line Revised September 2026 [A03].
Purpose: the manual explains Arizona motor vehicle laws without technical language to help readers obtain a driver license or instruction permit, and refers to Arizona Revised Statutes Title 28 for the most current law [A03].
Written test: MVD issues an Arizona instruction permit only after the applicant passes the Division written test; commercial learner permits are excluded from this rule [A01].

Misconceptions: the horn and rear-mirror distances are not two different numbers, both are the same distance; hazard flashers are for a stopped vehicle, while flashing red lights on the front are banned for ordinary vehicles even when disabled; a quick text is not an exception, since the only use the manual allows while driving is reporting an emergency, and anything else means pulling off and stopping first; buckling up is a distraction if you do it while moving, so fasten the belt before you start.
Mnemonics: Hold nothing, hands on the wheel — talk hands-free or stop; in a curve, eyes to the outside edge.
Glossary: whiplash means a neck injury from the head snapping back and forth, usually in a rear-end crash; muffler cut-out means a device that lets exhaust skip the muffler; foresight means sizing up traffic early enough to act; voice-to-text means dictating a message without touching the device.
Dynamic facts: the legal window-tint limits are not printed in the manual and must come from Arizona law, and the wireless-device rules are set by statute that can be amended, so confirm current wording on azdot.gov before teaching any detail beyond these pages.
