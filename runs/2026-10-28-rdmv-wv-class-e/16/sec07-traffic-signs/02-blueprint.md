This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination. The issuing authority is the West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles within the West Virginia Department of Transportation, and the source is the current revised edition of the West Virginia Driver's Licensing Handbook pinned in the run configuration. Section Seven covers the meaning of sign shapes and colors: the single-message shapes from octagon to pentagon, square and rectangular regulatory signs, diamond warning signs, construction and maintenance signs with channeling devices, arrow panels and flaggers, and guide signs with their color families and route markers.
## B — Audience, Section Seven Traffic signs

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination, reading at a plain-English level with no prior driving knowledge assumed. Section Seven teaches how to read a sign from its shape and color alone and what each family of signs requires of the driver. It depends on the definitions chapter for right-of-way and no other chapter. The rules progress from single-message shapes, through regulatory and warning signs, to work zone devices and guide signs.
