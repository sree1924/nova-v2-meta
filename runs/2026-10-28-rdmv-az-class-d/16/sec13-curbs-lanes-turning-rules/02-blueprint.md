Misconceptions: a white curb is for passengers, not for loading freight, and a yellow curb requires you to stay with the car; a double solid white line may never be crossed even though both sides carry traffic going the same way; the right-edge solid white line is crossed only for an emergency or a hazard; turning left against a red light is allowed only from a one-way road onto another one-way road.
Mnemonics: White curb, people in and out; yellow curb, goods in and out with you beside the car; red curb, never stop.
Glossary: reversible lane means a lane whose travel direction changes by time of day; high occupancy vehicle lane means a lane reserved for vehicles carrying more than one person, entered and left across a solid white line; swinging wide means drifting away from the curb before or during a turn.
Dynamic facts: reversible-lane schedules, posted turn prohibitions and which intersections allow turns from more than one lane are set locally and can change at any time.
