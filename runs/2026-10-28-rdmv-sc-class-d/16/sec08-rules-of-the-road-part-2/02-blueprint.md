Misconceptions: a speed limit is the maximum for ideal conditions, not a speed that is always safe; a yield sign means slowing and giving way, not always stopping; the shared center left turn lane is never a passing lane; when the yellow center lines pair a dashed line with a solid one, only the driver on the dashed side may pass; a missing crosswalk does not remove the duty to stop before the intersection.
Mnemonics: white regulates, green, brown or blue guides; eight sides for stop, point-down triangle for yield; on the S road marker, county first, road last.
Glossary: regulatory sign means a sign stating a law you must obey; guide sign means a sign giving route, place or service information; secondary system road means a local road numbered by county; stop line means the painted line where you stop for a sign or signal; pavement marking means a line, arrow, word or symbol painted on the road.
