Misconceptions: quick sober-up methods and eating do not sober anyone up, only time does; beer is not a weaker choice, a standard beer holds as much alcohol as a shot or a glass of wine; believing you are under the legal limit does not mean alcohol is leaving your driving alone; a legal over-the-counter medicine can still impair driving.
Mnemonics: point-oh-eight for adults, point-oh-one under twenty-one; twice the risk just over point-oh-five, six times at point-one, twenty-five times at point-one-five; the impaired-driver signs as speeding, weaving, slow, jerky and quick stops.
Glossary: BAC means blood alcohol concentration, the share of alcohol in the blood; IDRC means Intoxicated Driver Resource Center; designated driver means the person who stays sober to drive others home; ignition interlock means a device that blocks starting the vehicle after drinking; peripheral vision means side vision.
Dynamic facts: the refusal penalties, surcharges and campaign website can change; confirm them on official New Jersey websites.
