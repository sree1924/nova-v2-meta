## A — Exam authority, Traffic signs, route markers and sign colors

This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the regular passenger-car written knowledge and road sign tests in Michigan. The issuing authority is the Michigan Department of State, led by the Secretary of State, and the source is the handbook What Every Driver Must Know. This section covers the nine basic sign shapes, route markers, the meaning of sign colors, regulatory signs, the traffic sign challenge with the meaning of each pictured regulatory, warning and guide sign, and the opening pavement marking rules for white lines. The handbook pages do not state an official question count, passing score, test format or time limit for the Secretary of State knowledge test, so this dossier asserts no value for any of them and records each as an explicit unknown. Practice scoring follows only the accepted LearningLoop product policy, never an official threshold.
## B — Audience, Traffic signs, route markers and sign colors

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants for a Michigan operator's license, reading at a plain-English level, plus newcomers and parents of teen drivers who use the same handbook. This section covers the nine basic sign shapes, route markers, the meaning of sign colors, regulatory signs, the traffic sign challenge with the meaning of each pictured regulatory, warning and guide sign, and the opening pavement marking rules for white lines. It teaches how to read a sign from its shape and color and what each common regulatory, warning and guide sign tells the driver to do. It assumes no prior driving knowledge and no prerequisites beyond literacy, so wording stays concrete, procedural and free of legal jargon throughout.
