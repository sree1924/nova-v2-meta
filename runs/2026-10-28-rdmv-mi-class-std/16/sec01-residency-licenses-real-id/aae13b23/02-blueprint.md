## A — Exam authority, Residency, licenses and REAL ID

This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the regular passenger-car written knowledge and road sign tests in Michigan. The issuing authority is the Michigan Department of State, led by the Secretary of State, and the source is the handbook What Every Driver Must Know. This section covers Michigan residency, licensing requirements, the license types with their group designators and endorsements, REAL ID, and the steps for drivers who move to Michigan. The handbook pages do not state an official question count, passing score, test format or time limit for the Secretary of State knowledge test, so this dossier asserts no value for any of them and records each as an explicit unknown. Practice scoring follows only the accepted LearningLoop product policy, never an official threshold.
## B — Audience, Residency, licenses and REAL ID

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants for a Michigan operator's license, reading at a plain-English level, plus newcomers and parents of teen drivers who use the same handbook. This section covers Michigan residency, licensing requirements, the license types with their group designators and endorsements, REAL ID, and the steps for drivers who move to Michigan. It teaches who may hold a Michigan license, which license or endorsement fits which vehicle, how REAL ID works, and how newcomers convert or test into a Michigan license. It assumes no prior driving knowledge and no prerequisites beyond literacy, so wording stays concrete, procedural and free of legal jargon throughout.
