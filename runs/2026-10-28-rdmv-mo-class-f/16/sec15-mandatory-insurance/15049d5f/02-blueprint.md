## A — Exam authority for the mandatory insurance section (Missouri Class F operator license written test)
Issuer: the Missouri Department of Revenue publishes the Missouri Driver Guide [A01].
Source document: Missouri Driver Guide, with the front cover reading Revised August 2026 [A02].
Written test: 25 multiple-choice questions, all drawn from information in the guide [A03].
Passing standard: 20 of the 25 questions must be answered correctly, which is 80 percent [A03].
The driver examination has four parts (written, vision, road sign and driving skills) and is given by the Missouri State Highway Patrol; the Class F written test is not open book and leaves out the commercial vehicle chapter [A04].
Time limit: the guide does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MO-INS-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: liability insurance and financial responsibility, accepted proof of insurance, self-insurance, bonds and cash deposits, minimum and uninsured motorist coverage, keeping proof in the vehicle, lapse and unpaid-damage suspensions, crash steps, the Move It law, accident reports, and crashes in designated work zones.

Misconceptions: a crash with a parked vehicle is still a crash you must not leave; after a minor crash with no injuries you move the vehicle, but you never move an injured person; you contact your insurer even when the crash was not your fault; proof of insurance belongs in the vehicle, not at home.
Mnemonics: steer it and clear it for minor crashes; the coverage pattern reads twenty-five, fifty, twenty-five in thousands of dollars.
Glossary: liability insurance means coverage for harm you cause to other people or their property; Financial Responsibility means the legal duty to be able to pay for that harm; binder means a temporary insurance policy; SR filing means the insurer's proof-of-insurance filing with the state; uninsured motorist coverage means coverage for your injuries when the at-fault driver has no insurance; VIN means vehicle identification number.
Dynamic facts: coverage minimums, bond amounts, suspension fees and the accident report form number are statutory or administrative and can change, and the Move It campaign wording is dated to the guide's edition.
