Misconceptions: staying under the per se alcohol limit does not make you legal if you are impaired; refusing a test does not avoid a penalty, it brings revocation; legal marijuana is not legal to drive on above the blood limit; an administrative revocation happens regardless of the court case; a suspended license does not come back by itself; the open container law covers passengers too, not just the driver.
Mnemonics: point oh eight for adults, point oh two under twenty-one, point oh four for commercial; a hundred eighty-five days, then one year, then three years of revocation for first, second and later DUI convictions; star N H P to report.
Glossary: DUI means driving under the influence; implied consent means agreeing to chemical testing by driving in Nevada; illegal per se means a test level that is a violation by itself; administrative penalty means action by the DMV; criminal penalty means action by a court; ignition interlock means a breath-testing device that must be passed before the engine starts; open container means an opened alcoholic beverage in the vehicle.
Dynamic facts: DUI fines, jail ranges, the DUI school cost estimate and the reinstatement fees are set by statute or vary over time, so confirm them with the Nevada DMV.
