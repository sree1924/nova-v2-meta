## A — Exam authority, Section Four Driver responsibilities

This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination. The issuing authority is the West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles within the West Virginia Department of Transportation, and the source is the current revised edition of the West Virginia Driver's Licensing Handbook pinned in the run configuration. The registrar verified no official question count, passing score, test format or time limit for the knowledge examination, so this dossier asserts no value for any of them. The handbook's own examination chapter describes a question minimum and a passing line in its text; those lines travel only as cited knowledge facts in the knowledge-examination section of this run, never as verified exam facts. Practice scoring follows only the accepted LearningLoop product policy. Section Four covers the duties that come with the driving privilege: school attendance for minors, referral and re-examination of unsafe drivers, crash reporting, compulsory liability insurance and its proof and penalties, the electronic communications device law with its fines, the point system with its thresholds and point scale, the interstate compacts, court compliance and child support suspensions, fraudulent license use, the mandatory revocation list, driving while suspended or revoked, and the DMV online services.
## B — Audience, Section Four Driver responsibilities

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination, reading at a plain-English level with no prior driving knowledge assumed. Section Four teaches what a licensed driver must do to keep the privilege and what each failure costs in points, fines, suspensions and revocations. It depends on the definitions chapter for suspension, revocation and driving under the influence and on the licensing chapter for the out-of-state record review. The rules progress from personal fitness and school duties, through crash reporting and insurance, to the device law, the point system, interstate compacts, court compliance and mandatory revocation.
