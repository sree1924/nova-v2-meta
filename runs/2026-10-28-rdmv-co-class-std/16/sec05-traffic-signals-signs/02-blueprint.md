## A — Exam authority: Colorado driving knowledge test

Issuer: the Colorado Division of Motor Vehicles administers driver licensing and the written test described in this handbook [A01].
Exam: the Driving Knowledge Test, also called the written test, covers driving knowledge and safety [A01].
Scope of questions: road signs, driving under the influence, driving rules, safety rules and legal items drawn from the whole handbook [A01].
Relevance of this section: backing up, traffic signals and signs falls inside that whole-handbook scope [A01].
Format: the handbook calls it a written test and points to practice quizzes online and in the myColorado app; the item format is not stated (verify — sec05-D-A03) [A01].
Outcome: passing the written test makes an applicant eligible for an instruction permit [A01].
Question count: not stated on the handbook pages (verify — sec05-D-A01).
Passing standard: not stated on the handbook pages (verify — sec05-D-A02).
Time limit: not stated on the handbook pages (verify — sec05-D-A04).
Edition: the handbook carries a form number and revision date on its contents page; effective date is not stated (verify — sec05-D-A05).
