Misconceptions: driving far below the limit is not automatically safe and can cause crashes; the posted limit is a ceiling, not a target, in poor conditions; oncoming drivers are allowed but not required to yield to a funeral procession; farm equipment may use most roads but not interstates; on a curve you slow before entering rather than braking in the middle of it.
Mnemonics: slow in, steady through; mirror, shoulder, signal, go; count the gap with the two-second rule.
Glossary: stopping distance means the reaction distance plus the braking distance; reaction time means the delay before you begin to brake; funeral procession means a line of vehicles escorting a funeral; blind spot means an area the mirrors do not show, cleared by a look over the shoulder.
Dynamic facts: speed limits can be changed by posted signs or by statute; the default limits here apply only where no other limit is posted.
