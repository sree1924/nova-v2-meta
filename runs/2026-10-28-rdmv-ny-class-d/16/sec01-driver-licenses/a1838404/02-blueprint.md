## B - Audience, Chapter One Driver licenses

The audience is first-time never-licensed applicants, teens through adults, preparing for the regular passenger-car written knowledge test, reading at a plain-English level. Chapter One teaches license classes, applications, permits and junior rules; it assumes no prior driving knowledge and carries no prerequisites beyond literacy. Content progresses from who may drive and which class fits, through proofs and testing steps, to junior limits and renewal duties.
