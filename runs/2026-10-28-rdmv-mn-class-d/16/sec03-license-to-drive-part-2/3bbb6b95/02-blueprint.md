## A — Exam authority for the graduated licensing, license classes and renewal section (Minnesota Class D written test)
Issuer: the Minnesota Department of Public Safety, whose Driver and Vehicle Services division licenses drivers, permits the printing of the Minnesota Driver’s Manual [A01].
Source document: Minnesota Driver’s Manual, with the edition line June 2026 printed on the front cover [A02].
Written test format: multiple choice and true-or-false questions on Minnesota traffic laws and road signs, taken on paper or on a computer, with English, several other languages and American Sign Language at computer test stations [A03].
Passing standard: a score of 80 percent, and only one written test may be taken per day [A03].
Retest fee: after two consecutive failed written tests, a fee of $10 is charged for the third and every later attempt [A03].
Vision screening: normal or corrected vision of at least 20/40 and peripheral vision of at least 105 degrees [A03].
Question count: the manual does not state how many questions the written test has, so the count stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MN-GDL-A-COUNT).
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MN-GDL-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: who may sign for a minor, the instruction permit and provisional license requirements and limits, the supervised driving log hours, Vanessa's Law, the under-twenty-one license, the age-fifteen licenses, the Class D vehicle and towing limits, and renewal rules.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: graduated licensing, license classes and renewal
Audience: Minnesota teens moving through graduated licensing and the parents or guardians who sign and log their practice hours, plus adult permit holders and current drivers checking renewal rules.
Why it matters to a first-timer: the permit eligibility rules [A01], the provisional license requirements and night limit [A02], and the passenger limits [A03] are the core teen-driver items on the written test.
Level: dense threshold recall (ages, months, hours, passenger counts, weights); high confusion risk because teen and adult rules differ by only a few numbers.
Prerequisites: the first half of chapter one on eligibility and the three tests.
