Misconception: you must stop for a school bus only when it is on your side of the road; you stop on either side unless a barrier separates you. [A01]
Misconception: when a siren approaches in an intersection you should stop right there; drive through first and then pull over. [A01]
Misconception: pedestrians will always hear your car; some are deaf, hard of hearing, deaf-blind or blind. [A01]
Mnemonic: Tie at the stop, yield to the right. [A01]
Mnemonic: Downhill backs, uphill climbs. [A01]
Glossary: Right-of-way means the legal duty of one road user to let another go first. [A01]
Glossary: Uncontrolled intersection means an intersection with no stop signs or traffic lights. [A01]
Glossary: Staggered formation means several plows working together offset diagonally from one another, which may not be passed. [A01]
