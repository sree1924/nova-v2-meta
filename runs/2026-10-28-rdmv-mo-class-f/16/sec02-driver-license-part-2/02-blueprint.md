## A — Exam authority for the endorsements, renewal, license loss and the graduated license steps section (Missouri Class F operator license written test)
Issuer: the Missouri Department of Revenue publishes the Missouri Driver Guide [A01].
Source document: Missouri Driver Guide, with the front cover reading Revised August 2026 [A02].
Written test: 25 multiple-choice questions, all drawn from information in the guide [A03].
Passing standard: 20 of the 25 questions must be answered correctly, which is 80 percent [A03].
The driver examination has four parts (written, vision, road sign and driving skills) and is given by the Missouri State Highway Patrol; the Class F written test is not open book and leaves out the commercial vehicle chapter [A04].
Time limit: the guide does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MO-GDL-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: the school bus and motorcycle endorsements, renewal timing and the lapse window that triggers retesting, the donor and notation choices at the counter, the conduct that costs a license, and the permit, intermediate and full under-twenty-one GDL requirements, including supervised hours and passenger and night limits.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: endorsements, renewal, license loss and the graduated license steps
Audience: first-time Missouri Class F applicants under eighteen moving through the Graduated Driver License steps, their supervising parents, and any licensed driver who needs the renewal and lapse rules.
Why it matters to a first-timer: the GDL law sets the permit step every new teen driver starts with [A06] and the supervised hours, holding period and passenger and night limits that come before a full license [A07].
Level: rule recall with several look-alike day counts, ages and look-back windows; moderate load because the permit, intermediate and full steps each have their own conditions.
Prerequisites: the license classes, ages and document rules in the opening part of the first chapter.
