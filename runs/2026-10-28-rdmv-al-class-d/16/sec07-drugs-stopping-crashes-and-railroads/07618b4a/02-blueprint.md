## A — Exam authority for the Alabama driver license knowledge test

Issuer: the Alabama Law Enforcement Agency, Driver License Division, issues Alabama driver licenses [A07] and publishes the Alabama Driver Manual.
Edition: the front cover of the manual prints the edition line "November 2024" under the Driver License Division name [A01]; no effective date is printed, so the effective date stays unconfirmed (verify — sec07-A-EFFECTIVE-DATE).
Exam path: the manual describes a vision screening, a knowledge test with questions on Alabama traffic laws, road signs and safe-driving rules taken from this manual, and a road test given by a driver license examiner [A13].
Learner path: a fifteen-year-old who passes the required exam receives a Class D learner license with the Y restriction [A11].
Delivery: automated testing is available to every applicant taking the knowledge test [A13], and oral and foreign-language versions exist [A11].
Scoring: the manual states no question count, no passing score or percent, and no time limit for the knowledge test; all three stay unknown and no number is asserted here (verify — sec07-A-SCORING).
Question format: beyond automated delivery, the manual does not describe the item format of the knowledge test (verify — sec07-A-FORMAT).
Section link: this dossier section covers drugs that impair driving, avoiding rear-end collisions with the two-second rule, the factors and chart for stopping distance, what to do and report after a crash, and railroad crossing warnings, stops and safety tips, all drawn from the manual the test is built on [A13].
Practice mastery targets in this product are product policy from the accepted learning loop, never an official passing line.
Dynamic facts (updatable): the knowledge test fee, office locations, appointments and online services are set by ALEA and can change between editions, so they are kept out of stable knowledge [A13]; why_dynamic: administrative details outside the printed rules of the road.
## B — Audience and scope

Audience: first-time Alabama applicants who have never held a license and are preparing in English for the driver license knowledge test; this section matters most to new drivers learning following distance and crash-scene duties, and to anyone who drives across Alabama's many railroad crossings.
Level: plain-English reading level; no prior driving vocabulary is assumed, so each term from the manual is defined where it first appears.
Prerequisites: the alcohol facts of the previous section, which the drug-alcohol mix builds on.
Section scope: manual chapter four, second part: prescription, non-prescription and illegal drugs and the drug-alcohol mix, chronic illness, avoiding rear-end collisions with the two-second rule, stopping distance and its pavement, tire, chassis, brake and wind factors with the stopping-distance chart, what to do if you have a crash, reporting traffic mishaps and the written safety-responsibility crash form, duties when involved in or witnessing a crash, railroad crossing warning devices, vehicles that must stop at crossings, and railroad safety tips.
Out of scope: commercial, motorcycle-manual and vessel testing content, office logistics and fees, and anything not printed on the selected manual pages.
