## C — Lane Usage and Maneuvers

FACT_ID: sec06-F01 | anchor: A01
A lane fits one line of vehicles and drivers hold the proper lane marked or not, yellow dashes split opposing flows with safe passing allowed, white dashes split same-direction flows with safe passing allowed, solid yellow splits opposing flows proving left-side correctness while warning of crossing danger with single or double solid barring passes but permitting left-turn crossings, and solid white splits same-direction flows where passing runs hazardous near intersections and merges [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0032]

FACT_ID: sec06-F02 | anchor: A01
Two-lane roads center on one broken yellow line with driving right except for safe passes, three-lane roads lend the marked center to left turns from either direction with passing banned, and four-lane roads split on double solid yellow with dashed white lane dividers [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0032]

FACT_ID: sec06-F03 | anchor: A02
Change lanes only when sure of safety by checking clearance ahead and behind, mirrors, signaling, shoulder-checking the moving side, rechecking behind to both sides, clearing blind spots, and staying out of the leader blind spots, while slowing to gift space when a leader signals [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0033]

FACT_ID: sec06-F04 | anchor: A02
Limited-access expressways admit only at interchanges without crossing traffic and run free of stops, signals, crossings, steep hills, and sharp curves, and their speed rules bar unfit or under-minimum cars, bound travel between forty minimum and fifty-five to seventy-five maximum with too-slow driving illegal, demand safe-for-conditions constant speeds, and ask adjusting for mergers plus moving right for faster traffic [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0033]

FACT_ID: sec06-F05 | anchor: A02
Slower traffic keeps right, faster traffic takes middle or left, drivers center their lane without weaving since lane-hopping gains nothing, and nobody cruises abreast in packs or block flow from blind spots [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0033]

FACT_ID: sec06-F06 | anchor: A03
Plan entrance, direction, and exit ahead, trail three seconds or four in bad weather, scan fifteen to twenty seconds forward for braking and merging, heed every highway sign with early exit-lane moves, and never reverse or cross medians but ride to the next exit and re-enter [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0034]

FACT_ID: sec06-F07 | anchor: A03
Expressway entry yields to onboard traffic, runs the acceleration lane rightward at adjusting speed, then signals left and merges on safety [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0034]

FACT_ID: sec06-F08 | anchor: A03
Exits need an early proper lane, flow speed held to the ramp, a right signal at least one hundred feet out, slowing inside the deceleration lane to the posted cap with speedometer checks, no last-minute turnoffs, and the posted ramp safe speed [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0034]

FACT_ID: sec06-F09 | anchor: A03
Breakdowns and forced stops signal onto the extreme right shoulder clear of traffic with flashers lit, and nobody walks the roadway edges while children stay from traffic [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0034]

FACT_ID: sec06-F10 | anchor: A04
Four-or-more-lane roads forbid left-lane loafing that impedes flow except where traffic, merging, or layout demands other lanes, and passing never excuses exceeding the limit [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0035]

FACT_ID: sec06-F11 | anchor: A04
Left passes need two hundred feet of clear unzoned road, a view-preserving gap behind big rigs, mirror and shoulder checks against simultaneous passers, a left signal with a clean move over, passing through, and a signaled return only once the passed car shows in the mirror [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0035]

FACT_ID: sec06-F12 | anchor: A04
Right passes fit only wide multi-lane roads free of parked cars and obstacles, one-way roads, or left-turning leaders, never by leaving pavement, and no-passing zones cover solid-yellow-in-lane stretches, unmarked hills and curves, and one-hundred-foot obstructed-view approaches near bridges, viaducts, tunnels, overpasses, rails, intersections, and crosswalks [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0035]

FACT_ID: sec06-F13 | anchor: A04

Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0035]

FACT_ID: sec06-F14 | anchor: A05
Signals warn every turn, lane change, slowdown, roadside move, and stop, unsignaled turns are illegal, signaling must start one hundred feet or a third of a block early, and arm signals extend straight for left, point up for right, and point down palm-back for slow or stop with the arm drawn in through the turn [A05].
Source: [A05 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0036]

FACT_ID: sec06-F15 | anchor: A05
Good turns signal first, survey behind and both sides, seize the correct lane early with faster traffic demanding earlier moves, brake to safety before the line or crosswalk with gear set and slight acceleration through while never braking, clutching, or shifting mid-turn, execute from the right lane at the right pace, and finish in the proper lane, attempting only permitted easy turns, circling back rather than turning wrong-laned, and holding wheels straight while awaiting oncoming gaps so rear hits cannot shove you into traffic [A05].
Source: [A05 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0036]

FACT_ID: sec06-F16 | anchor: A06
Left turns run five steps hugging but never crossing the centerline, from early follower checks and centerline-side signaling through signaling plus slowing one hundred feet out, gearing and looking both ways, entering just right of center, and drifting right after, while dual left-turners each pass left of the other from left lane to left lane [A06].
Source: [A06 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0037]

FACT_ID: sec06-F17 | anchor: A06
Right turns run four steps without any leftward swing, from an early rightward move through signaling plus slowing one hundred feet out, gearing and looking both ways, and hugging the right curb through, never turning wide, with turning speed capped at ten miles per hour or less [A06].
Source: [A06 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0037]

FACT_ID: sec06-F18 | anchor: A07
One-way and divided-street turns leave from the curb-nearest lane, straight travel may use any lane with extremes reserved for turning and no weaving, an intersecting diagram lets westbound left-laners turn left and southbound right-laners turn right, and turning speed stays ten miles per hour or less [A07].
Source: [A07 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0038]

FACT_ID: sec06-F19 | anchor: A08
Backing fights blocked vision and touchy control, so set brake and reverse, grip twelve o'clock left-handed, drape the right arm over the seatback, stare through the rear glass instead of mirrors, creep gently while steering the tail where it should go into the nearest correct lane with quick front and side glances, keep watching rearward to the stop, circle the block rather than backing through intersections, and scout behind for bikes, trikes, and small children before boarding especially around vans and pickups [A08].
Source: [A08 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0039]
