## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: vehicle emergencies, crash avoidance, skids, debris and animals
Audience: first-time Arizona drivers and permit holders preparing for the written test in English, who need a ready plan for breakdowns and sudden hazards.

Mnemonics: Blowout, hold straight and coast; skid, steer where you want to go; overheating, hood up, cap on.
Glossary: blowout means a sudden tire failure; ABS means an anti-lock braking system that keeps wheels from locking; ABC-rated extinguisher means one rated for common, liquid and electrical fires; compromise means splitting space so the biggest or most likely danger gets the most room; skid means losing tire grip so the vehicle slides.
