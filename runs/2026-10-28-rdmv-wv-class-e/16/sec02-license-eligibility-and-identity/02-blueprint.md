## A — Exam authority, Section Two License eligibility and identity

This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination. The issuing authority is the West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles within the West Virginia Department of Transportation, and the source is the current revised edition of the West Virginia Driver's Licensing Handbook pinned in the run configuration. The registrar verified no official question count, passing score, test format or time limit for the knowledge examination, so this dossier asserts no value for any of them. The handbook's own examination chapter describes a question minimum and a passing line in its text; those lines travel only as cited knowledge facts in the knowledge-examination section of this run, never as verified exam facts. Practice scoring follows only the accepted LearningLoop product policy. Section Two covers who must hold a West Virginia license, who is exempt and who can be denied, the REAL ID and Not For Federal Use card types, the proof documents for identity, Social Security number, legal name change and residency, the rules for new residents, transfers, duplicates and expired licenses, the national Problem Driver Point System review, address-change duties, license color coding, and the document lists for immigrant, non-immigrant and refugee applicants.
## B — Audience, Section Two License eligibility and identity

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination, reading at a plain-English level with no prior driving knowledge assumed. Section Two teaches the administrative side of getting licensed, from who needs a license through the document checklists to what new residents and non-citizens must bring. It assumes no prior driving knowledge and depends only on the definitions chapter for terms such as suspension and revocation. The rules progress from the licensing requirement and its exemptions, through the two card types and the four proof categories, to residency, out-of-state records, special applicant groups and card appearance.
