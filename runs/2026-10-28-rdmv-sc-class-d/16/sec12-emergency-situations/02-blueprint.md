Misconceptions: pumping the brakes switches ABS off, so you hold firm, steady pressure; a vibrating ABS pedal is normal and not a fault; after a blowout you do not brake hard but let the vehicle slow; jerking back onto the pavement at a drop-off is the dangerous move; a dead engine does not lock the steering, it only makes it heavier.
Mnemonics: brake, steer or accelerate are the three escape options; for ABS, stomp and stay; for a blowout, grip, glide, pull off; for a breakdown, off the road, flashers on, flares well behind.
Glossary: ABS means anti-lock braking system; swerving means quickly steering around a problem; skid means losing tire grip so the vehicle slides; drop-off means an uneven edge where the pavement meets the shoulder; panic steering means jerking the wheel to get back on the pavement at once; blowout means a rapid loss of air from a tire; escape path means a safe route out of traffic; emergency flashers means the hazard lights that show your vehicle is disabled.
