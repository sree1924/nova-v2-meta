## A — Exam authority for the Alabama driver license knowledge test

Issuer: the Alabama Law Enforcement Agency, Driver License Division, issues Alabama driver licenses [A07] and publishes the Alabama Driver Manual.
Edition: the front cover of the manual prints the edition line "November 2024" under the Driver License Division name [A01]; no effective date is printed, so the effective date stays unconfirmed (verify — sec05-A-EFFECTIVE-DATE).
Exam path: the manual describes a vision screening, a knowledge test with questions on Alabama traffic laws, road signs and safe-driving rules taken from this manual, and a road test given by a driver license examiner [A13].
Learner path: a fifteen-year-old who passes the required exam receives a Class D learner license with the Y restriction [A11].
Delivery: automated testing is available to every applicant taking the knowledge test [A13], and oral and foreign-language versions exist [A11].
Scoring: the manual states no question count, no passing score or percent, and no time limit for the knowledge test; all three stay unknown and no number is asserted here (verify — sec05-A-SCORING).
Question format: beyond automated delivery, the manual does not describe the item format of the knowledge test (verify — sec05-A-FORMAT).
Section link: this dossier section covers sharing the road with trucks and buses and their No-Zones, parking rules including hills, no-parking places and parallel parking, and the Alabama safety belt and child restraint laws, all drawn from the manual the test is built on [A13].
Practice mastery targets in this product are product policy from the accepted learning loop, never an official passing line.

## B — Audience and scope

Audience: first-time Alabama applicants who have never held a license and are preparing in English for the driver license knowledge test; this section matters most to new drivers learning to share highways with trucks and buses, to anyone practicing hill and parallel parking for the road test, and to parents who carry young children.
Level: plain-English reading level; no prior driving vocabulary is assumed, so each term from the manual is defined where it first appears.
Prerequisites: the turning and sharing-the-road rules of the previous section, which this chapter part continues.
Section scope: manual chapter three, second part: the closing motorcycle crash-outcome note, sharing the road with large vehicles and the side, rear, front, backing and turning No-Zones, truck stopping distance, standing vehicles on rural highways, parking on a hill with and without a curb, places where parking is not allowed, the parallel-parking steps, and the Alabama safety belt and child restraint laws with their seat stages.
Out of scope: commercial, motorcycle-manual and vessel testing content, office logistics and fees, and anything not printed on the selected manual pages.
