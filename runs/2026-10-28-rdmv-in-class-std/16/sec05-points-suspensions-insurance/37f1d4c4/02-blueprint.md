## A — Exam authority for the points, suspensions and insurance requirements section (Indiana learner's permit and driver's license knowledge exam)
Issuer: the Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV) publishes the Indiana Driver's Manual; both names appear on the front cover [A02].
Source document: the front cover prints no edition; the back cover reads Published July 2026 and points readers to IN.gov/BMV for updates [A04].
Knowledge exam: a multiple-choice, computer-based exam drawn from Indiana law, defensive driving practices and the manual, which also tests reading of regulatory, warning and traffic signs and signals [A01].
Exam structure: the exam has two components, one on common signs (Chapter 6) and one on traffic rules and laws (Chapters 6, 7 and 8) [A03].
Passing standard: 80 percent or higher correct on each component [A03].
Branch logistics: arrive at least 30 minutes before the branch closes to test that day; after a failure, wait until the next business day to retake [A01].
Question count: the manual gives no number of questions for either component or in total, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify IN-POINTS-A-COUNT).
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown (verify IN-POINTS-A-TIME).
This section feeds the knowledge exam: this section covers the point values, insurance minimums and suspension rules that the traffic rules and laws component can draw on, including the legal BAC limit and chemical test penalties.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: points, suspensions and insurance requirements
Audience: new Indiana drivers and permit holders who need to know what a ticket, an uninsured drive or an impaired drive does to their record and privileges.
Why it matters to a first-timer: point values and the two-year active window explain how quickly tickets add up [A01], every driver must carry the state minimum liability coverage [A02], and chemical test penalties apply from the first offense [A04].
Level: threshold and penalty recall (point bands, dollar minimums, day counts, suspension lengths) plus a few cause-and-effect chains around SR22 filings.
Prerequisites: the first chapters on credential types and restrictions; the safe-operation chapter covers alcohol and impaired driving in more depth.
