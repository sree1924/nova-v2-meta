## A — Exam authority for the Connecticut Class D knowledge test

Issuer: the Connecticut Department of Motor Vehicles issues driver licenses and publishes the Connecticut Driver’s Manual [A03].
Edition: the imprint page prints the edition line “Revised March 2023” [A03]; no effective date is printed, so the effective date stays unconfirmed (verify — sec03-A-EFFECTIVE-DATE).
Exam path: a first time applicant passes a vision test and a 25-question knowledge test to earn a learner permit [A07], then a road test for the license [A09].
Scoring: the knowledge test spans 25 questions with 20 correct answers needed to pass [A07]; the manual states no time limit, which stays unknown (verify — sec03-A-TIME-LIMIT).
Question format: beyond samples on the DMV mobile app, the manual does not describe the item format of the knowledge test (verify — sec03-A-FORMAT).
Section link: this dossier section covers vehicle equipment checks, seat belt and child restraint laws, belt myths, cell phone law and insurance law, all drawn from the manual the test is built on [A14].
Practice mastery targets in this product are product policy from the accepted learning loop, never an official passing line.
Dynamic facts (updatable): fines, fees, office locations and online services are set by DMV and can change between editions, so they are kept out of stable knowledge [A14]; why_dynamic: administrative details outside the printed rules of the road.

Audience: first-time Connecticut applicants preparing in English for the Class D knowledge test; this section matters to every driver checking a car before a trip, to parents fitting child seats, and to all drivers facing the phone and insurance laws.
