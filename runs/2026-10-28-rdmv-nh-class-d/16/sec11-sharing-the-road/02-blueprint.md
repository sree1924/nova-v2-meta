FACT_ID: sec11-F048 | anchor: P45

FACT_ID: sec11-F050 | anchor: P45

FACT_ID: sec11-F051 | anchor: P46

FACT_ID: sec11-F052 | anchor: P46
Stop behind the marked intersection line so that a turning large vehicle has the room the marking preserves.

FACT_ID: sec11-F053 | anchor: P46
Never cut off a large vehicle to reach a turn or exit or to get ahead of it at a one-lane construction area.

FACT_ID: sec11-F054 | anchor: P46
Yield to an emergency vehicle using a siren or flashing lights from any direction by moving right and stopping without obstructing an intersection.

FACT_ID: sec11-F055 | anchor: P46
After the emergency vehicle passes, signal before reentering the roadway and do so only when safe.

FACT_ID: sec11-F056 | anchor: P46
Keep at least 500 feet behind a fire truck using warning lights or a siren, and do not drive or park in the block where it has stopped for a fire alarm.

FACT_ID: sec11-F057 | anchor: P46

FACT_ID: sec11-F058 | anchor: P46
When traffic allows it safely, move into a lane away from the incident or emergency personnel and promptly leave a lane that is wholly or partly blocked.

FACT_ID: sec11-F059 | anchor: P46
Follow authorized traffic directions and emergency signals around a crash, fire, maintenance activity or other roadway emergency.

FACT_ID: sec11-F060 | anchor: P46
A move-over-law violation brings a fine and penalty assessment, with a larger fine for another offense within 12 months.

FACT_ID: sec11-F061 | anchor: P46
For a police traffic stop, pull aside, stop fully and select park; stay inside unless the officer requests that you leave.

FACT_ID: sec11-F062 | anchor: P46
Switch off the engine, activate hazard lamps and, at night, illuminate the interior during the described police stop.

FACT_ID: sec11-F063 | anchor: P46
Remain calm and belted, ask passengers to do likewise, keep hands on the wheel and limit unnecessary movement.

FACT_ID: sec11-F064 | anchor: P47
When an officer requests license and registration documents, explain where they are before reaching slowly while keeping one hand on the wheel.

FACT_ID: sec11-F065 | anchor: P47

FACT_ID: sec11-F068 | anchor: P47

FACT_ID: sec11-F069 | anchor: P47
Use exceptional caution at night in a work zone even when no workers are visible.

FACT_ID: sec11-F070 | anchor: P47

FACT_ID: sec11-F071 | anchor: P47

FACT_ID: sec11-F072 | anchor: P47
