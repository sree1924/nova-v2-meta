# Accuracy and teaching notes

This section covers the guide’s ordinary trailer safety material rather than the excluded Class D chauffeur chapter. The three-thousand-pound brake threshold and required equipment remain subject to current issuer/legal verification. Use vehicle/trailer manufacturer values for ratings, pressures and hitch setup; no universal pressure increase or equipment capacity is inferred. The source illustration is not treated as evidence for unseen dimensions or connection geometry. [A01] [A02]
Memory cue, authored product policy: “Match the hitch, cross the chains, test the lights, secure the load”. The cue retrieves complete cited rules and does not replace their conditions.
Terminology: The coupler connects to a matching hitch ball; the trailer tongue is the forward connection structure. A load rating is a tire capacity rather than a permission to overload the whole vehicle.
Misconception to correct: A secure main hitch makes chains unnecessary, or a tire’s capacity overrides the vehicle’s rating. The guide requires a separate safety connection and manufacturer-based load planning.
