Misconceptions: carrying insurance is not enough, proof must be in the vehicle or on your phone; a police-investigated crash still reaches the DMV through the officer's report; traffic school does not erase the conviction, only up to three points; DUI is not a points offense, it brings revocation; out-of-state tickets do count on a Nevada record.
Mnemonics: twenty-five, fifty, twenty for liability; seven fifty and ten days for the SR-1; twelve points in twelve months means suspension; traffic school takes three, once a year; reckless eight, careless six.
Glossary: liability insurance means coverage that pays for injury or damage you cause others; SR-1 means the Report of Crash Form; SR-22 means DMV-monitored proof of liability insurance for reinstatement; demerit point means a value added to your record for each violation; revocation means cancellation of the license as opposed to a temporary suspension.
Dynamic facts: minimum coverage amounts, lapse fines, the reporting threshold and the point schedule are set by law and can change, and the DMV mailing address, phone and email contacts change too, so confirm them on the DMV website.
