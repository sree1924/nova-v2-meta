## A — Exam authority: North Carolina regular Class C knowledge and signs tests
Issuer: the North Carolina Department of Transportation, Division of Motor Vehicles (NCDMV) publishes the handbook and runs licensing [A01].
Source document: North Carolina Driver Handbook; the back cover prints the revision note Revised May 2025 [A03].
License path in scope: a first-time applicant for a regular Class C license, the noncommercial class for single vehicles rated under 26,001 pounds [A04].
Test structure for an original license: a vision check, a traffic signs test, a knowledge test and an on-road driving test, and each one has to be passed on its own [A02].
Traffic signs test: the applicant identifies signs by color and shape and explains what each one means; all tested signs are in the handbook [A02].
Knowledge test: covers traffic laws and safe driving practices; an audio version is offered on request for applicants who have difficulty reading [A02].
Road test: taken only after every other test is passed, and not part of the learner permit [A02].
Learner permit path: available from age 18 and requires vision, signs and law-knowledge testing [A05].
Not stated in the handbook: the number of knowledge-test questions, the passing score and any time limit; these stay unknown and are not shown to learners (verify NC-A-01).
Practice quizzes in this product use a product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites for licensing and testing
Audience: first-time North Carolina applicants preparing for the regular Class C knowledge and signs tests, in English, studying from the state handbook.
Adult entry route: the learner permit is open to applicants aged 18 and older [A01]; younger applicants follow graduated licensing, covered in the next handbook section.
Level: plain-language rules recall; no prior driving experience assumed; the road-test skill list is included so learners know what is graded later [A03].
Prerequisites: none for this section; it is the entry point that explains which tests exist and in what order they are taken [A02].
Scope boundary: commercial classes and motorcycle permits appear only so learners can tell them apart from the regular Class C license; their detailed study belongs to the CDL and Motorcycle handbooks [A02].
Reading support: an audio knowledge test can be requested, so explanations should stay short and speakable [A02].
