## A — Exam authority for the alcohol, drugs and driving section (Missouri Class F operator license written test)
Issuer: the Missouri Department of Revenue publishes the Missouri Driver Guide [A01].
Source document: Missouri Driver Guide, with the front cover reading Revised August 2026 [A02].
Written test: 25 multiple-choice questions, all drawn from information in the guide [A03].
Passing standard: 20 of the 25 questions must be answered correctly, which is 80 percent [A03].
The driver examination has four parts (written, vision, road sign and driving skills) and is given by the Missouri State Highway Patrol; the Class F written test is not open book and leaves out the commercial vehicle chapter [A04].
Time limit: the guide does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MO-ALC-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: the legal BAC limits, field and chemical tests, the administrative action notice and hearing, court-convicted jail, fine and license ladders, the Abuse and Lose Law, Minor in Possession, SR-22 proof of insurance, chemical test refusal, ignition interlock rules, and the effects of alcohol, medicines and marijuana.

## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: alcohol, drugs and driving
Audience: first-time Missouri Class F applicants, from teens on an instruction permit to adult newcomers; the youth rules matter most to drivers under twenty-one.
