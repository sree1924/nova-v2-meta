## C - Your Vehicle

FACT_ID: sec05-F01 | anchor: A01
An owned vehicle requires Florida registration, a license plate, and a Florida title unless held by an out-of-state lienholder; non-residents who start work or enroll children in Florida public school have ten days to register with Florida insurance and title proof, and out-of-state vehicles need vehicle identification number verification except new vehicles, new or used mobile homes, trailer-type recreational vehicles, and trailers or semitrailers under two thousand pounds [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-fl-e:florida-department-of-highway-safety-and:handbook:pdf-0045]

FACT_ID: sec05-F02 | anchor: A01
Service centers title and plate by weight and type, plates stay current with papers aboard, dealers file for buyers while private buyers file themselves, titles precede plates, and out-of-state licensees license first [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-fl-e:florida-department-of-highway-safety-and:handbook:pdf-0045]

FACT_ID: sec05-F03 | anchor: A01
Renewals run yearly or biennial to the birthday midnight, mobile homes and semis to December 31, fleets to June 30, open 90 days early, and recheck insurance [A01].
