## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: lane travel, speed, pavement markings and space cushion

Misconceptions: driving under the limit is not legal if conditions call for slower; an advisory speed is not a limit, yet ignoring it can bring a basic rule citation; a two-way left-turn lane and a painted median are not travel lanes.
Mnemonics: alleys fifteen, business and school twenty, homes and parks twenty-five, otherwise fifty-five; yellow splits opposite directions, white splits the same direction; broken means you may, solid means you may not.
Glossary: basic rule means the duty to drive at a speed reasonable for conditions; space cushion means the open room around your car; fog line means the solid white edge line; yield line means a row of pavement triangles marking where to yield.
Dynamic facts: variable speed signs change the limit or advisory speed with live conditions, and interstate limits vary; obey the sign in front of you.
