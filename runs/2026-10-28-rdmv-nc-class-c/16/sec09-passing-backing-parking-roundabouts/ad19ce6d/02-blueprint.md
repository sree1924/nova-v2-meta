Misconceptions: a driver being passed must not speed up and must help the passer; passing on the right is not generally allowed just because there is room; having the right of way does not excuse you from slowing at an intersection; cars in a roundabout do not yield to entering cars; in a multi-lane roundabout you pick your lane before entering, not inside it.
Mnemonics: look, signal, horn, then pass; the car on the right goes first when arrivals tie; a left turn waits for straight traffic; quarter right, half straight, three-quarters left, full circle back.
Glossary: double parking means parking on the roadway side of another parked vehicle; interchange means roads crossing at different levels with ramps; single-point interchange means one signal-controlled intersection over or under a through road; roundabout means a small circular intersection with counterclockwise flow; truck apron means the raised concrete island trucks may use in a roundabout.
Dynamic facts: posted no-parking signs add restrictions wherever they appear; the distances here are the fixed rules the handbook prints.
