Misconceptions: stopping is not the only way to avoid a crash, since turning or even speeding up can be safer; the ABS emergency method is firm, steady pressure held until you stop, not pumping; you never swerve into the oncoming lane; a crash with no injuries still needs a police report when damage passes the thresholds or a car cannot move; leaving after an injury crash is a crime, not just a mistake.
Mnemonics: stop, turn or speed up; ABS means stand on it and hold; after a crash, call, stay, warn, swap information, record; blowout means hold straight and ease off; stuck pedal means neutral, pull off, engine off.
Glossary: ABS means an anti-lock braking system that keeps wheels from locking; emergency flashers means hazard warning lights; flares means roadside warning lights placed to alert traffic; blowout means a sudden tire failure; neutral means the gear position that disconnects the engine from the wheels; witness means a person who saw the crash.
