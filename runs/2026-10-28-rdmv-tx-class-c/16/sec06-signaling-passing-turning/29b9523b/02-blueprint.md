## C — Signaling, Passing, and Turning

FACT_ID: sec06-F01 | anchor: A01
Drivers signal every lane change, turn, curb pull-out, slowdown or stop, highway entry or exit, and roadside pull-over, using lamps or bold hand-and-arm gestures from the window, keeping lamps working for darkness, pumping brakes to flag stops, signaling a full one hundred feet ahead, and canceling promptly since stray signals still read as turns [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0044]

FACT_ID: sec06-F02 | anchor: A01
The left side stays forbidden under no-passing markings or signs, with two or more lanes each way, within one hundred feet of intersections, rail crossings, bridges, viaducts, and tunnels, and on hills, curves, and blind spots, while drivers otherwise hug the right except to pass on two- or three-lane streets, on one-way roads, or past blocked right sides [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0044]

FACT_ID: sec06-F03 | anchor: A01
Safe passing waits for clear road, signals lane changes, taps horns against surprises, and returns gently, since botched passes turn head-on and deadly [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0044]

FACT_ID: sec06-F04 | anchor: A02
Two-lane passes open with following distance, mirror and blind-spot checks, and a left signal, continue past no-passing checks with enough room to regain the right before oncoming traffic closes inside two hundred feet plus a warning horn tap, and close by passing left, waiting for the overtaken car in the mirror, then signaling right and canceling [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0045]

FACT_ID: sec06-F05 | anchor: A02
Right-side passing is lawful only when safe on clear wide multi-lane roads, on one-way roads, or along paved shoulders past slowing, stopped, disabled, or left-turning vehicles, and never by leaving the pavement [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0045]

FACT_ID: sec06-F06 | anchor: A02
Passed drivers hold speed and lane, ease right on unmarked lanes, and smooth the passer's path, while passers never linger in blind spots but either complete the pass or fall back out of sight [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0045]

FACT_ID: sec06-F07 | anchor: A02
Turns start with early decisions against last-second swerves, all-around looks before lane moves, and quick proper-lane entries, skipping any turn missed within half a block [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0045]

FACT_ID: sec06-F08 | anchor: A03
Turns continue with signals one hundred feet out, hand signals dropped near the corner for two-handed steering, reasonable pre-turn speed without mid-turn braking or clutching, lane discipline through the maneuver, and proper-lane finishes [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0046]

FACT_ID: sec06-F09 | anchor: A03
Right turns stage early lane signals into the far-right lane, add right signals with slowing one hundred feet out, check both ways, hug the right edge, and steer two-handed [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0046]

FACT_ID: sec06-F10 | anchor: A03
Left turns stage early signals into the center lane, add left signals with slowing one hundred feet out, scan every direction, stay right of center entering while yielding to oncoming traffic, exit right of center into the least-blocking lane, and change lanes afterward as needed [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0046]

FACT_ID: sec06-F11 | anchor: A03
Lefts from one-way streets onto two-way streets run from the left lane into the same-direction right lane [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0046]

FACT_ID: sec06-F12 | anchor: A04
Lefts onto one-way streets enter the least-blocking lane, and drivers heed markings and signs authorizing multi-lane turns or special lane moves [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0047]
## C2 — Confusables and KG hints, Signaling Passing and Turning

FACT_ID: sec06-R01 | anchor: A01
Use a signal for lane changes, turns, departures from parallel parking, slowing or stopping, highway entry or exit, and pulling to the roadside. Before turning or stopping, keep the signal on for at least 100 feet; switch off the turn signal after finishing the turn [A01].
Why it matters: group signaling; difficulty low; prereq none; confusables sec06-F01 [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0044]

FACT_ID: sec06-R02 | anchor: A01
Stay off the road's left side wherever signs or paint forbid it, where traffic has at least two lanes each way, within 100 feet of or across a junction or rail crossing, on restricted-view hills or bends, and within 100 feet of a bridge, viaduct or tunnel. The stated keep-right exceptions are overtaking on a two- or three-lane street, one-way travel, or a blocked right side [A01].
Why it matters: group lane-discipline; difficulty medium; prereq none; confusables sec06-F02 [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0044]

FACT_ID: sec06-R03 | anchor: A01
Overtake only with a clear path, signal before moving between lanes, sound a brief horn warning when needed to avoid surprising the driver ahead, and avoid returning too close in front. Bad passing can cause fatal head-on collisions [A01].
Why it matters: group passing; difficulty low; prereq sec06-F02; confusables sec06-F03 [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0044]

FACT_ID: sec06-R04 | anchor: A02
Overtakers need oncoming traffic beyond two hundred feet before starting, and the passed car visible in the mirror before returning [A02].
Why it matters: group passing; difficulty medium; prereq sec06-F03; confusables sec06-F04 [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0045]

FACT_ID: sec06-R05 | anchor: A02
Passing on the right requires safe conditions: a road clear of parked vehicles and other obstructions with room for at least two lanes each way, or a one-way road. A paved shoulder may be used to pass a vehicle slowing or stopped in the highway's traveled area, disabled, or about to turn left. Stay on the pavement [A02].
Why it matters: group passing; difficulty medium; prereq sec06-F03; confusables sec06-F05 [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0045]

FACT_ID: sec06-R06 | anchor: A02
A driver being overtaken must not speed up and must remain in the same lane. If overtaken on the left where lanes are unmarked, shift safely as far right as possible. When overtaking, clear the other driver's blind spot by completing the pass or dropping back [A02].
Why it matters: group passing; difficulty low; prereq sec06-F04; confusables sec06-F06 [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0045]

FACT_ID: sec06-R07 | anchor: A03
Check behind and on both sides before changing lanes for a turn. Choose the proper lane early; if you cannot reach it at least half a block before the turn, continue straight. Give the turn signal at least 100 feet ahead. A hand signal should show your intent before the junction, then end for the turn so both hands can steer [A03].
Why it matters: group turning; difficulty medium; prereq none; confusables sec06-F07, sec06-F08 [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0046]

FACT_ID: sec06-R08 | anchor: A03
Right turns hug the right edge throughout, while left turns yield oncoming traffic and exit right of center into open lanes [A03].
Why it matters: group turning; difficulty high; prereq sec06-F07; confusables sec06-F09, sec06-F10 [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0046]
