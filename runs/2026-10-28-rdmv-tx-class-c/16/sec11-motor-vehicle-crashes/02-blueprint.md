## C — Motor Vehicle Crashes

FACT_ID: sec11-F01 | anchor: A01
Most Texas crashes trace to speeding, botched yielding or stopping, and alcohol impairment, and crash drivers trade names, addresses, phones, vehicle numbers, plate numbers, license facts, and insurance facts when asked and available, copying insurer names and policy numbers verbatim while insurer gaps go to the insurance department hotline, plus crash locations and witness contacts [A01].

FACT_ID: sec11-F02 | anchor: A01
Law enforcement hears immediately about injuries or deaths, immovable vehicles, fleeing drivers, and suspected intoxication [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0065]

FACT_ID: sec11-F03 | anchor: A01
Uninvestigated crashes without injury, death, or one thousand dollars of property damage need a written transportation-department report on its form within ten days of the crash [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0065]

FACT_ID: sec11-F04 | anchor: A01
Injury-or-death crashes demand immediate stops at or near scenes without needless obstruction plus immediate returns and stays, handing name, address, registration, and insurer to injured parties and vehicle attendants, flashing licenses when asked and available, and rendering reasonable aid including doctor-or-hospital transport when needed or requested [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0065]

FACT_ID: sec11-F05 | anchor: A01
Fleeing injury-or-death crashes punishes death as second-degree felony up to ten thousand dollars with two to twenty TDCJ years, serious bodily injury as third-degree felony up to ten thousand dollars with two to ten years, and plain injury with up to five TDCJ years or one county year and up to five thousand dollars [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0065]

FACT_ID: sec11-F06 | anchor: A01
Vehicle-damage-only crashes with driven or attended cars demand immediate near-scene stops, prompt moves to investigation sites, frontage roads, cross streets, or suitable spots when main-lane cars stay drivable, immediate returns and stays, and the same name, address, registration, insurer, and on-request license handoffs [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0065]

FACT_ID: sec11-F07 | anchor: A02
Fleeing vehicle-damage crashes costs Class C up to five hundred dollars under two hundred dollars of damage, or Class B up to two thousand dollars and one hundred eighty jailed days at two hundred dollars or more [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0066]

FACT_ID: sec11-F08 | anchor: A02
Unattended-vehicle strikes demand immediate stops with owner searches and name-and-address handoffs, or visible written notices carrying names, addresses, and crash circumstances, under the same under-two-hundred Class C and two-hundred-plus Class B ladder [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0066]

FACT_ID: sec11-F09 | anchor: A02
Fixture, landscaping, and structure strikes demand reasonable owner searches with crash notice, name, address, and registration handoffs, on-request license shows, ten-day written transportation reports for uninvestigated crashes under one thousand dollars to any one person without injury or death, and the same Class C and Class B fleeing ladder [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0066]

FACT_ID: sec11-F10 | anchor: A02
Hit-and-run victims report crashes for investigation since uninsured motorist coverage pays reported cases, and helpers state locations clearly, summon skilled aid without trusting denials of injury, avoid moving victims or preserve positions when moves prove essential, stanch serious bleeding with clean thick pressure pads, and shelter victims from heat or cold [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0066]
