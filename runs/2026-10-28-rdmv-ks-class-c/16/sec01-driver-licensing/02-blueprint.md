The handbook states a question count of 25, an 80 percent passing standard, and an untimed test; the usual completion estimate is not a deadline [A94].
This dossier covers Driver licensing; its learning sequence and pedagogic emphasis are product choices, not an official topic weighting.
Effective date and total question-bank size remain unconfirmed (verify sec01-EFFECTIVE, sec01-BANK).
## B — Driver licensing

Audience: first-time Kansas non-commercial Class C applicants studying Driver licensing.
The selected scope excludes motorcycle and commercial licensing; those require their own manuals [A11].
Prerequisites and sequence: Distinguish an instructional permit, a restricted license, a farm permit and unrestricted driving before applying age and passenger conditions. This is an editorial teaching order, with no claimed official domain weights.
Coverage follows the mapped handbook pages and preserves restrictions, exceptions and decision conditions in separate fact blocks.
