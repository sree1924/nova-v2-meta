## A — Exam authority, Section Three Permits and graduated licensing

This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination. The issuing authority is the West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles within the West Virginia Department of Transportation, and the source is the current revised edition of the West Virginia Driver's Licensing Handbook pinned in the run configuration. The registrar verified no official question count, passing score, test format or time limit for the knowledge examination, so this dossier asserts no value for any of them. The handbook's own examination chapter describes a question minimum and a passing line in its text; those lines travel only as cited knowledge facts in the knowledge-examination section of this run, never as verified exam facts. Practice scoring follows only the accepted LearningLoop product policy. Section Three covers the exam-room rules and vision standard, the regular Class E instruction permit, the three graduated driver's license levels with their ages, hours, passenger limits, conviction windows and penalties, Level Three eligibility, under-eighteen transfers, license restriction codes, the Drive for Five expiration cycle and renewal fees, the optional card designations, the duty to carry the license, the fee table and the documents to keep in the vehicle.
## B — Audience, Section Three Permits and graduated licensing

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination, reading at a plain-English level with no prior driving knowledge assumed. Section Three teaches how a new driver moves from permit to full license and what each graduated level allows, costs and forbids. It depends on the definitions chapter for the graduated driver's license term and on the licensing chapter for the proof documents it refers back to. The rules progress from testing and the regular permit, through Level One, Level Two and Level Three in order, to restrictions, expiration, fees and designations.
