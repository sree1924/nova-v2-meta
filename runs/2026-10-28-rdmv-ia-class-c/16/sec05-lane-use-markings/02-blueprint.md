Misconceptions: white divides same-direction traffic while yellow divides opposing traffic, never the reverse; sharrows grant no exclusive bike space unlike true bike lanes; a flashing yellow X restricts the lane to turning rather than closing it.

Mnemonic: white stays together, yellow keeps apart; solid means stay, dashed means may; green arrows open, red crosses close.

Glossary: reversible lane (a lane whose direction flips by time of day); yield line (a row of white triangles marking where to yield); sharrow (a shared-lane bike marking with no exclusive space); bike box (a green full-width waiting zone for cyclist turns); reserved lane (a lane restricted to special vehicles, often diamond-marked).

Dynamic facts: none in these pages; every marking rule above is stable handbook knowledge with no date-variable or place-variable values.
