## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: turn signals, turning lanes, U-turns, intersections and roundabouts
Audience: every first-time Oregon Class C applicant; turning and right-of-way rules are everyday driving law and common knowledge-test material.
Why it matters to a first-timer: whether a turn on red is legal depends on the kind of road you turn from and onto [A03], the U-turn bans use different visibility distances in and out of cities [A04], and roundabouts reverse the habit of entering first and yielding later [A06].
Level: condition-and-action recall (which lane, which road type, who yields) with a few exact distances; moderate load.
Prerequisites: the signs and traffic signals section, especially red signals and stop signs, and the lane travel parts, especially lane changes and scanning.

Misconceptions: a turn on red is not always banned, since two kinds of turn onto a one-way road are allowed after a full stop and yield; you may cross a bike lane to turn but not drive in it first; being in the wrong lane is fixed at the next intersection, not by a late swerve; the manual calls first-to-stop-goes-first at a four-way stop a courtesy, with yielding to the driver on the right when in doubt, so never assume the other driver will yield; you never stop inside a roundabout for a siren.
Mnemonics: left, ahead, right, left again before any turn; nearest lane to nearest lane; onto a one-way you may go on red, onto a two-way you may not; in a roundabout, yield to get in, go counterclockwise, signal right to get out.
Glossary: roundabout means a circular intersection with a center island and one-way counterclockwise flow; dual turn lanes means two lanes allowed to turn the same direction together; T intersection means a road that ends at a through road; vulnerable road users means pedestrians and bicyclists; straddle means a large truck using both roundabout lanes at once.
Dynamic facts: none in these pages; the signal distance, U-turn visibility distances and red-light turn rules are fixed statements of the current manual.
