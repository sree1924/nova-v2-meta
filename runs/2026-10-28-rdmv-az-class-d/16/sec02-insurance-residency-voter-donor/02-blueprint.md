Audience: first-time Arizona Class D or graduated-license applicants, and newcomers deciding when they must switch to an Arizona license, preparing in English.
Why it matters to a first-timer: the vehicle brought to the road test must carry current registration and insurance and be driven without driver-assist features [A01], and moving to Arizona starts the licensing duty as soon as a residency condition applies [A02].

Misconceptions: carrying insurance is not enough if the VIN on file is wrong — a mismatch can still lead to suspension; registering to vote is offered with the license but is never required; an out-of-state student with a job here is still not a resident; driver-assist features cannot be used to pass the road test.
Mnemonics: for liability minimums remember twenty-five, fifty, fifteen — one person, two or more people, property; for residency, think Work, School, Business, Seven months, Vote.
Glossary: liability insurance means coverage that pays for injuries or damage you cause to others; National Driver Register means the nationwide database of problem drivers checked on every application; ADAS means Advanced Driver Assistance Systems such as auto-drive and parking assistance; medical condition indicator means a code on the license flagging a condition that may need immediate attention.
Dynamic facts: voter-registration contact numbers, the donor registry sign-up channels and online service names can change and are kept out of stable knowledge; insurance minimums come from statute and change only by law.
