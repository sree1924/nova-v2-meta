## Audience and source scope
Passenger-car applicants need to understand truck stopping, turning and visibility before deciding where to follow or pass. Distinguish a truck's leftward setup for a right turn from a completed lane change. Keep the truck's safety cushion intact at exits and lane closures rather than treating empty space as spare capacity.
