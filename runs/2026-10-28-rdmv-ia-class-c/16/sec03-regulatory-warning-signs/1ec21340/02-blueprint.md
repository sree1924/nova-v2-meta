Misconceptions: a red circle with a slash always prohibits, while plain regulatory signs instruct; advance school crossing signs may be yellow despite the fluorescent school-warning rule; the divided-highway sign describes the road ahead rather than ordering you to divide.

Mnemonic: slash means stop doing it; diamonds warn, chevrons curve, cross traffic crosses; deer, buses, and children each get their own advance sign.

Glossary: regulatory (an enforceable rule sign); prohibition (a red-circle-slash ban such as no U-turn); crossroad (a road crossing yours); chevron (a sharp-alignment marker reinforcing a curve); ramp speed (the advised exit-ramp speed); cross traffic (vehicles crossing your path).

Dynamic facts: none in these pages; every sign meaning above is stable handbook knowledge with no date-variable or place-variable values.
