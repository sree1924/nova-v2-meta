## A — Exam authority for the obtaining a new credential, permits and exams section (Indiana learner's permit and driver's license knowledge exam)
Issuer: the Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV) publishes the Indiana Driver's Manual; both names appear on the front cover [A02].
Source document: the front cover prints no edition; the back cover reads Published July 2026 and points readers to IN.gov/BMV for updates [A04].
Knowledge exam: a multiple-choice, computer-based exam drawn from Indiana law, defensive driving practices and the manual, which also tests reading of regulatory, warning and traffic signs and signals [A01].
Exam structure: the exam has two components, one on common signs (Chapter 6) and one on traffic rules and laws (Chapters 6, 7 and 8) [A03].
Passing standard: 80 percent or higher correct on each component [A03].
Branch logistics: arrive at least 30 minutes before the branch closes to test that day; after a failure, wait until the next business day to retake [A01].
Question count: the manual gives no number of questions for either component or in total, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify IN-NEWCRED-A-COUNT).
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown (verify IN-NEWCRED-A-TIME).
This section feeds the knowledge exam: this is the entry chapter for a first-time applicant: credential types and Real ID, residency and transfer rules, learner's permit eligibility and supervised practice, the vision screening, when the knowledge exam is required, and how the driving skills exam is booked, staffed and scored.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: obtaining a new credential, permits and exams
Audience: every first-time Indiana applicant about to apply for a learner's permit, plus new residents transferring an out-of-state or out-of-country license and parents supervising teen practice.
Why it matters to a first-timer: this chapter sets the documents, vision screening and knowledge exam needed for a permit [A01] and states what the knowledge exam covers and when the driving skills exam is required [A02].
Level: procedural recall with several exact age and time thresholds (permit ages, supervising-driver ages, holding periods, expiry windows, booking windows); low conceptual load.
Prerequisites: none; this is the first chapter of the manual and Appendix A supplies the document lists it refers to.
