## A — Exam authority, Section Sixteen Emergencies and roadside assistance

This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination. The issuing authority is the West Virginia Division of Motor Vehicles within the West Virginia Department of Transportation, and the source is the current revised edition of the West Virginia Driver's Licensing Handbook pinned in the run configuration. The registrar verified no official question count, passing score, test format or time limit for the knowledge examination, so this dossier asserts no value for any of them. The handbook's own examination chapter describes a question minimum and a passing line in its text; those lines travel only as cited knowledge facts in the knowledge-examination section of this run, never as verified exam facts. Practice scoring follows only the accepted LearningLoop product policy. Section Sixteen covers the pre-drive checks of brakes, leaks, the engine bay and tires, blowouts, brake failure, overheating, skids, a stuck gas pedal, wheels off the road, steering and headlight failure, vehicle fires, a vehicle approaching in your lane, stalling on railroad tracks, the oil pressure light, jump starting, emergency and first aid kits, first aid order, the Good Samaritan law, crash duties and reporting, the Courtesy Patrol, and the littering and vehicle waste rules.
## B — Audience, Section Sixteen Emergencies and roadside assistance

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the West Virginia Class E knowledge examination, reading at a plain-English level with no prior driving knowledge assumed. Section Sixteen teaches how to prevent, recognize and respond to roadside emergencies and what a driver owes at a crash scene. It depends on the driver responsibilities chapter for the crash reporting threshold and on the weather chapter for skid and brake techniques it extends. The rules progress from vehicle checks, through each emergency response in turn, to first aid, crash duties, roadside assistance and waste disposal.
