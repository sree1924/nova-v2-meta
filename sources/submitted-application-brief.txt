## Question 1: What do you want to build for this hackathon?

**NOVA V2 — A Controlled Evaluation of Meta AI Models**

I want to evaluate Meta's AI models within NOVA, a contract-governed AI Agent Factory I developed as a personal engineering hobby.

NOVA V1 established a reusable production framework spanning 65 educational stores and 130 learning probes, with minimal manual intervention.

For this hackathon, I propose integrating Meta's Model API into the existing NOVA Belt and selecting one V1 educational probe as a controlled baseline.

The objective is knowledge decompression: determining whether Meta's models can transform compressed instructional content into deeper explanations, stronger conceptual understanding, contextual examples, and better assessments.

Meta's models will operate within the same bounded contracts, structured knowledge, quality gates, and independent verification framework used by the existing belt.

I will evaluate instructional depth, factual accuracy, assessment quality, execution cost, and human intervention against the V1 baseline.

The deliverable will be a working Meta-powered V2 prototype with documented comparative results.

This is a model evaluation, not a predetermined technology adoption decision. If Meta's models demonstrate meaningful improvements, the architecture allows them to be incorporated into future workflows. If not, the experiment will identify their limitations.

My goal is simple: test a capable AI model against a functioning production system and let measurable results determine its value.

## Question 2: Tell us about an AI project you recently built that showcases your technical skills

**NOVA — A One-Person AI Agent Factory Built as a Hobby**

In June 2026, I started NOVA as a personal engineering hobby to explore a question: How far can one person scale software production through autonomous AI agents with minimal human intervention?

Within three weeks, I designed and built NOVA V1, a reusable, contract-governed multi-agent production framework called the NOVA Belt.

Rather than manually coding individual applications or writing educational material, I architected a system of specialized AI agents responsible for domain research, knowledge engineering, instructional content, audio lessons, flashcards, assessments, software development, testing, and deployment.

The technical foundation includes standardized agent contracts, structured knowledge models, bounded execution responsibilities, independent multi-model validation, automated quality gates, and reusable application infrastructure.

**Production milestones:**

- Built the V1 production framework in approximately three weeks.
- Launched 20 public-facing stores by the second month.
- Subsequently deployed 40 stores within a month.
- Expanded the overall portfolio to 65 educational stores delivering 130 domain-specific learning probes.

**Public examples produced through this framework:**

https://passmycitizenship.com

https://passmycna.com

https://passradtech.com

https://cdlexamcoach.com

https://passcosmetology.com

V1 was intentionally an engineering foundation and public validation experiment, not a fully polished commercial product.

The next evolution, NOVA V2, focuses on knowledge decompression: transforming compact educational material into comprehensive explanations, deeper conceptual instruction, and improved learning experiences.

The distinguishing technical accomplishment is that I built and governed this production system as one individual, while specialized AI agents performed most implementation tasks with minimal human intervention. Human involvement remained focused on architecture, contracts, exception handling, validation, and final release decisions.

NOVA demonstrates that the practical value of autonomous AI lies not simply in model intelligence, but in strong data models, explicit contracts, repeatable execution, independent verification, and human governance.
