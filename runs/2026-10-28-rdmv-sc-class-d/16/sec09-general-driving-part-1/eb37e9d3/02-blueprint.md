Issuer: the South Carolina Department of Motor Vehicles (SCDMV) publishes the South Carolina Driver's Manual; the front cover names the agency and the manual [A01].

Misconceptions: a green light does not mean go at once, because crossing traffic may still be clearing; another driver's turn signal does not prove they will turn; a three-point turn may not use a driveway, while a two-point turnabout does; amber flashing lights on a stopping school bus already require you to stop; on a multi-lane highway the stopping rule covers drivers behind the bus, while on a two-lane road it covers drivers in both directions.
Mnemonics: right turns hug the right, left turns hug the left; at a stop, look left, right, left; roundabouts go counterclockwise and entering traffic gives way; two-lane road, everyone stops for the bus.
Glossary: turnabout means a maneuver that reverses your direction of travel; DDI means diverging diamond interchange; RCUT means restricted crossing U-turn intersection; roundabout means a circular intersection around a raised center island, also called a traffic circle; stop arm means the sign arm a school bus extends when it stops.
