Misconceptions: points do not vanish once a ticket is paid, they stay on the record and only come off through clean years or approved courses; a defensive driving course cannot be repeated every year for credit; surcharges are separate from court fines and are not reduced by point credits; tickets from other states do follow a New Jersey driver home.
Mnemonics: three points off for Driver Improvement, two off for defensive driving, up to three off per clean year; six points in three years triggers the surcharge; five points for racing, tailgating, reckless driving and passing a school bus; eight points for leaving an injury crash.
Glossary: point system means the MVC record of moving violations weighted by seriousness; violation surcharge means a yearly MVC charge paid for three years; Driver Improvement Program means the course offered after a scheduled suspension notice; Probationary Driver Program means the course required for new drivers with repeat violations; interstate compact means an agreement among states to share violation information.
Dynamic facts: program fees, surcharge amounts, payment channels and the lists of compact member states can change; confirm them on the official MVC website.
