## A — Exam authority for the Alabama driver license knowledge test

Issuer: the Alabama Law Enforcement Agency, Driver License Division, issues Alabama driver licenses [A07] and publishes the Alabama Driver Manual.
Edition: the front cover of the manual prints the edition line "November 2024" under the Driver License Division name [A01]; no effective date is printed, so the effective date stays unconfirmed (verify — sec10-A-EFFECTIVE-DATE).
Exam path: the manual describes a vision screening, a knowledge test with questions on Alabama traffic laws, road signs and safe-driving rules taken from this manual, and a road test given by a driver license examiner [A13].
Learner path: a fifteen-year-old who passes the required exam receives a Class D learner license with the Y restriction [A11].
Delivery: automated testing is available to every applicant taking the knowledge test [A13], and oral and foreign-language versions exist [A11].
Scoring: the manual states no question count, no passing score or percent, and no time limit for the knowledge test; all three stay unknown and no number is asserted here (verify — sec10-A-SCORING).
Question format: beyond automated delivery, the manual does not describe the item format of the knowledge test (verify — sec10-A-FORMAT).
Section link: this dossier section covers the basic speed law and statutory speed limits, where a complete stop is required, school bus stops, right of way, passing, pedestrians, backing, lane changes and hand signals, loads, traffic stops, mandatory liability insurance, window tint and the move-over law, all drawn from the manual the test is built on [A13].
Practice mastery targets in this product are product policy from the accepted learning loop, never an official passing line.
Dynamic facts (updatable): the knowledge test fee, office locations, appointments and online services are set by ALEA and can change between editions, so they are kept out of stable knowledge [A13]; why_dynamic: administrative details outside the printed rules of the road.
## B — Audience and scope

Audience: first-time Alabama applicants who have never held a license and are preparing in English for the driver license knowledge test; this section matters most to every first-time applicant, because speed limits, school bus stops, right of way and passing rules are core knowledge-test material, and to new drivers learning what to do at a traffic stop.
Level: plain-English reading level; no prior driving vocabulary is assumed, so each term from the manual is defined where it first appears.
Prerequisites: the signs, markings and signals of the previous two sections, which these laws refer to.
Section scope: manual chapter six: why traffic laws exist, speed regulations and the basic speed law, statutory and minimum speed limits, places that require slow speeds, the list of required complete stops including red-light turns and school or church buses, school bus warning lights, right-of-way and yielding rules, no-passing zones and passing on the left and right, when not to cross the center line, pedestrian duties and safety rules, following emergency vehicles and fire hoses, backing, shoulders, window obstructions, changing lanes, hand signals, improper turns, load limits, what to do during a law enforcement stop, documents required, the mandatory liability insurance law, window tinting and the move-over law.
Out of scope: commercial, motorcycle-manual and vessel testing content, office logistics and fees, and anything not printed on the selected manual pages.
