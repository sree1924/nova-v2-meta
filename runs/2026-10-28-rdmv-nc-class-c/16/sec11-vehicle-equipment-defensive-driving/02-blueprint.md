Misconceptions: pumping the pedal does not help anti-lock brakes, which need steady hard pressure; turn-signal lights are legal but not always visible in low sun; mirrors do not cover the rear corners, so a head check is still required; giving room to the riskier hazard can mean moving closer to oncoming cars; the horn is for reasonable warning only, not routine use; hand signals are harder to see at night than flashing signals.
Mnemonics: hand signals point the way, up is right, down is stop; look far ahead, glance back often, check over the shoulder; brake hard and hold with ABS.
Glossary: ABS means an anti-lock braking system that keeps wheels from locking under hard braking; blind spot means an area near the rear corners not visible in the mirrors; emergency flashers means all four turn signals blinking together as a warning; margin of safety means open space kept around your vehicle; carbon monoxide means a poisonous exhaust gas that can enter the cabin through a leak.
Dynamic facts: the fuel cost figures in this section are economic estimates that can drift with fuel prices and are flagged as dynamic.
