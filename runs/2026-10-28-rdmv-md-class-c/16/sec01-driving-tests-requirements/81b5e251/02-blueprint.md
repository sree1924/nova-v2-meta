## A — Exam authority for the Maryland MVA knowledge test

Issuer: the Maryland Department of Transportation Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA) sets the licensing requirements and gives the knowledge test [A07].
Publication: the manual's copyright page names the MDOT/MVA as the owner of the Maryland Driver's Manual [A02].
Edition: the copyright page prints the form line "DL-002 (10-26)" and the words "October 2026 Edition" [A02]; the front cover prints no edition, so this run's source liveness is unconfirmed and the current edition and effective date stay unconfirmed by the issuer (verify — sec01-A-EDITION).
License class: the manual's licensing chapter calls the noncommercial license for cars, pickup trucks and vans a Class C driver's license [A09].
Exam path: a first license requires passing a vision screening, a knowledge test and a driving skills test [A07].
Knowledge test: it covers motor vehicle laws, safe driving practices and traffic signs, is taken at an MVA location, and the manual says it holds all the information that is tested [A07].
Scoring: the manual's knowledge-test paragraph prints a question count and a time allowance, but while the source edition is unconfirmed neither figure is asserted here; the manual states no passing score or percent anywhere (verify — sec01-A-SCORING).
Question format: the manual does not describe the item format of the knowledge test (verify — sec01-A-FORMAT).
Practice: the MVA website hosts an Online Driver Test Tutorial with practice questions [A07]; practice mastery targets in this product are product policy from the accepted learning loop, never an official passing line.
Section focus: this section covers the three licensing tests themselves: the vision standards, what the knowledge test covers and where it is taken, the skills-test appointment and test-vehicle rules, and the duty to report medical conditions. [A07]
Dynamic facts (updatable): MVA phone numbers, appointment booking, website addresses, office locations, accepted-document lists and fees can change between editions and are kept out of stable knowledge; why_dynamic: administrative details set by the MVA outside the printed rules of the road.
## B — Audience and scope

Audience: first-time Maryland applicants who have never held a driver's license and are preparing in English for the MVA knowledge test for a noncommercial Class C license; this includes teens entering the Rookie Driver graduated licensing path, adults licensing for the first time, and holders of out-of-country licenses who must still test.
Level: plain-English reading level; no prior driving vocabulary is assumed, so every term from the manual is defined where it first appears.
Prerequisites: none; this is the first chapter of the manual and sets out the tests every later chapter prepares you for.
Section scope: manual Section One on driving test requirements: the vision screening and its standards, the knowledge test and the online practice tutorial, the driving skills test appointment, the vehicle and supervising-driver rules for test day, and reportable medical conditions.
Out of scope: commercial and motorcycle licensing content, document-by-document identity paperwork beyond what the manual prints, and anything not printed on the selected manual pages.
