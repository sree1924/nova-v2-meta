FACT_ID: sec04-F15 | anchor: A03

FACT_ID: sec04-F16 | anchor: A04

FACT_ID: sec04-F18 | anchor: A04

## C2 - Confusables and KG hints, Chapter Four Traffic control

FACT_ID: sec04-R01 | anchor: A01
A stop sign requires a full stop every time before yielding right-of-way—stopping before the stop line, else the crosswalk, else the nearest point with a view of cross traffic—whereas a yield sign requires slowing down, checking traffic, yielding right-of-way, and stopping fully only when traffic conditions require it [A01].
Why it matters: group signs; difficulty medium; prereq none; confusables sec04-F02, sec04-F03 [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=29]

FACT_ID: sec04-R02 | anchor: A02
Yellow warnings advise caution with advisory speeds while orange work signs command slowdowns with enforceable limits and flag obedience, advice versus orders [A02].
Why it matters: group signs; difficulty medium; prereq none; confusables sec04-F05, sec04-F06 [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=30]

FACT_ID: sec04-R03 | anchor: A03
Steady red allows right turns and one-way-to-one-way lefts after stopping, a red arrow allows nothing, and city reds allow nothing unposted, three reds with three answers [A03].
Why it matters: group signals; difficulty high; prereq none; confusables sec04-F10, sec04-F11 [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=31]

FACT_ID: sec04-R04 | anchor: A03
Flashing red copies the stop sign while flashing yellow only urges caution, and steady yellow warns of red while a yellow arrow ends protected turning [A03].
Why it matters: group signals; difficulty high; prereq none; confusables sec04-F11, sec04-F12 [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=31]

FACT_ID: sec04-R05 | anchor: A04
Broken center lines invite passing, solid-and-broken splits permission by side, double solid forbids everything but lefts, and single solid allows only forced moves, a four-step strictness ladder [A04].
Why it matters: group markings; difficulty high; prereq none; confusables sec04-F16, sec04-F17, sec04-F18 [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=32]

FACT_ID: sec04-R06 | anchor: A04
Stop lines and crosswalks trigger stops only when a device, officer or yielding user demands, so bare paint alone never forces a halt [A04].
Why it matters: group markings; difficulty medium; prereq sec04-F02; confusables sec04-F19 [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=32]

Diamond lanes exclude general traffic except posted compliers and corner-turning entrants, while officer orders override every marking including the diamonds themselves [A05].
Why it matters: group priority; difficulty medium; prereq none; confusables sec04-F21, sec04-F22 [A05].
