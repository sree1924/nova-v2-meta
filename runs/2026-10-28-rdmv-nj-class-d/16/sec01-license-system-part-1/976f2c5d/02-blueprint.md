## A — Exam authority for the license laws, license types and ID verification section (New Jersey Class D basic license knowledge test)
Issuer: the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC) publishes the New Jersey Driver Manual, which prepares applicants for the knowledge portion of the driver test [A01].
Source document: New Jersey Driver Manual, with 2026 printed on the front cover [A02].
Knowledge test: 50 questions, plus a survey question about organ donation [A03].
Passing standard: 80 percent, which means 40 of the 50 questions must be answered correctly [A04].
Vision screening is required for every motorist, and basic license applicants need 20/50 vision with or without corrective lenses [A04].
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify NJ-LIC-A-TIME).
This section feeds the knowledge test: the basic license laws (what to carry, address and name-change deadlines, new-resident deadlines), the license types and ages, how cards are issued, and the Standard and REAL ID document rules.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: license laws, license types and ID verification
Audience: first-time New Jersey applicants for the Class D basic driver license, plus new residents transferring a license, preparing for the knowledge test in English.
Why it matters to a first-timer: anyone never licensed must enter the Graduated Driver License Program [A01], and every applicant must assemble the identity, address and Social Security documents before the first visit [A02].
Level: administrative recall of deadlines, ages, fees and document rules; low conceptual load but many look-alike time limits and two parallel document systems (Standard and REAL ID).
Prerequisites: none; this is the opening chapter of the manual and sets up the GDL and testing sections that follow.

Misconceptions: a validated permit does not let you drive alone; the address-change deadline applies even if you are leaving the state; a temporary paper license is not the finished card, which arrives by mail; a foreign photo license cannot stand alone as identity proof.
Mnemonics: REAL ID is two plus one plus six (two address proofs, one full Social Security proof, six points); a move is reported in one week and a name change in two weeks — Move fast, Name next.
Glossary: GDL means the Graduated Driver License Program for new drivers; REAL ID means the federally compliant license version proven with the two-plus-one-plus-six model; endorsement means an added privilege on a license, such as M for motorcycle; primary and secondary documents are the two tiers of identity proof that add up to the six points.
Dynamic facts: license and permit fees, phone numbers, website addresses and online document selectors can change; the manual itself says fees are subject to change, so confirm current values on the MVC website.
