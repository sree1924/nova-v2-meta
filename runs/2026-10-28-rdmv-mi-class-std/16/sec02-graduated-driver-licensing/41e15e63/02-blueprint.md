## A — Exam authority, Graduated Driver Licensing

This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the regular passenger-car written knowledge and road sign tests in Michigan. The issuing authority is the Michigan Department of State, led by the Secretary of State, and the source is the handbook What Every Driver Must Know. This section covers Graduated Driver Licensing for teens under eighteen, the driver education segments, the Level One, Level Two and Level Three licenses with their restrictions, adults who were never licensed, and the identity, legal presence and residency documents. The handbook pages do not state an official question count, passing score, test format or time limit for the Secretary of State knowledge test, so this dossier asserts no value for any of them and records each as an explicit unknown. Practice scoring follows only the accepted LearningLoop product policy, never an official threshold.

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants for a Michigan operator's license, reading at a plain-English level, plus newcomers and parents of teen drivers who use the same handbook.
