Issuer: the Colorado Division of Motor Vehicles administers driver licensing and the written test described in this handbook [A01].
Exam: the Driving Knowledge Test, also called the written test, covers driving knowledge and safety [A01].

Relevance of this section: bicyclists, motorcyclists, pedestrians and distracted driving falls inside that whole-handbook scope [A01].

## B — Audience and scope: bicyclists, motorcyclists, pedestrians and distracted driving

Audience: first-time Colorado applicants preparing for the written driving knowledge test, including teens seeking an instruction permit and adults new to the state or never licensed.
Level: plain English at a middle-school reading level; many applicants are English learners, so terms are defined on first use and legal phrasing is restated simply.
Prerequisites: none assumed beyond basic familiarity with riding in a car; no prior driving experience or traffic-law vocabulary is expected.
