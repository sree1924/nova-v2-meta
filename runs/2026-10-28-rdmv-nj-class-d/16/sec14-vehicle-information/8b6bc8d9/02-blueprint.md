## A — Exam authority for the vehicle title, registration, plates, inspection and insurance section (New Jersey Class D basic license knowledge test)
Issuer: the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC) publishes the New Jersey Driver Manual, which prepares applicants for the knowledge portion of the driver test [A01].
Source document: New Jersey Driver Manual, with 2026 printed on the front cover [A02].
Knowledge test: 50 questions, plus a survey question about organ donation [A03].
Passing standard: 80 percent, which means 40 of the 50 questions must be answered correctly [A04].
Vision screening is required for every motorist, and basic license applicants need 20/50 vision with or without corrective lenses [A04].
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify NJ-VEH-A-TIME).
This section feeds the knowledge test: titling, registering and insuring a vehicle before driving, the new-resident deadline, registration terms, initial registration documents, plate placement, height and lighting, plate covers and counterfeit plates, returning plates, inspection schedules for passenger, diesel and commercial vehicles, new car dealer stickers, mandatory liability insurance, the insurance ID card and insurance fraud.

Misconceptions: a brand-new car does not skip registration paperwork, it simply gets a longer first term; a reflective plate still needs a working plate light; the insurance card belongs in the vehicle, not at home, though a phone version counts; canceling insurance means returning the plates, not just keeping the car parked.
Mnemonics: sixty days to title and register after moving in, fourteen days to inspect an older car; four years for a brand-new car's first registration, one year after that; plates between one foot and four feet high; rear plate readable from fifty feet.
Glossary: title means the document that names a vehicle's legal owner; registration means the state record that lets a vehicle be driven on public roads; PIF means a State-licensed private inspection facility; new car dealer sticker means the inspection sticker given to new vehicles in place of an early inspection; liability insurance means coverage for harm you cause to others.
Dynamic facts: inspection rules, plate fines, the mailing addresses for returning plates and the MVC and insurance phone numbers can change; confirm them on official New Jersey websites.
