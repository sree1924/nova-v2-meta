## C — Backing, parking, visibility, hazards

FACT_ID: sec10-F01 | anchor: A01
Drivers backing up most often fail to look both ways behind; mirrors leave gaps, so twist body and head right to sight through the rear window, reverse slowly, and flick glances to each side for walkers and traffic [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec10-F02 | anchor: A01
Park as far from traffic as the road allows: deep onto shoulders, tight to curbs within one foot, on the right of two-way streets, and on either side of one-way roads [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec10-F03 | anchor: A01
Where a curb exists on a hill, set the front wheels to block a streetward roll: aim them left when parked facing uphill and right when parked facing downhill [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec10-F04 | anchor: A01
Without a curb on a hill, point the front wheels right so any roll sends the rear away from traffic, whether facing uphill or downhill [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec10-F05 | anchor: A01
Before swinging a door open, scan for cars, bicyclists, and pedestrians around the parked vehicle [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec10-F06 | anchor: A01
Parking is banned beside another parked vehicle, on crosswalks and sidewalks, ahead of driveways, at yellow curbs and No Parking signs, in disabled spaces and their striped aisles, on hard road surface where no curb runs, in bike lanes, and anywhere the car blocks or endangers a traffic lane [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec10-F07 | anchor: A01
Keep clear within 15 feet of hydrants, within 20 feet of intersections, within 15 feet of fire, ambulance, or rescue station entrances, within 500 feet of fire equipment working an alarm, and within 50 feet of railroad crossings [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec10-F08 | anchor: A01
Crashes trace most to missed risks: keep track of surrounding vehicles, scan ahead, to the sides, and behind, stay ready for walkers and cyclists, and switch headlights on at night and in poor sight [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec10-F09 | anchor: A01
State law demands headlights in rain, fog, snow, or sleet once sight drops to 500 feet, and whenever wipers run for bad weather; burning them even by day lifts your profile to others [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec10-F10 | anchor: A02
Answer fading sight and slick pavement by slowing first, doubling the normal gap ahead, and lighting the headlamps [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec10-F11 | anchor: A02
Burn headlights from sunset to sunrise and from first fading light; take low-beams in lit towns, against oncoming traffic, and within 200 feet behind another vehicle, reserving high-beams for highways clear of approaching vehicles within 500 feet [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec10-F12 | anchor: A02
Faced with stubborn high-beams ahead, avert your stare toward the road edge, flick ahead to fix the other car's place, and repeat past it; never fire your own high-beams back [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec10-F13 | anchor: A02
Fog throws your own beams back at you: drop to low-beams in thick fog, steer by edge markings, and slow since even thin fog warps distance judgment [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec10-F14 | anchor: A02
In rain, run low-beams, wipe light rain and drizzle with fluid ready since dry sprinkles smear, treat the first half-hour as slickest from oil mixing with water, and shun ponded water where the car can hydroplane and lose control [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec10-F15 | anchor: A02
Strip every trace of snow and ice, roof, hood, tail, glass, mirrors, and all lamps, before rolling, since shed slabs menace others; fit snow tires or chains to grip and shorten stops [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec10-F16 | anchor: A02
Treat packed snow like ice: brake softly, shed speed before stops and turns, and to halt on slick sheets ease off the gas with gentle braking, since rolling front tires grip best and steady slowness beats hard braking; expect bridges and shade to ice first [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec10-F17 | anchor: A02
Virginia law brands aggressive driving as aiming to harass, intimidate, injure, or obstruct someone while breaking traffic rules such as skipping stops, yields, control devices, or room for overtakers [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec10-F18 | anchor: A02
Aggressors run signs and signals, speed, tailgate, weave, pass right even on shoulders and unpaved edges, change lanes wildly, and gesture, scream, honk, and flash; counter by yielding space, refusing races and stares, and ignoring the noise [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec10-F19 | anchor: A02
An aggressive-driving conviction can suspend a license for ten days or as long as six months [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0025]
