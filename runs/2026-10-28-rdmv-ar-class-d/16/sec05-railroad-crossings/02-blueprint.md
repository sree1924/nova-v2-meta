## Audience and source scope
Passenger-car applicants need to recognize advance railroad warnings, crossbucks, flashing lights, gates and multiple-track warnings. Teach the difference between approaching a closed crossing and already being on the tracks when a gate begins descending. Emergency-notification information is for identifying the responsible railroad and exact crossing, not an invented universal phone number.
