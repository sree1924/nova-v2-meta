## C — Safety Responsibility

FACT_ID: sec03-F01 | anchor: A01
The Texas Motor Vehicle Safety Responsibility Act makes every owner and operator of a motor vehicle financially answerable for death, injury, or property damage they cause, with at least minimum liability insurance required [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec03-F02 | anchor: A01
Minimum liability coverage runs thirty thousand dollars for injury or death of one person, sixty thousand dollars for two or more people, and twenty-five thousand dollars for property damage [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec03-F03 | anchor: A01
Every driver keeps liability insurance or qualifies as self-insured unless exempt, shows proof when licensing, registering, or inspecting, and hands proof to any officer on request or any person involved in a crash [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec03-F04 | anchor: A01
Accepted proof starts with a minimum-limits policy, a standard insurance-department proof form naming insurer, policy number and period, each insured party, limits or a compliance statement, and each covered vehicle's make and model, plus a compliance-showing insurance binder [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec03-F05 | anchor: A01
Further proof includes a comptroller certificate over a fifty-five-thousand-dollar money or securities deposit, a DPS surety bond on file, a county-judge certificate over a fifty-five-thousand-dollar cash or cashier's check deposit, or a DPS self-insurance certificate for owners of more than twenty-five vehicles [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec03-F06 | anchor: A01
A wireless-device image carrying the standard-form facts also proves coverage, missing proof draws a citation, and courts may dismiss when a policy covered the citation moment [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec03-F07 | anchor: A01
Conviction without sufficient proof fines one hundred seventy-five to three hundred fifty dollars first, then suspends the license, fines three hundred fifty to one thousand dollars, and court-impounds an owner-driven vehicle one hundred eighty days with proof due before release [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0024]

FACT_ID: sec03-F08 | anchor: A02
Registration, license, or privilege suspends after a suspendable traffic conviction lacking insurance proof to DPS, an unpaid crash judgment past sixty days, or a defaulted crash-installment agreement [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec03-F09 | anchor: A02
Suspension also follows an uninsured crash killing, injuring, or inflicting one thousand dollars of property damage where judgment looks likely, and proof must be maintained two years from the latest conviction date [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0025]

FACT_ID: sec03-F10 | anchor: A02
Deeper compliance guidance lives on the DPS financial-responsibility pages and with the Enforcement and Compliance Service in Austin [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0025]
