Exam: the Driving Knowledge Test, also called the written test, covers driving knowledge and safety [A01].

Relevance of this section: reckless and aggressive driving, emergencies and crashes falls inside that whole-handbook scope [A01].

FACT_ID: sec15-R04 | anchor: A02
Brake failure starts with pumping the pedal, then the parking brake applied slowly, then lower gears; a jammed gas pedal calls for shifting to neutral. [A02]

Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0029]

FACT_ID: sec15-R05 | anchor: A02
A blowout calls for gentle braking while holding straight, not hard braking or an immediate stop on the road. [A02]

FACT_ID: sec15-R06 | anchor: A02
When vehicles are drivable and neither injuries nor drugs or alcohol are involved, the Move It Law requires clearing a minor divided-highway crash off the road, whereas Accident Alert Status requires exchanging details and reporting within twenty-four hours [A02].

Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0029]

FACT_ID: sec15-R07 | anchor: A02
Swerving hard to miss a large animal can be more dangerous than hitting it, and running off the road beats a head-on crash. [A02]

FACT_ID: sec15-R08 | anchor: A03
For any stationary highway vehicle flashing its hazards, the Move Over Law requires moving over one lane when safe, or slowing to at least twenty miles per hour below the posted speed limit if unsafe or on a road with one lane each way [A03].
Why it matters: group crashes; difficulty two; prereq sec15-F33. [A03]
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0030]

Misconception: staring down an aggressive driver shows you will not be bullied; avoid eye contact and let them go. [A01]
Misconception: you must leave cars where they stopped after any crash until police arrive; the Move It Law requires moving them off in a qualifying minor crash. [A02]
Misconception: easing off the pedal when ABS pulses is correct; keep pressing, because ABS only works with the pedal down. [A01]
Misconception: a Good Samaritan should move every victim to safety; move a victim only if their life is in danger. [A03]
Mnemonic: Slow, steer or speed: the three ways out. [A01]
Mnemonic: Pump, park, gear down: brake failure. [A02]
Glossary: Reckless driving means driving that endangers people or property, intended or not. [A01]
Glossary: Accident Alert Status means a period of severe weather when officers cannot respond to every crash. [A02]
Glossary: Move Over Law means the rule to change lanes or slow down for stopped vehicles with hazards flashing. [A03]
Glossary: Good Samaritan immunity means legal protection for a reasonable, prudent rescuer from being sued over a victim's injury. [A03]
