Misconceptions: on a road with a center turning lane but only two lanes, both directions still stop for a school bus; only a median or a wider road with a turning lane frees oncoming traffic; a pedestrian crossing on green beats a turning car; the move-over rule applies to parked public service vehicles too, not only police; signing a ticket is not a confession; a farm operator's left-hand signal can mean the machine is about to turn left, not that you may pass.
Mnemonics: median or many lanes with a middle turn lane, only followers halt; lights on a shoulder, move over or slow down.
Glossary: crosswalk means a marked or implied pedestrian crossing at an intersection; mechanical stop signal means the stop arm a school bus extends; move-over law means the duty to change lanes or slow near a stopped emergency or service vehicle; limited-access highway means a road entered only at interchanges; infraction means a noncriminal violation.
Dynamic facts: school bus hours are typical times rather than fixed rules, and traffic-stop procedures and fines can change by statute; confirm current law.
