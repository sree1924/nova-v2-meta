Misconceptions: turning the ignition off while moving does not stop the car safely, it locks the steering; braking during a side skid or a blowout makes things worse; a quiet road with no signals is not protected; a burning car is not something to put out yourself; jerking the wheel to get off the shoulder causes a slingshot into traffic.
Mnemonics: night table goes seventy-five, two-eleven, four-oh-nine feet at twenty, forty and sixty; stop, turn or speed up; off the shoulder at a quarter turn once at or under twenty-five; run toward the train at an angle; give way, same direction, still, never head-on.
Glossary: reaction distance means ground covered before the brake is pressed; braking distance means ground needed to bring the vehicle to a full stop; uncontrolled intersection means a crossing with no lights or signs; work zone means a road construction area announced by an advance warning sign; glancing blow means a sideswipe instead of a direct hit.
Dynamic facts: the nationwide work zone crash statistics and the property damage threshold for written crash reports can change; confirm them on official New Jersey and federal websites.
