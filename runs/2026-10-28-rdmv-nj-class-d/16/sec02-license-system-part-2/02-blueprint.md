## A — Exam authority for the graduated licensing, permits and registries section (New Jersey Class D basic license knowledge test)
Issuer: the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC) publishes the New Jersey Driver Manual, which prepares applicants for the knowledge portion of the driver test [A01].
Source document: New Jersey Driver Manual, with 2026 printed on the front cover [A02].
Knowledge test: 50 questions, plus a survey question about organ donation [A03].
Passing standard: 80 percent, which means 40 of the 50 questions must be answered correctly [A04].
Vision screening is required for every motorist, and basic license applicants need 20/50 vision with or without corrective lenses [A04].
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify NJ-GDL-A-TIME).
This section feeds the knowledge test: the three GDL roads and their ages, the curfew, phone, passenger, seat belt and decal restrictions, special learner versus examination permits, newcomer and visitor rules, the non-driver ID and the donor, next-of-kin and voter options.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: graduated licensing, permits and registries
Audience: first-time New Jersey drivers of every age entering the Graduated Driver License Program, their parents or supervising drivers, and newcomers from other states or countries.
Why it matters to a first-timer: every never-licensed driver passes through the GDL stages [A01], and the permit and probationary restrictions are classic knowledge-test items [A05].
Level: rule recall with several look-alike thresholds (ages, months, hours, passenger limits); moderate load because three roads run in parallel.
Prerequisites: the license-law and identity-document section that opens the manual.

Misconceptions: turning eighteen does not end probationary restrictions by itself, because the driver must visit a Licensing Center to upgrade; hands-free phones are banned for GDL drivers, not just hand-held ones; a license from another state does not remove the need for a New Jersey examination permit; an International Driving Permit is not a license.
Mnemonics: the three GDL roads are Early bird, Young adult and Adult, starting at ages sixteen, seventeen and twenty-one; the curfew starts one minute after eleven at night and ends at five in the morning.
Glossary: GDL means the Graduated Driver License Program; special learner permit means the permit bought by a driving-school instructor for a student in a behind-the-wheel course; examination permit means the permit for applicants who practice with a supervising driver and no instructor; probationary license means the restricted license held for a year before the basic license; supervising driver means an adult who meets the age, license and experience rules.
Dynamic facts: decal prices, fines, phone numbers and online services can change and are isolated as dynamic; confirm them on the MVC website.
