Memory cue, authored product policy: “See and be seen; space to stop, space to turn”. The cue retrieves complete cited rules and does not replace their conditions.
Terminology: A no-zone is a truck blind area. A squeeze hazard is the space beside a wide-turning truck or bus that closes as it turns toward the curb.
Misconception to correct: A narrow motorcycle needs only part of a lane, or an empty gap ahead of a truck is unused space. The guide reserves a full lane for motorcycles and stopping room for trucks.
