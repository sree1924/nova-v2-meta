## A — Exam authority for the occupant protection, vehicle condition and starting the car section (New Jersey Class D basic license knowledge test)
Issuer: the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC) publishes the New Jersey Driver Manual, which prepares applicants for the knowledge portion of the driver test [A01].

Knowledge test: 50 questions, plus a survey question about organ donation [A03].
Passing standard: 80 percent, which means 40 of the 50 questions must be answered correctly [A04].
Vision screening is required for every motorist, and basic license applicants need 20/50 vision with or without corrective lenses [A04].
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify NJ-BELT-A-TIME).

## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: occupant protection, vehicle condition and starting the car
Audience: first-time New Jersey Class D applicants, including teen GDL drivers and adults who will carry children as passengers.

Mnemonics: child seats step up by age two, four and eight — rear-facing, harness, then booster; stop within twenty-five feet at twenty miles per hour; idle no more than three minutes; the edge of a penny marks the thinnest legal tread.
Glossary: primary offense means a violation an officer can stop you for by itself; secondary law means a violation that can be ticketed only after a stop for another reason; ABS means an anti-lock braking system; SRS and SIR mean supplemental restraint system and supplemental inflatable restraint, the air bag markings; carbon monoxide means the odorless, colorless gas in vehicle exhaust; five-point harness means the harness the child passenger law requires in rear-facing and forward-facing child restraints.
