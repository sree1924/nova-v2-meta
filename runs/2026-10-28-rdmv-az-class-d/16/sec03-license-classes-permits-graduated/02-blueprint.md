## A — Exam authority for the license classes, instruction permits and the graduated license section (Arizona Class D operator license, written test)
Issuer: the Arizona Department of Transportation Motor Vehicle Division (ADOT MVD) provides this manual as its guide to Arizona traffic laws and to obtaining a driver license or identification card [A05].
Source document: Arizona Driver License Manual and Customer Service Guide, whose contents page carries the line Revised September 2026 [A03].
Purpose: the manual explains Arizona motor vehicle laws without technical language to help readers obtain a driver license or instruction permit, and refers to Arizona Revised Statutes Title 28 for the most current law [A03].
Written test: MVD issues an Arizona instruction permit only after the applicant passes the Division written test; commercial learner permits are excluded from this rule [A01].
Practice: the manual sends applicants to the MVD website to practice the written test [A02].

## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: license classes, instruction permits and the graduated license
Audience: Arizona teens seeking a graduated instruction permit and graduated license, adults seeking an operator permit and Class D license, and the parents or guardians who sign for minors.
Why it matters to a first-timer: the permit age, supervisor rules and first-months night and passenger limits govern every early drive [A01], and the hold period, practice hours and penalty ladder decide when a teen can be licensed and what a violation costs [A02].
Level: dense recall of ages, durations, hour counts and exceptions; high risk of mixing up similar numbers, so confusable pairs are drilled in C2.
Prerequisites: none; this section introduces the license classes that the driving chapters assume.
