Misconceptions:
- A parking ticket adds points: false, parking and defective equipment violations carry no points.
- Ten points means an automatic fixed suspension: false, ten points brings a suspension letter and a hearing right, and the length depends on points within two years.
- All speeding is the same number of points: false, speeding runs from two to eight points depending on speed over the limit.
- Failing to yield to a pedestrian equals failing to yield to an ambulance: false, the pedestrian offense is four points and the emergency vehicle offense is five.

Mnemonics:
- Two, two, four, four, five, five: license and red light, officer and crosswalk, emergency vehicle and school bus.
- Ten triggers, two years measures: the suspension letter and the window that sets its length.

Glossary:
- Point system: the Vermont record that adds points for each motor vehicle conviction and suspends the privilege at ten
- Suspension hearing: the hearing a driver may request after the ten point letter to verify the convictions and point total
- Defective equipment violation: a vehicle condition offense that carries no points

- None: every rule in this section is stable handbook law for the pined edition; no officeholder, date or fee amount is carried as knowledge.
