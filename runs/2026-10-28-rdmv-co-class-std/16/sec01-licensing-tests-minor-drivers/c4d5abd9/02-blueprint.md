## A — Exam authority: Colorado driving knowledge test

Issuer: the Colorado Division of Motor Vehicles administers driver licensing and the written test described in this handbook [A01].
Exam: the Driving Knowledge Test, also called the written test, covers driving knowledge and safety [A01].
Scope of questions: road signs, driving under the influence, driving rules, safety rules and legal items drawn from the whole handbook [A01].
Format: the handbook describes it as a written test with practice quizzes offered online and in the myColorado app; the item format is not stated (verify — sec01-D-A03) [A01].
Outcome: passing the written test makes an applicant eligible for an instruction permit [A01].
Scheduling: written tests are not given shortly before an office closes; see the cited page for the exact cutoff [A01].
Question count: not stated on the handbook pages (verify — sec01-D-A01).
Passing standard: not stated on the handbook pages (verify — sec01-D-A02).
Time limit: not stated on the handbook pages (verify — sec01-D-A04).
Edition: the handbook carries a form number and revision date on its contents page; effective date is not stated (verify — sec01-D-A05).
Stability: licensing rules come from state statute and the handbook is revised at most yearly; no volatility signal in this section.
Dynamic facts isolated: whether state offices schedule drive tests may change, and the handbook itself says so; treat drive-test availability as updatable, not stable knowledge [A01].
