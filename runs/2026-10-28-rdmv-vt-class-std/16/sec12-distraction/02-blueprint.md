FACT_ID: sec12-F13 | anchor: A02
Vermont law forbids a driver from using a portable electronic device while operating a vehicle on a public highway, and the ban still applies when the vehicle is stopped [A02].

FACT_ID: sec12-F14 | anchor: A02
A first violation of the portable electronic device law carries a fine of at least one hundred dollars and at most two hundred dollars, and a second or later violation a fine of at least two hundred fifty dollars and at most five hundred dollars [A02].
Why it matters: Two fine ranges.
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-vt-class-std:vermont-department-of-motor-vehicles-age:handbook:pdf-0056]

FACT_ID: sec12-F15 | anchor: A02
A conviction for using a portable electronic device while driving in a properly designated work zone with construction, maintenance or utility personnel present adds two points to the driving record for a first conviction and five points for a second or later conviction [A02].
Why it matters: Work zone device use adds points.
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-vt-class-std:vermont-department-of-motor-vehicles-age:handbook:pdf-0056]

FACT_ID: sec12-F16 | anchor: A02
Driving requires full attention, so if a call must be made ask a passenger to place it or stop at a safe place first [A02].
Why it matters: Passenger or pull over.
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-vt-class-std:vermont-department-of-motor-vehicles-age:handbook:pdf-0056]

Misconceptions:
- Texting at a red light is legal because the car is not moving: false, the device ban covers a stationary vehicle on a public highway.
- A hands free rule means phones are fine as long as you do not text: false, the law bars use of a portable electronic device while operating the vehicle, and the handbook says to let a passenger call or pull over.
- Device points apply to every violation: false, points attach only to convictions in a designated work zone with workers present.
- Only phones distract: false, the handbook names food, children, pets, grooming, smoking, loud stereos and strong emotions as distractions.

Mnemonics:
- Hundred to two hundred first, two fifty to five hundred next: the device fine ranges.
- Two then five in the zone: work zone points for a first and a repeat device conviction.
- Passenger or pull over: the only two ways to make a call.

Glossary:
- Portable electronic device: a handheld device such as a phone, tablet or laptop whose use is barred while operating a vehicle on a public highway, even when stopped
- Margin of safety: the cushion of time and space a driver keeps against surprises, which distraction shrinks
- Designated work zone: a properly marked highway area with construction, maintenance or utility workers present, where device convictions add points
