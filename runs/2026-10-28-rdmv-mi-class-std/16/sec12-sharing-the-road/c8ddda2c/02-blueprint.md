This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the regular passenger-car written knowledge and road sign tests in Michigan. The issuing authority is the Michigan Department of State, led by the Secretary of State, and the source is the handbook What Every Driver Must Know. This section covers commercial vehicles with their stopping distances, no zones and wide turns, passing trucks safely, slow-moving vehicles and horse-drawn buggies, pedestrians including those with white canes or guide dogs, children, motorcycles, scooters and mopeds, bicyclists with the passing distance law, and autocycles. ## B — Audience, Sharing the road

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants for a Michigan operator's license, reading at a plain-English level, plus newcomers and parents of teen drivers who use the same handbook. This section covers commercial vehicles with their stopping distances, no zones and wide turns, passing trucks safely, slow-moving vehicles and horse-drawn buggies, pedestrians including those with white canes or guide dogs, children, motorcycles, scooters and mopeds, bicyclists with the passing distance law, and autocycles.
