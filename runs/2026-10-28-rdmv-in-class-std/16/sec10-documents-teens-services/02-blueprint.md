## A — Exam authority for the document requirements, teens behind the wheel and other BMV services section (Indiana learner's permit and driver's license knowledge exam)
Issuer: the Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV) publishes the Indiana Driver's Manual; both names appear on the front cover [A02].
Source document: the front cover prints no edition; the back cover reads Published July 2026 and points readers to IN.gov/BMV for updates [A04].
Knowledge exam: a multiple-choice, computer-based exam drawn from Indiana law, defensive driving practices and the manual, which also tests reading of regulatory, warning and traffic signs and signals [A01].
Exam structure: the exam has two components, one on common signs (Chapter 6) and one on traffic rules and laws (Chapters 6, 7 and 8) [A03].
Passing standard: 80 percent or higher correct on each component [A03].
Branch logistics: arrive at least 30 minutes before the branch closes to test that day; after a failure, wait until the next business day to retake [A01].
Question count: the manual gives no number of questions for either component or in total, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify IN-DOCS-A-COUNT).
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown (verify IN-DOCS-A-TIME).
This section feeds the knowledge exam: only indirectly, because the appendices are not among the chapters the sample-exam page names for the two components, so these facts mainly prepare a first-time applicant for the branch visit, with the teen tips reinforcing rules tested from the safe-operation chapter.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: document requirements, teens behind the wheel and other BMV services
Audience: every first-time Indiana applicant gathering documents for a learner's permit, license or ID card, and teens and parents preparing for supervised driving.
Why it matters to a first-timer: missing or wrong documents stop a permit application before any test is taken [A01], and the teen risk factors explain the habits the manual links to teen crashes [A04].
Level: checklist recall (document categories, date windows) plus practical habits; low conceptual load.
Prerequisites: the first chapter's credential types and the learner's permit rules; the safe-operation chapter for the driving rules the teen tips repeat.
