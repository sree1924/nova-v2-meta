FACT_ID: sec10-F04 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec10-F05 | anchor: A01
When turning left watch for approaching traffic in the opposite lane, and if that traffic will reach the intersection at about the same time as you, stop until it is safe to turn [A01].

FACT_ID: sec10-F06 | anchor: A01
While waiting to turn left do not turn the wheels to the left, because a bump from behind could push your vehicle into oncoming traffic [A01].

FACT_ID: sec10-F07 | anchor: A01
Where no sign prohibits a turn on red, traffic facing a red signal may turn right after a complete stop and yielding to pedestrians and traffic, and the same rule covers a left turn from a one-way street onto a one-way street [A01].

FACT_ID: sec10-F08 | anchor: A02
It is against the law to turn your vehicle around near a curve, near the crest of a hill where a vehicle approaching within five hundred feet in either direction cannot see you, or where signs prohibit turning [A02].

FACT_ID: sec10-F09 | anchor: A02
Before backing, check completely around the vehicle for people and obstacles, look to the front, sides and rear and keep looking to the rear while backing, do not depend on the mirror, and back slowly into the proper traffic lane [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0055]

FACT_ID: sec10-F10 | anchor: A02
Backing up while in a traffic lane is not advisable except to back into a parallel parking space, and you must never back across other traffic lanes or through an intersection [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0055]

FACT_ID: sec10-F11 | anchor: A02
A vehicle must always be parked in the same direction as traffic moves on that side of the street, completely off the traveled portion of the road, and double parking is illegal and discourteous [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0055]

FACT_ID: sec10-F12 | anchor: A02
Whenever you park and leave the vehicle, turn off the engine, set the parking brake, lock the vehicle and take the keys, and because getting out on the street side is dangerous, look carefully for bicycles, motorcycles or larger vehicles that might hit your open door or you [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0055]

FACT_ID: sec10-F13 | anchor: A02
Except to avoid a conflict with other traffic or to obey a law, officer or signal, stopping, standing or parking is prohibited on a sidewalk, in front of a public or private driveway, within an intersection, within fifteen feet of a fire hydrant, and on a crosswalk [A02].

FACT_ID: sec10-F14 | anchor: A02
Parking is also prohibited within twenty feet of a crosswalk at an intersection, within thirty feet of any stop sign or traffic control signal, in front of a wheelchair ramp or curb cut, and within fifty feet of the nearest rail of a railroad crossing [A02].

## C — Turning and parking (part two)

FACT_ID: sec10-F15 | anchor: A03
Parking is further prohibited beside or opposite a street excavation or obstruction where it would obstruct traffic, on the traffic side of a vehicle stopped or parked at the curb, on any bridge, elevated structure or highway tunnel, and within twenty feet of a mailbox served by a carrier making daily deliveries by motor vehicle [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0056]

FACT_ID: sec10-F16 | anchor: A03
Parking is prohibited on any controlled-access highway, anywhere on a highway where it endangers the safety and convenience of the traveling public, and wherever official signs prohibit it, and a curb in a no-parking zone may be painted yellow [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0056]

FACT_ID: sec10-F17 | anchor: A03
To angle park on your right, signal and slow down, stay parallel to the curb at least five feet out from the parked vehicles, steer sharply right when your front end is even with the rear of the space, pull slowly into the middle of the space, then straighten the wheel and roll forward until the front wheel barely touches the curb [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0056]

FACT_ID: sec10-F18 | anchor: A03
Backing out of an angle space is hazardous, so do not rely on the rear view mirror alone, move back cautiously watching for a pedestrian or vehicle that may suddenly appear if your view is blocked, and do not back across the center line [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0056]

FACT_ID: sec10-F19 | anchor: A03
When parking on a grade turn the front wheels toward the curb side of the highway so the vehicle will not roll, which means wheels to the curb when facing downhill, wheels away from the curb when facing uphill with a curb, and wheels to the right when facing uphill without a curb [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0056]

FACT_ID: sec10-F20 | anchor: A03
On a grade set the emergency or parking brake and reinforce it by placing an automatic transmission in park, or a manual transmission in reverse on a downgrade and in first gear on an upgrade [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0056]

FACT_ID: sec10-F21 | anchor: A03
Keep change easily accessible in the vehicle for metered parking, and if you must stay in the parked vehicle in a busy area keep the doors locked until you are ready to exit and stay alert to your surroundings [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0056]

FACT_ID: sec10-F22 | anchor: A04
When parallel parking on a two-way street the right wheels must be parallel to and within eighteen inches of the right curb, when parking on the left side of a road the wheels must be within eighteen inches of the left curb, and the vehicle must face the direction of traffic and be centered in the space [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0057]

FACT_ID: sec10-F23 | anchor: A04
To parallel park, choose a space large enough, signal and stop even with the front vehicle about two feet out from it, then when traffic is clear turn the front wheels all the way to the right and back slowly toward the curb while looking behind you rather than relying on the mirror [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0057]

FACT_ID: sec10-F24 | anchor: A04
When your front bumper is opposite the rear bumper of the vehicle ahead, quickly turn the wheel all the way to the left and back slowly toward the vehicle behind without touching it, ending about six inches and never more than eighteen inches from the curb, then straighten the wheels, pull into final position and center the vehicle [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0057]

FACT_ID: sec10-F25 | anchor: A04
When leaving a parallel parking space always signal to show your intentions and make sure traffic is clear before pulling back onto the roadway [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0057]

FACT_ID: sec10-F26 | anchor: A04
Following too closely causes most rear-end collisions, the space ahead of your vehicle is the easiest space to control and is called the following distance, and you must weigh traffic speed and road condition to leave enough distance to stop when necessary [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0057]

FACT_ID: sec10-F27 | anchor: A04
To apply the three-to-four second rule choose a fixed object ahead such as a signpost, tree, overpass or bridge abutment, start counting one thousand one, one thousand two, one thousand three when the vehicle ahead passes it, and if you reach the object before finishing the count you are following too closely [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0057]

FACT_ID: sec10-F28 | anchor: A04
When it is dark or raining the following distance should be at least four seconds, and when there is ice or snow stay at least six seconds behind the vehicle in front [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0057]

FACT_ID: sec10-R06 | anchor: A02

A driver waiting to turn left keeps the wheels straight to avoid being pushed into oncoming traffic, and stops to let opposing traffic through when both would reach the intersection at about the same time [A01].

FACT_ID: sec10-R08 | anchor: A03
An angle-parking entry starts at least five feet out from the parked vehicles, while the exit must never back across the center line [A03].
Why it matters: group angle-parking; difficulty medium; prereq sec10-F17; confusables sec10-F17, sec10-F18 [A03]

FACT_ID: sec10-R09 | anchor: A02
Backing into a parallel space is the only backing the handbook calls acceptable within a traffic lane, and backing across other lanes or through an intersection is never allowed [A02].
Why it matters: group backing; difficulty medium; prereq sec10-F10; confusables sec10-F09, sec10-F10 [A02]

FACT_ID: sec10-R10 | anchor: A03
On a grade a manual transmission is left in reverse when facing downhill but in first gear when facing uphill, while an automatic transmission simply goes into park [A03].
