## A — Exam authority for the sign colors and shapes, regulatory, warning, construction and guide signs, dark signals and gridlock section (Minnesota Class D written test)
Issuer: the Minnesota Department of Public Safety, whose Driver and Vehicle Services division licenses drivers, permits the printing of the Minnesota Driver’s Manual [A01].
Source document: Minnesota Driver’s Manual, with the edition line June 2026 printed on the front cover [A02].
Written test format: multiple choice and true-or-false questions on Minnesota traffic laws and road signs, taken on paper or on a computer, with English, several other languages and American Sign Language at computer test stations [A03].
Passing standard: a score of 80 percent, and only one written test may be taken per day [A03].
Retest fee: after two consecutive failed written tests, a fee of $10 is charged for the third and every later attempt [A03].
Vision screening: normal or corrected vision of at least 20/40 and peripheral vision of at least 105 degrees [A03].
Question count: the manual does not state how many questions the written test has, so the count stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MN-SIGNSA-A-COUNT).
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MN-SIGNSA-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: the sign color codes, the regulatory signs and their required actions, lane-use and limited-use lane signs, disability parking, warning signs for railroads, schools, pedestrians, slow-moving vehicles, intersections, road shape and merging, construction signs and the work zone fine, service, guide and route signs, the dark signal rule and the intersection gridlock rule.

## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: sign colors and shapes, regulatory, warning, construction and guide signs, dark signals and gridlock
