Misconceptions:
- Plates belong to the owner and can go on any car: false, plates are for one vehicle, though they may transfer to a replacement vehicle when the old one is sold or traded.
- Having insurance is enough even without the card: false, the card must be in the vehicle, shown at stops and tests, and a missing card must be produced within fifteen days.
- A repainted car needs no DMV notice: false, a color change, like a name or address change, must be reported within thirty days.
- Idling in the driveway to warm up is efficient: false, warm ups should be about thirty seconds, driving gently warms the engine best, and unattended idling in public is illegal.
- The purchase tax is always on the price paid: false, it is six percent of the price or fair market value, whichever is greater.

Mnemonics:
- Twenty-five, fifty, ten: one person, two or more, property.
- Sixty to register, thirty to update, fifteen to inspect: the three ownership deadlines in days.
- Six percent of the bigger number: the purchase and use tax.

Glossary:
- Purchase and use tax: the six percent tax paid when a vehicle is bought, based on price or fair market value, whichever is greater
- Lien holder: the lender whose name goes on the registration and who holds the title until the vehicle loan is repaid
- Maintenance of financial responsibility: the Vermont law requiring liability insurance on any vehicle driven
- Rebuilt vehicle title: the title issued in Vermont to a vehicle that previously carried a salvage title
- Manufacturer's Certificate of Origin: the document required to register any new vehicle
