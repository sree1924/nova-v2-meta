## A — Exam authority: Colorado driving knowledge test

Issuer: the Colorado Division of Motor Vehicles administers driver licensing and the written test described in this handbook [A01].
Exam: the Driving Knowledge Test, also called the written test, covers driving knowledge and safety [A01].
Scope of questions: road signs, driving under the influence, driving rules, safety rules and legal items drawn from the whole handbook [A01].
Relevance of this section: hill parking, freeway driving, lane changes and passing falls inside that whole-handbook scope [A01].

FACT_ID: sec09-R05 | anchor: A02

FACT_ID: sec09-R07 | anchor: A02
Passing on the right is allowed on one-way streets and roads with two or more lanes each way, but passing on the shoulder or off the pavement never is. [A02]

Return to your lane after passing when you see both headlights of the passed vehicle in the rearview mirror, and finish before a solid yellow line begins. [A02]

Misconception: you may merge onto the freeway anywhere along the ramp; wait until the solid white line ends. [A01]
Misconception: the left lane is a fast cruising lane; it is reserved for passing, left turns and heavy traffic. [A01]
Misconception: slowing down before reaching the deceleration lane is safest; hold your speed until you are in it. [A02]
Misconception: you may never cross a solid yellow line to pass; a brief crossing is allowed to pass a bicyclist when the way ahead is clear. [A02]
Mnemonic: Up and away, down toward the curb, no curb turn right. [A01]
Mnemonic: Signal, mirror, shoulder, move. [A02]
Glossary: Acceleration lane means the ramp lane used to build speed before merging onto a freeway. [A01]
Glossary: Metered ramp means an entrance ramp with a signal that releases vehicles onto the freeway. [A01]
Glossary: Deceleration lane means the lane used to slow down before reaching a freeway exit ramp. [A02]
Glossary: Blind spot means the area beside and behind your vehicle that your mirrors do not show. [A02]
