Misconceptions: in a blowout you do not brake first, you steer and let the speed fall; off the pavement edge you do not jerk the wheel back; in a skid you steer the way the rear is sliding and stay off the brakes; with a stuck accelerator the key is turned only far enough to stop the engine, never to the lock position; water is never used on a gasoline, oil or electrical fire; a missed freeway exit means continuing to the next one, never stopping or backing; if an oncoming car is in your lane you go right, never into the oncoming lane.

Mnemonics: Blowout: grip, steer, coast, then brake; skid: off the brakes, off the gas, steer where the rear goes; freeway: never cross, never turn around, never back up; fire: stop, switch off, get out, smother small ones, never water.

Glossary: blowout (the sudden collapse of a tire from loss of air); escape route (a side road or open area used to stop a car whose brakes have failed); steering lock (the ignition position that locks the wheel, to be avoided when stopping a runaway engine); skid (loss of tire grip from a sudden change of speed or direction); overcorrecting (steering too far in a skid and starting one the other way); center divider (the barrier between freeway directions that must never be crossed).

Dynamic facts: none; emergency procedures and freeway rules are stable teaching content of this printing.
