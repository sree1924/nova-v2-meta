## A — Exam authority for the license requirements, classes, documents, fees and restrictions section (Missouri Class F operator license written test)
Issuer: the Missouri Department of Revenue publishes the Missouri Driver Guide [A01].
Source document: Missouri Driver Guide, with the front cover reading Revised August 2026 [A02].
Written test: 25 multiple-choice questions, all drawn from information in the guide [A03].
Passing standard: 20 of the 25 questions must be answered correctly, which is 80 percent [A03].
The driver examination has four parts (written, vision, road sign and driving skills) and is given by the Missouri State Highway Patrol; the Class F written test is not open book and leaves out the commercial vehicle chapter [A04].
Time limit: the guide does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MO-LIC-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: who must hold a Missouri license, the permit and license classes with their ages, when the full four-part examination applies, the lawful status, identity, residency and Social Security proofs, the fee chart and the restriction codes.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: license requirements, classes, documents, fees and restrictions
Audience: first-time Missouri applicants for the Class F operator license, including teens entering the Graduated Driver License program and new residents transferring a license, preparing for the written test in English.
Why it matters to a first-timer: the chapter opens the path with the instruction permit at the youngest eligible age [A01] and lists the documents and four-part examination every new applicant faces [A04].
Level: administrative recall of ages, classes, document rules, fees and restriction codes; low conceptual load but many look-alike ages and two parallel document systems (REAL ID-compliant and non-REAL ID).
Prerequisites: none; this is the opening chapter of the guide and sets up the examination and GDL material that follows.
