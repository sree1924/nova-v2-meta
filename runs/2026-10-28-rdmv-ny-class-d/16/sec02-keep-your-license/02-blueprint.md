Suspension takes the license away for a time before return, possibly with a termination fee, while revocation cancels it outright so reapplication, possibly denied, starts over after the revocation term; the same fates apply to out-of-state driving privileges and to unlicensed permission. [A01]

FACT_ID: sec02-F02 | anchor: A01
Junior permits and licenses draw a sixty-day suspension for a serious three-point-plus violation or two lesser ones, a sixty-day revocation for the same pattern within six months of restoration, one hundred twenty days for texting or cell-phone use, and the permit must be held six months before any road test. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=19]

Licensed adults eighteen or older serve six months of probation, where DWAI, speeding, reckless driving, tailgating, speed contests or any two violations trigger sixty days off plus a fresh six-month probation, a repeat round revokes at least six months with probation after restoration, and texting or cell-phone use costs one hundred twenty days. [A01]
