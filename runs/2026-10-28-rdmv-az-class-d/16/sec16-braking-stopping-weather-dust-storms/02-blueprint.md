Misconceptions: in a dust storm you do not keep your lights on once you are parked off the road, you turn them off and take your foot off the brake; the stopping-distance chart is a guide, not a promise; the first minutes of rain are the most slippery, not the safest; a tailwind lengthens your stop rather than shortening it.
Mnemonics: Pull Aside, Stay Alive for dust storms; see it, react, then brake; tailwind means brake early, headwind means more gas and harder steering.
Glossary: perception time means the time to notice and recognize a danger; reaction time means the time from noticing a danger to starting to brake; hydroplaning means tires riding on a film of water and losing grip; jackknife means a trailer swinging sideways against the towing vehicle; crosswind means wind blowing across your path.
