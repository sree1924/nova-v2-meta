FACT_ID: sec05-F039 | anchor: P22

FACT_ID: sec05-F040 | anchor: P22

FACT_ID: sec05-F055 | anchor: P23
Slow to an appropriate speed before entering a curve and reduce speed further for poor traction, following traffic or an unseen curve exit.

FACT_ID: sec05-F056 | anchor: P23
Hard braking after entering a curve can cause tires to lose grip.

FACT_ID: sec05-F057 | anchor: P23
At the first signs of rain, snow, sleet or ice, reduce speed because weaker tire grip makes stopping and turning more difficult.

FACT_ID: sec05-F058 | anchor: P23
Hydroplaning occurs when the steering tires ride on pooled water instead of maintaining road contact.

FACT_ID: sec05-F059 | anchor: P24
Reduce hydroplaning risk by slowing in rain or pooled water and maintaining sufficient tire tread and proper tire pressure.

FACT_ID: sec05-F060 | anchor: P24
Choose another route rather than driving through a flooded road or a large accumulation of standing water.

FACT_ID: sec05-F061 | anchor: P24
On gravel or dirt, slow down, leave more space ahead and expect possible skidding while stopping.

FACT_ID: sec05-F062 | anchor: P24
On a narrow rural road, reduce speed, keep centered in your own travel lane and watch for approaching vehicles encroaching into it.

FACT_ID: sec05-F063 | anchor: P24
Watch narrow-bridge warnings and take turns on a single-lane bridge; generally the first driver to reach it proceeds first.

FACT_ID: sec05-F064 | anchor: P24
Steel bridges or open gratings can weaken traction, calling for lower speed, greater following room and a secure grip on the wheel.

FACT_ID: sec05-F065 | anchor: P24
Where vegetation, hills or blind corners restrict sight, reduce speed and remain prepared to stop.

FACT_ID: sec05-F066 | anchor: P24
Approach an uncontrolled rural intersection cautiously, slowing and preparing to stop for crossing or opposing traffic.

FACT_ID: sec05-F067 | anchor: P24
Night driving is more hazardous because available headlight illumination limits how far the driver can see.

FACT_ID: sec05-F068 | anchor: P24
High beams may be used when there is no opposing traffic, but dim them for approaching vehicles and use low beams behind another vehicle.

FACT_ID: sec05-F069 | anchor: P24
Use low beams in fog, rain or snow because high-beam glare can make forward vision worse.

FACT_ID: sec05-F070 | anchor: P24
If approaching headlights dazzle you, direct your gaze toward the right road edge rather than looking into the lights.

FACT_ID: sec05-F071 | anchor: P24
Scan ahead for dark silhouettes and glance toward both road edges for hazards approaching from the sides.

FACT_ID: sec05-F072 | anchor: P24
Avoid tinted or colored lenses at night or on overcast days because they reduce vision.

FACT_ID: sec05-F073 | anchor: P24
Increase following time by at least one second at night, or by at least two seconds on an unfamiliar road at night.

FACT_ID: sec05-F074 | anchor: P25
Warning technologies commonly provide sound, light or vibration alerts without automatically preventing a collision or fault.

FACT_ID: sec05-F075 | anchor: P25
Treat driver-assistance warnings as added support for mirrors and direct head checks, never as a replacement for active monitoring.

FACT_ID: sec05-F076 | anchor: P25
When backing, monitor side mirrors, the interior mirror, direct over-shoulder views and any fitted camera.

FACT_ID: sec05-F077 | anchor: P25
A reversing camera activates when reverse is selected and displays the area behind the vehicle on the vehicle's screen.

FACT_ID: sec05-F078 | anchor: P25
A backup warning system scans behind the vehicle in reverse and may use sound, vibration or both to indicate an obstruction.

FACT_ID: sec05-F079 | anchor: P25
A blind-spot monitor may assist a lane change, but the driver must still check mirrors and look over the shoulder.

FACT_ID: sec05-F080 | anchor: P25
A curve-speed warning alerts the driver when the approach to a curve or exit is too fast.

FACT_ID: sec05-F081 | anchor: P25
A surround-view monitor combines overhead and nearby views of the vehicle and may add sonar warnings about nearby obstructions.

FACT_ID: sec05-F082 | anchor: P25
Forward-collision warning scans ahead and alerts the driver about a slower or stationary vehicle or object; that warning alone does not apply the brakes.
