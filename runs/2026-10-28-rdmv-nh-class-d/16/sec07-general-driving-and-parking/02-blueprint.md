FACT_ID: sec07-F013 | anchor: P32

FACT_ID: sec07-F014 | anchor: P32

FACT_ID: sec07-F015 | anchor: P32

FACT_ID: sec07-F016 | anchor: P32
After backing into the driveway, stop, select forward drive and check both directions before signaling a left turn into the proper lane.

FACT_ID: sec07-F017 | anchor: P32

FACT_ID: sec07-F018 | anchor: P32
Before reversing out of the driveway, check every direction, signal right and back slowly only when the path is clear; straighten and stop in the nearest lane before moving forward.

FACT_ID: sec07-F019 | anchor: P32
Use a three-point turn only on a two-lane road that is too narrow for a U-turn.

FACT_ID: sec07-F020 | anchor: P32

FACT_ID: sec07-F021 | anchor: P32
After the first movement of a three-point turn, select reverse, check both sides and traffic, and reverse with right steering only when clear.

FACT_ID: sec07-F022 | anchor: P32
Finish a three-point turn by selecting forward drive, signaling left and rechecking traffic and blind spots before entering the right-hand lane in the new direction.

FACT_ID: sec07-F023 | anchor: P32

FACT_ID: sec07-F024 | anchor: P32

FACT_ID: sec07-F026 | anchor: P32

FACT_ID: sec07-F032 | anchor: P33

FACT_ID: sec07-F040 | anchor: P34
An unattended vehicle must have its engine switched off and key removed; the manual also advises locking it.

FACT_ID: sec07-F041 | anchor: P34
Use park for an automatic vehicle or leave a manual vehicle in gear, and apply the parking brake to prevent movement.

FACT_ID: sec07-F042 | anchor: P34
Do not open a parked vehicle's door into passing traffic.

FACT_ID: sec07-F043 | anchor: P34
Check signs and curb markings for restrictions before choosing a parking location.

FACT_ID: sec07-F044 | anchor: P34
Reserved accessible spaces and their adjacent striped access aisles are protected by parking law, with fines for violations.

FACT_ID: sec07-F045 | anchor: P34
An access aisle supplies room for a door, wheelchair, lift or ramp and may require its entire marked width for safe entry or exit.

FACT_ID: sec07-F046 | anchor: P34
A person unable to enter or leave safely because a vehicle blocks an access aisle should immediately contact local police.

FACT_ID: sec07-F047 | anchor: P34
People with walking disabilities or their drivers may report an illegally parked vehicle using a photograph and sworn statement submitted to local police.

FACT_ID: sec07-F048 | anchor: P34
Parking is prohibited beside another parked vehicle, on a sidewalk or crosswalk, inside an intersection, on a bridge or in a highway tunnel.

FACT_ID: sec07-F049 | anchor: P34
Do not park in front of a driveway, in a hazardous position, or where traffic lacks sufficient room to pass.

FACT_ID: sec07-F050 | anchor: P34
Keep parked vehicles at least 15 feet from a fire hydrant and at least 20 feet from an intersection crosswalk.

FACT_ID: sec07-F051 | anchor: P34
Do not park within 30 feet of a stop sign, yield sign or traffic signal.

FACT_ID: sec07-F052 | anchor: P34
The prohibited fire-station approach covers 20 feet from its driveway entrance and 75 feet on the opposite side of the street.

FACT_ID: sec07-F053 | anchor: P34
Do not park within 50 feet of a railroad crossing's nearest rail.

FACT_ID: sec07-F054 | anchor: P35
For perpendicular or angled parking, check traffic, signal, enter slowly and center the vehicle before stopping and securing it.

FACT_ID: sec07-F055 | anchor: P35
When backing out of an angled or perpendicular space, continue checking mirrors and direct views until the front bumper clears the neighboring vehicle before turning the rear toward the intended direction.

FACT_ID: sec07-F056 | anchor: P35
After clearing the parking space, stop, select forward drive and accelerate smoothly while straightening the steering.

FACT_ID: sec07-F057 | anchor: P35
To parallel park, select a suitable space, check traffic, signal and look behind while backing slowly into it.

FACT_ID: sec07-F058 | anchor: P35
During parallel parking, turn to bring the vehicle into the space, stop short of the rear vehicle and use forward drive to adjust its position.

FACT_ID: sec07-F059 | anchor: P35
Before leaving a parallel space, check all directions with mirrors and direct head checks and back sufficiently to create room ahead.

FACT_ID: sec07-F060 | anchor: P35
Signal before moving forward out of a parallel space and ensure the front bumper clears the vehicle ahead, correcting position if necessary.
