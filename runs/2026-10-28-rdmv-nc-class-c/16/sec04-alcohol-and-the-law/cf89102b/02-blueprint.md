Misconceptions: refusing the chemical test does not avoid a penalty, it triggers its own revocation; a driver below the per se limit can still be convicted if impairment is shown; underage drivers face an any-amount rule, not the adult limit; a closed container in a car's passenger area is not the same as an open one, but in a commercial vehicle both are banned.
Mnemonics: two roads to DWI — Impaired or Over the limit; the restoration restriction tightens from a small allowance on the first restoration to none on the next.
Glossary: BAC means blood alcohol concentration; DWI means Driving While Impaired, the single impaired-driving offense; chemical test means a breath or blood test; ignition interlock means a device that blocks starting the vehicle when alcohol is detected; Limited Driving Privilege means court-granted restricted driving during a revocation.
Dynamic facts: DWI penalties can change by legislation and are flagged as dynamic; confirm current law on the General Assembly website.
