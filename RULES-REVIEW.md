# Meta AI Hackathon Rules Review

Checked October 26, 2026, in the signed-in participant browser.

## Participant requirements

[Participant guide](https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/details), sections 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, and 8:

- Meaningful Meta model use; hosted API or Meta openweights.
- Public repository; accessible three-minute video; list contributors; one project per team.
- Building: October 27 to November 4. Submission: November 4, 9 p.m. EST, or 7 p.m. Denver.
- Basic RAG and simple education chatbots are unsuitable examples.
- Scoring: impact 30%, Meta tooling 25%, engineering 20%, demo 25%.
- Meta credits: $150; API calls require a card.

Section 4: “What you present at this Hackathon must have been built during the event.” Also: “Startups may build on existing products or codebases”. Indie framework reuse remains unresolved.

## Other models

Neither reviewed page expressly bans non-Meta LLMs or requires exclusivity. Neither expressly authorizes every use.

For Nova, recommend Meta as the measured generator. Log engineering assistants and independent evaluation models separately; retain deterministic and source-based verification. This separation is our experiment design, not a category defined by the rules.
