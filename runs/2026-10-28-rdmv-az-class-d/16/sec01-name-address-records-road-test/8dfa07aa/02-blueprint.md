## A — Exam authority for the name and address changes, records, legal presence and the road test section (Arizona Class D operator license, written test)
Issuer: the Arizona Department of Transportation Motor Vehicle Division (ADOT MVD) provides this manual as its guide to Arizona traffic laws and to obtaining a driver license or identification card [A05].
Source document: Arizona Driver License Manual and Customer Service Guide, whose contents page carries the line Revised September 2026 [A03].
Purpose: the manual explains Arizona motor vehicle laws without technical language to help readers obtain a driver license or instruction permit, and refers to Arizona Revised Statutes Title 28 for the most current law [A03].
Written test: MVD issues an Arizona instruction permit only after the applicant passes the Division written test; commercial learner permits are excluded from this rule [A01].
Practice: the manual sends applicants to the MVD website to practice the written test [A02].
Road test link: before a road test, applicants must show they understand the meaning of Arizona traffic signs [A04].

## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: name and address changes, records, legal presence and the road test
Audience: first-time Arizona applicants for a Class D operator license or a graduated license, plus new residents moving a license from another state, preparing in English.
Why it matters to a first-timer: the change-reporting deadline applies from the day a license is issued [A01], and the road test cannot start until the applicant shows sign knowledge and follows spoken English directions in a vehicle they supply [A03].
Level: administrative recall of deadlines, documents and road-test requirements; low conceptual load but several look-alike rules about what is done online and what is done in person.
Prerequisites: none; these pages come before the license classes and the driving chapters.
