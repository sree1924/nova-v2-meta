# Accuracy and teaching notes

All BAC thresholds and legal consequences retain current issuer/legal review debt; thresholds do not define a safe alcohol dose or guarantee lawful driving below them. The source gives fixed hourly alcohol removal and one-drink-per-hour waiting advice, a fixed cannabis effect duration and claims visible impairment after every drink; these are not adopted as biological guarantees or driving-clearance formulas. The felony-record voting prohibition is an overbroad civic-rights claim and is withheld. Memory cue, authored product policy: “No shortcut to sober; stop, identify, help; suspension requires reinstatement”. The cue retrieves complete cited rules and does not replace their conditions.
Terminology: BAC is blood alcohol concentration. Implied consent describes the source’s chemical-testing framework; suspension removes the permission to drive until restoration conditions are met.
Misconception to correct: Coffee or a shower removes alcohol impairment, or a legal numerical threshold promises that a person can safely drive. The source rejects sobering tricks and warns that impairment can begin with one drink.
