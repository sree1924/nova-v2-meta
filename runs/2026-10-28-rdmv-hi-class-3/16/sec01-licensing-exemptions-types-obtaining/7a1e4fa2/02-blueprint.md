Mnemonics: Fifteen and a half to learn, sixteen to go provisional, seventeen for class three; one hundred eighty permit days before the provisional step and six provisional months before class three; the supervising adult is twenty-one or older and sits up front on the right.

Glossary: instruction permit (supervised-driving authorization for a beginner); provisional license (the restricted intermediate license for drivers under eighteen); class three license (the ordinary non-commercial car license); limited purpose license (a driving-only license for applicants without a social security number); REAL ID (the federally acceptable identification standard); implement of husbandry (farm equipment moved on the highway).
