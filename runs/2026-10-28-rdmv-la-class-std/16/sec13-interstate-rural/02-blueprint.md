# Audience and boundaries

This section supports first-time Louisiana passenger-vehicle applicants studying interstate driving, rural roads and trip planning. The study scope follows the registered Class E route; the guide also contains other vehicle classes, which are outside this product scope. Memory cue, authored product policy: “Plan the exit, match the gap, keep space; rural does not mean risk-free”. The cue retrieves complete cited rules and does not replace their conditions.
Terminology: A deceleration lane provides room to slow after leaving through traffic. Open, closed and changing zones describe the availability and visibility of travel space.
Misconception to correct: Mainline drivers must make room for a merging car, or a missed exit justifies reversing. Entering traffic yields, and a missed exit requires continuing forward to a later interchange.
