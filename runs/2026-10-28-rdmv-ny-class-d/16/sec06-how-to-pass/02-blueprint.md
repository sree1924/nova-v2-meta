FACT_ID: sec06-F05 | anchor: A01
Left passes die on solid yellow center lines, unreturnable gaps before a far solid line, oncoming traffic inside two hundred feet, blind curves and hillcrests on two-way roads, within one hundred feet of two-way railroad crossings, within one hundred feet of obstructed two-way bridges, tunnels and viaducts, and whenever oncoming traffic would suffer interference. [A01]

FACT_ID: sec06-F06 | anchor: A01

Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=39]

FACT_ID: sec06-F07 | anchor: A01
Right passes are allowed only past a left-turning vehicle ahead, on two-way roads marked or wide enough for two-plus lanes each way without prohibitions or obstructions, and never across shoulders or edge lines except by sign or officer. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=39]

FACT_ID: sec06-F08 | anchor: A02
