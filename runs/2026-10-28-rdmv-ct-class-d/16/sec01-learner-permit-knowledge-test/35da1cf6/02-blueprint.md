## A — Exam authority for the Connecticut Class D knowledge test

Issuer: the Connecticut Department of Motor Vehicles issues driver licenses and publishes the Connecticut Driver’s Manual [A03].
Edition: the imprint page prints the edition line “Revised March 2023” [A03]; no effective date is printed, so the effective date stays unconfirmed (verify — sec01-A-EFFECTIVE-DATE).
Exam path: a first time applicant passes a vision test and a 25-question knowledge test to earn a learner permit [A07], then a road test for the license [A07].
Scoring: the knowledge test spans 25 questions with 20 correct answers needed to pass [A07]; the manual states no time limit, which stays unknown (verify — sec01-A-TIME-LIMIT).
Question format: beyond samples on the DMV mobile app, the manual does not describe the item format of the knowledge test (verify — sec01-A-FORMAT).
Section link: this dossier section covers the learner permit, the Class D license, teen driving rules, first time driver requirements, and the knowledge and vision tests, all drawn from the manual the test is built on [A07].
Practice mastery targets in this product are product policy from the accepted learning loop, never an official passing line.
Dynamic facts (updatable): test fees, office locations, appointments and online services are set by DMV and can change between editions, so they are kept out of stable knowledge [A07]; why_dynamic: administrative details outside the printed rules of the road.
## B — Audience and scope

Audience: first-time Connecticut applicants who have never held a license and are preparing in English for the Class D knowledge test; this section matters most to sixteen- and seventeen-year-olds starting the learner path, to their parents and guardians, and to adults of any age who must take the Safe Driving Practices course.
Level: plain-English reading level; no prior driving vocabulary is assumed, so each manual term is defined where it first appears.
Prerequisites: none; this is the opening chapter of the manual and assumes no earlier reading.
Section scope: manual chapter one, first part: the Class D license, companion manuals, the DMV website, teen driving guidance, the learner permit, the eight-hour Safe Driving Practices course, health and fitness standards, the knowledge and vision tests, permit validity, the qualified trainer rule, teen passenger restrictions, and driver education paths for sixteen- and seventeen-year-olds.
Out of scope: commercial and motorcycle testing content, road test maneuvers, fees, office logistics, and anything not printed on the selected manual pages.
