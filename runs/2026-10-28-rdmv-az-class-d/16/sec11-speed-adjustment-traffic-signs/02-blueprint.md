Misconceptions: an orange speed sign is advice for a curve or grade, while a white sign is the legal maximum; the school-zone speed applies only while the portable signs are out, otherwise the normal limit applies; the rural freeway limit and the designated open highway limit are different numbers; driving well below the flow of traffic in the left lane is not the safe choice, slower traffic keeps right.
Mnemonics: Octagon stops, triangle yields, pennant says no passing, pentagon means school, circle means railroad ahead.
Glossary: regulatory sign means a sign that states a law you must obey; advisory speed means a recommended safe speed on an orange or small yellow sign; pennant means the sideways triangular no-passing sign; on-track equipment means rail vehicles other than trains that use the tracks.
Dynamic facts: unposted speed limits are set by statute and can be amended, and which Arizona school districts use yellow-green school signs is a local choice that keeps changing.
