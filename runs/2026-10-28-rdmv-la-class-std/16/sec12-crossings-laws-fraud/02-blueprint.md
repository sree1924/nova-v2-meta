# Knowledge-test information

The handbook requires a correct-answer rate of at least eighty percent to pass the knowledge examination. [A01]
The guide describes computer testing in most offices and questions based on its traffic rules, signs, signals and safe-driving material. [A01]
An exact question count and a test duration are not stated in the evidence used here. Those values remain unknown. This preparation uses practice questions; it does not claim an official question bank, a complete examination form or a guaranteed result. First-time applicants should retain the full conditions attached to each rule rather than treat a short study cue as a replacement for the guide.
# Audience and boundaries

This section supports first-time Louisiana passenger-vehicle applicants studying rail crossings, other laws and vehicle crime prevention. The study scope follows the registered Class E route; the guide also contains other vehicle classes, which are outside this product scope. Learning activities ask the learner to distinguish the person, document or situation covered by a stated rule. Driver licensing decisions, document adjudication and practical driving instruction remain outside these written-knowledge exercises.

No fixed train stopping distance is guaranteed. Current open-bed, headset, funeral, insurance and licensing provisions retain legal/currentness debt. Telephone and program URLs are contact information, not driving-rule recall targets. Dated rail-crash totals remain unasserted. [A01] [A02] [A03] [A04] [A05] [A06]
Memory cue, authored product policy: “Stop clear, cross clear, stay off the tracks; document and protect”. The cue retrieves complete cited rules and does not replace their conditions.
Terminology: A crossbuck marks a railroad crossing; an emergency-notification sign identifies that crossing for the railroad. A fictional theft is a knowingly false stolen-vehicle claim.
Misconception to correct: A raised gate or silent warning system guarantees that no train is coming, or a gap across the rails guarantees room for the whole vehicle. Check both ways and ensure the far side is clear.
