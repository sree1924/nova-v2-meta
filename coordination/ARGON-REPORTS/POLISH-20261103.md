3. **Root cause of Fable's 2-click partial-quiz bug (`P2 #5`) found & eliminated:**
   - Previously (`store/learner-assets/gate.js` `L718`, `L1261–1267`), clicking `Check answers` on an incomplete quiz jumped `current = firstMissing` (e.g. `Q1`), where `updateQuizUI()` immediately hid `[data-quiz-submit-bar]` whenever `current !== pages.length - 1 && ansCount !== pages.length` — stranding the learner without a submit button on a partial set.
   - Replaced with a 1-click inline confirmation box (`[data-partial-confirm]` inside `[data-quiz-submit-bar]`): first click on `Check answers` keeps the learner in place, scrolls the confirm card into view, and exposes `Grade n of m now` (`type="submit"`) + `Keep answering`. Clicking `Grade n of m now` grades immediately in one click and renders the summary card with direct link to `#/<st>/results/<attemptId>` (`after-nd-quiz-confirm-{375,1440}.png`, `after-nd-quiz-graded-{375,1440}.png`, `after-nd-results-{375,1440}.png`).
4. **Two visual defects caught during `375×812` & `1440×900` pixel inspection and fixed before final re-stage:**
   - **Mobile `<h1>` visually clipped (`375px`):** Legacy mobile CSS (`store/learner-assets/learner.css` `L2997–3007`) clipped `.plp-quest-frame h1` to `1×1px` (`clip: rect(0,0,0,0)`), hiding the study title at `375px`. Overrode `.plp-quest-frame.is-study-header h1` at `L6695–6706` (`position: static; font-size: 17.5px`) so all three required study header elements (`Chapter 1 · Licensing and testing`, `<h1>` title, and `Segment 1 of 6` / `Card 1 of 16` / `Question 1 of 16` pill) render visibly on `375px` while keeping video/card/question + primary action on Screen 1.
   - **Storefront square PNG sign aspect containment:** Added explicit `width: auto; height: auto; max-width: 100%; max-height: 102px; object-fit: contain` on `.neo-visual-img-box.is-sign img` (`store/src/app/globals.css` `L1027–1033`) so `1024×1024` `signs/*.png` diagrams scale cleanly inside the `118px` hero tile without CSS Grid implicit-height overflow. *(Note: `sign-wrong-way.png` itself places the red `WRONG WAY` plaque in the lower portion of its read-only `1024×1024` MUTCD canvas; the PNG file is untouched).*
