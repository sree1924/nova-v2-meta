Mnemonics: Downhill toward, uphill away, no curb to the right; in a roundabout, yield in and keep moving.
Glossary: roundabout means a circular intersection with one-way traffic around a central island; diverging diamond interchange means a freeway interchange where cross-street traffic briefly drives on the left side; crossover means the signalized point where traffic switches sides; international symbol of access means the wheelchair symbol marking accessible parking.
