Misconceptions: a dark signal is not a green light, it is a four-way stop; a flashing red at a railroad crossing is not cleared just because the train seems far away; a flagger's signal is not a suggestion, it carries the force of a regulatory sign; pavement lines are not advice, they have the force of law.
Mnemonics: red on top or on the left; eight sides for STOP, three for YIELD and the no-passing pennant, five for school; yellow lines split opposite directions, white lines split same-direction lanes; dashed means maybe, solid means never.
Glossary: regulatory sign means a sign that sets speed or movement rules; warning sign means a sign that alerts to a hazard ahead; guidance sign means a sign that identifies routes and destinations; crossbuck means the X-shaped railroad crossing sign; advisory speed sign means a posted safe speed for a curve below the limit; flagger means a work zone worker who directs traffic.
Dynamic facts: national truck route signage and work zone fine rules can change; confirm them on official New Jersey websites.
