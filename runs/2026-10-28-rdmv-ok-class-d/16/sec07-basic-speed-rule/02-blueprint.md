## C — The Basic Speed Rule

FACT_ID: sec07-F01 | anchor: A01
The Basic Speed Rule sets no exact cap but limits speed to what stays safe for current conditions with stopping short of anyone, so a posted sixty-five can still run too fast on a rainy foggy night two-lane [A01].

FACT_ID: sec07-F02 | anchor: A01
Speed must stay careful and prudent with skill and judgment plus reasonable and proper neither too fast nor too slow for traffic amount and pace, pedestrian presence especially schoolchildren, road surface, width, and structure, visibility, weather and air, and your own ability [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0040]

FACT_ID: sec07-F03 | anchor: A01
Never crawl so slowly as to block, hinder, or interfere with normal-speed traffic, and always fit speed to conditions so you halt within clear distance ahead, since rear-ending a suddenly stopped leader breaks the rule or proves tailgating even under the posted cap [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0040]

FACT_ID: sec07-F04 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec07-F09 | anchor: A02
