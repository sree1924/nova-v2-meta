FACT_ID: sec06-F15 | anchor: A02

FACT_ID: sec06-F16 | anchor: A02
Illegal equipment includes red or blue flashing lights, which only authorized emergency vehicles may carry, sirens, bells or whistles, a cutout or loud-sounding muffler, signs, posters or stickers on the windshield or windows unless required or permitted by law, and more than one spotlight [A02].

It is also illegal to have more than two side cowl or fender lights, more than two backup lights, backup lights that show light while the vehicle moves forward, or any load extending beyond the fender line on the left side or more than six inches beyond the fender line on the right side [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0035]
