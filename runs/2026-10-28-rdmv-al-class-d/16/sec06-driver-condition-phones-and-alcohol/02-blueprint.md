## A — Exam authority for the Alabama driver license knowledge test

Issuer: the Alabama Law Enforcement Agency, Driver License Division, issues Alabama driver licenses [A07] and publishes the Alabama Driver Manual.
Edition: the front cover of the manual prints the edition line "November 2024" under the Driver License Division name [A01]; no effective date is printed, so the effective date stays unconfirmed (verify — sec06-A-EFFECTIVE-DATE).
Exam path: the manual describes a vision screening, a knowledge test with questions on Alabama traffic laws, road signs and safe-driving rules taken from this manual, and a road test given by a driver license examiner [A13].
Learner path: a fifteen-year-old who passes the required exam receives a Class D learner license with the Y restriction [A11].
Delivery: automated testing is available to every applicant taking the knowledge test [A13], and oral and foreign-language versions exist [A11].
Scoring: the manual states no question count, no passing score or percent, and no time limit for the knowledge test; all three stay unknown and no number is asserted here (verify — sec06-A-SCORING).
Question format: beyond automated delivery, the manual does not describe the item format of the knowledge test (verify — sec06-A-FORMAT).
Section link: this dossier section covers how emotions, attitude and concentration affect driving, cell phone precautions and the texting ban, drowsiness and highway hypnosis, and drinking and driving with Alabama's blood alcohol limits, DUI penalties and implied consent law, all drawn from the manual the test is built on [A13].

## B — Audience and scope

Audience: first-time Alabama applicants who have never held a license and are preparing in English for the driver license knowledge test; this section matters most to teen drivers facing the under-twenty-one alcohol limit and the texting ban, and to every new driver learning how fatigue, emotion and alcohol cause crashes.
Level: plain-English reading level; no prior driving vocabulary is assumed, so each term from the manual is defined where it first appears.
Prerequisites: the point system of the earlier chapter on losing your license, which explains the driving-record points that texting violations add.
Section scope: manual chapter four, first part: driver error as the leading crash cause, how personality and emotions affect driving, concentration, cellular phone precautions, the texting-while-driving ban with its fines and points, highway hypnosis and fatigue, the medical aspects of driving, the relationship of alcohol to crashes, the effects of alcohol, driving under the influence with the general and special blood alcohol limits, the DUI conviction penalties, the implied consent law and the facts to remember about alcohol.
Out of scope: commercial, motorcycle-manual and vessel testing content, office logistics and fees, and anything not printed on the selected manual pages.
