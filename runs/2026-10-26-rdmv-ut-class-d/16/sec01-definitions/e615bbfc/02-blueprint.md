FACT_ID: sec01-F06 | anchor: A81

FACT_ID: sec01-F07 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec01-F08 | anchor: A01

A commercial license covers vehicles at or above the stated heavy weight threshold, vehicles built for the stated larger passenger count, plus hazardous materials, doubles or triples, tankers and school buses [A01].

FACT_ID: sec01-F09 | anchor: A02

Why it matters: speed plus occupancy plus exclusions.

FACT_ID: sec01-F10 | anchor: A82

FACT_ID: sec01-F12 | anchor: A03

Why it matters: age-gated credential type.

FACT_ID: sec01-F13 | anchor: A03

FACT_ID: sec01-F14 | anchor: A83

Division means the Driver License Division of the Department of Public Safety, operator means a person in actual physical control, and highway means the full width between property lines available for vehicular travel [A83].
Why it matters: foundational authority, actor and roadway terms.

Misconceptions: cancellation equals suspension; revocation equals temporary loss; any alcohol restriction equals interlock requirement; roundabout flow is clockwise; provisional status ends at a different birthday.

Mnemonics: C-R-S-D ladder for Cancellation, Revocation, Suspension, Denial by severity and cause; ARD means no alcohol while IRD means interlock required.

Glossary: ARD; IRD; IID; CDL; DPC; provisional license; temporary license; blind spots; head checks; traffic check; roundabout; highway; operator; Division.
