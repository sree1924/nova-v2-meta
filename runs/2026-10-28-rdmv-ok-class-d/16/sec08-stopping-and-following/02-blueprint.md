FACT_ID: sec08-F01 | anchor: A01
No exact stop time exists since distance hinges on reaction time, weather and road, vehicle weight, and brake condition, and every stop chains perception of about half a second seeing or hearing danger, reaction of about two-thirds of a second from brain to foot, and speed-dependent braking to a halt [A01].

FACT_ID: sec08-F02 | anchor: A01
Time the gap by counting seconds from the leader rear passing a fixed point to your arrival, judging too close when the point arrives before a four-count finishes, and stretch gaps on slick roads, behind large vehicles and motorcycles, and in night, fog, and bad weather [A01].

FACT_ID: sec08-F03 | anchor: A02

FACT_ID: sec08-F04 | anchor: A02

FACT_ID: sec08-F05 | anchor: A02

FACT_ID: sec08-F06 | anchor: A02
Icy caution starts with not driving at all, prevention beats cure since skids defy rescue, gentle brake tests sample traction that shifts with traffic, sun, and shade, invisible black ice ambushes bridges and shady spots even on clear roads, sudden braking and clutch use worsen slides, and sliding cars want feet off brakes with steering into the skid unless that path runs off-road, into objects, or against oncoming traffic [A02].

FACT_ID: sec08-F07 | anchor: A03
First rain floats grease and dirt into a slick film demanding slow extra-cautious slippery-when-wet driving, wet-road hydroplaning floats tires off pavement to sap traction, stretch stops, and imperil turns even for wide tires at reduced speed, heavy downpours warrant slowing or pulling off, and water crossing the roadway must never be entered since depth and current defy judgment [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0044]

FACT_ID: sec08-F08 | anchor: A03
Keep a cushioned envelope on every side against sudden leader stops with speed, traffic, road, and weather shaping stop ability, allow a car length per ten miles per hour or count a three-second fixed-object gap rising to four in bad weather and poor roads, shun packs, and space caravan vehicles two hundred feet apart outside business and residential zones so others can merge in [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0044]

FACT_ID: sec08-F09 | anchor: A03

FACT_ID: sec08-F10 | anchor: A04
Rear-enders yield to strict following attention, brake-light watches four or five cars deep with early foot-to-brake shifts, shrinking-gap alarms, fixed-object checks on distant stopped cars, anticipation of leader troubles, generous cushions, and slowing for leader left-turn signals [A04].
