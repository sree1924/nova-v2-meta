Mnemonic: signal every shift, a hundred slow roads, three hundred fast; pass one, pass legal, pass back gentle.

Glossary: three-point turn (a tight reversal using forward and reverse legs); U-turn (a reversal turning back along the same road); blind spot (an area mirrors cannot show); no turn on red (a sign banning red-light turns).
