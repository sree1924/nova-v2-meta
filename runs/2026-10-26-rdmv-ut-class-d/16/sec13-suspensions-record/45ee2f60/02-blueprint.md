## C — Suspensions and your record (Section 13)

FACT_ID: sec13-F01 | anchor: A01
Showing a license you know is canceled, suspended, or altered is unlawful, and altering a license can bring suspension [A01].
Why it matters: separates fraud behavior that triggers driver-control action.

FACT_ID: sec13-F02 | anchor: A01
The division keeps a computerized driving and crash history for every state driver, posting moving-violation convictions and suspensions from the prior three years [A01].
Why it matters: sets the lookback window for ordinary violations.
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0080]

FACT_ID: sec13-F03 | anchor: A01
Alcohol and drug linked violations, suspensions, and revocations stay on the driving record for ten years [A01].
Why it matters: contrasts the longer retention for substance-related entries.
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0080]

FACT_ID: sec13-F04 | anchor: A01
A commercial driver record goes to the commercial license information system, with suspensions and revocations sent on to the national driver registry [A01].
Why it matters: shows wider reporting for commercial privilege.
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0080]

FACT_ID: sec13-F05 | anchor: A01
Operating while denied, suspended, or revoked can draw jail of ninety days plus a fine [A01].
Why it matters: core penalty for driving without restored privilege.
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0080]

FACT_ID: sec13-F06 | anchor: A01
The original denial, suspension, or revocation period grows by that same original length, so a three-month suspension gains another three months [A01].
Why it matters: teaches the doubling rule with its worked illustration.
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0080]

FACT_ID: sec13-F07 | anchor: A01
When the underlying suspension or revocation is alcohol related, the fine can reach one thousand dollars and custody can reach six months [A01].
Why it matters: higher ceiling tied to alcohol basis.
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0080]

FACT_ID: sec13-F08 | anchor: A80
A suspension for mandatory grounds can run as long as two years or until age twenty-one [A80].
Why it matters: frames the maximum duration for young and repeat-risk drivers.
Source: [A80 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0080]

FACT_ID: sec13-F09 | anchor: A02
Suspension grounds include a mandatory-suspension conviction, a crash from reckless or unlawful conduct with injury, death, or serious property harm, license misuse, point-system buildup, DUI or drug findings, and court nonappearance or unpaid obligations [A02].
Why it matters: groups the conduct and compliance triggers together.
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0081]

FACT_ID: sec13-F10 | anchor: A02
Further suspension grounds include missing no-fault insurance or financial responsibility, unpaid child support, a court-ordered texting suspension, underage alcohol proof misuse, custodial interference, an uncompleted emergency-vehicle course, and a first road-rage citation carrying a one-year court suspension [A02].
Why it matters: captures insurance, support, classroom, and road-rage triggers.
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0081]

FACT_ID: sec13-F11 | anchor: A81
Revocation follows findings such as manslaughter or auto homicide, a repeat intoxicated-control offense, a false oath on application, felony use of a vehicle, failing to aid after a fatal or injury crash, fleeing police, firing or releasing weapons or incendiary items from a vehicle, restricted-status alcohol or interlock breaches, ordered careless-driving revocation, auto homicide with a handheld device, test refusal, and two road-rage citations in a year [A81][S81_0].
Why it matters: distinguishes the most serious revocation set from suspensions.
Source: [A81 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0081]

FACT_ID: sec13-F12 | anchor: A03
Point values assign eighty to reckless driving, thirty-five to seventy-five for speeding by severity, sixty to yield, tailgating, wrong side, and wrong way, fifty to red light, stop sign, lookout, passing, and texting, and forty to other moving violations [A03].
Why it matters: enables adding a driver history to a total.
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0082]

FACT_ID: sec13-F13 | anchor: A04
Half the points leave after one conviction-free year, all points leave after two straight conviction-free years, each conviction drops at three years, and a division-recommended defensive course erases fifty points once in any three-year span [A04].
Why it matters: gives every clearing path and its timing limit.
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ut-class-d:utah-dld:handbook:pdf-0083]

High-yield: point math first, denied-driving penalties next, revocation list next, retention and clearing last.
Misconceptions: suspension is not revocation; alcohol entries persist longer; the extension matches the original term; the classroom course is distinct from court traffic school.
Mnemonics: ninety-day no-privilege jail; two hundred over twenty-one and seventy under twenty-one; half after one, all after two, each at three.
Glossary: suspension is temporary loss; revocation ends privilege with reapplication; denial withholds privilege; MVR is the computerized history; point system tracks violations.
