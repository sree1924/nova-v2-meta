## A — Exam authority for the getting the license and probationary rules section (Indiana learner's permit and driver's license knowledge exam)
Issuer: the Indiana Bureau of Motor Vehicles (BMV) publishes the Indiana Driver's Manual; both names appear on the front cover [A02].
Source document: the front cover prints no edition; the back cover reads Published July 2026 and points readers to IN.gov/BMV for updates [A04].
Knowledge exam: a multiple-choice, computer-based exam drawn from Indiana law, defensive driving practices and the manual, which also tests reading of regulatory, warning and traffic signs and signals [A01].
Exam structure: the exam has two components, one on common signs (Chapter 6) and one on traffic rules and laws (Chapters 6, 7 and 8) [A03].
Passing standard: 80 percent or higher correct on each component [A03].
Branch logistics: arrive at least 30 minutes before the branch closes to test that day; after a failure, wait until the next business day to retake [A01].
Question count: the manual gives no number of questions for either component or in total, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify IN-LICENSE-A-COUNT).
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown (verify IN-LICENSE-A-TIME).
This section feeds the knowledge exam: this section finishes the path to a license: how the driving skills exam is scored and failed, financial liability for minors, the age, holding-period and practice-log rules, license validity, the probationary passenger, hour and phone limits, and the seat belt and texting laws that apply to every driver.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: getting the license and probationary rules
Audience: Indiana permit holders preparing to apply for a first license, especially teens under eighteen and the parents or guardians who sign for financial liability and practice logs.
Why it matters to a first-timer: this section sets the age, holding-period and practice-hour requirements for a license [A03] and the passenger, hour and phone limits on a new probationary driver [A04].
Level: dense numeric recall (ages, day counts, hour totals, curfew times, validity periods) plus two long failure lists; moderate memorization load.
Prerequisites: the first part of this chapter (credential types, learner's permit eligibility, supervised practice and the skills exam basics).
