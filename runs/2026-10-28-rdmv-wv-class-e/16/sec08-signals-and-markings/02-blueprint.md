## C — Signals and markings

FACT_ID: sec08-F01 | anchor: A01
Railroad crossing signs may be round or diamond-shaped and yellow with black symbols, or black letters on white bars in the shape of an X called a crossbuck, and a sign below the crossbuck gives the number of tracks when there are two or more [A01].

FACT_ID: sec08-F02 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec08-F03 | anchor: A01
Where there are two or more tracks you must be alert for a second train on the other set of tracks, and you should not enter a crossing until the vehicle ahead has moved far enough that you can clear the tracks, or you may be trapped and hit by another vehicle or a train [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0049]

FACT_ID: sec08-F04 | anchor: A01
A driver facing a circular green signal may enter the intersection and go straight, turn right or turn left unless signs or pavement markings prohibit a movement, every turn follows the right-of-way rules, and a left turn on circular green must yield to oncoming vehicles [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0049]

FACT_ID: sec08-F05 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec08-F06 | anchor: A01
A steady circular yellow means the green is ending and will change to red, you should drive through a yellow only if your vehicle is already within the intersection clearance, and a yellow arrow means the green arrow is ending and will change to red [A01].

FACT_ID: sec08-F07 | anchor: A01
Traffic facing a steady circular red must stop unless making a movement permitted by a green arrow, and must remain stopped until another indication allows it to proceed, the only exception being a permitted turn on red [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0049]

FACT_ID: sec08-F08 | anchor: A01
A driver may turn right on red at the intersection of two-way streets, or turn left on red from a one-way street onto another one-way street, but only after yielding to all other traffic and pedestrians and only where no sign prohibits the turn [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-wv-class-e:west-virginia-division-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0049]

FACT_ID: sec08-F09 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec08-F11 | anchor: A02
