## C2 — Licensing and examination knowledge
FACT_ID: sec11-F029 | anchor: P63
Do not race a train to a crossing.

FACT_ID: sec11-F030 | anchor: P63
In a manual-transmission vehicle, select a lower gear before the tracks and avoid shifting while crossing.

FACT_ID: sec11-F031 | anchor: P63
Be ready for buses or trucks ahead that are required to stop at railroad crossings.

FACT_ID: sec11-F032 | anchor: P63
Pass a vehicle near a railroad crossing only where legal and when the tracks are clearly visible and conditions are safe.

FACT_ID: sec11-F033 | anchor: P63
Stop when a train is close to a crossing, including an unmarked crossing; the handbook says to prepare to stop with a train within 1,500 feet.

FACT_ID: sec11-F034 | anchor: P64
If a vehicle is stuck on the tracks and warning lights begin flashing, immediately get everyone out.

FACT_ID: sec11-F035 | anchor: P64
After leaving a vehicle trapped on the tracks, run away from the vehicle and tracks toward the direction from which the train is approaching, then call nine-one-one once clear.

FACT_ID: sec11-F036 | anchor: P64
The handbook warns that a train may reach a crossing only 20 seconds after warning lights activate, so do not delay escape.

FACT_ID: sec11-F037 | anchor: P64
If a vehicle is stuck on tracks before warnings activate or where no warning lights exist, get out and immediately call nine-one-one and the crossing's emergency-notification number.

FACT_ID: sec11-F038 | anchor: P64
When reporting a vehicle on railroad tracks, give the location, crossing identifier if posted and intersecting road, and explicitly state that a vehicle blocks the tracks.

FACT_ID: sec11-F039 | anchor: P64
A railroad emergency-notification sign is usually blue and appears on the crossbuck or near the crossing.

FACT_ID: sec11-F040 | anchor: P64
The crossing's emergency-notification telephone connects to railroad dispatchers who can try to stop trains during an emergency.

FACT_ID: sec11-F041 | anchor: P64
The transportation-department number below the railroad dispatch telephone identifies the specific crossing.

FACT_ID: sec11-F042 | anchor: P64
Use the railroad emergency-notification system to report malfunctioning signals or gates, disabled vehicles blocking tracks, trespassers or other dangerous crossing conditions.

FACT_ID: sec11-F043 | anchor: P65
The blue crossing sign's emergency dispatch number and crossing-location identifier help emergency crews and railroad staff respond to the correct site.
