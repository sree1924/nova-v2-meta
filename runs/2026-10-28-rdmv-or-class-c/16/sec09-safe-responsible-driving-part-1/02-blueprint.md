## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: defensive driving, restraints, collision avoidance, lights, night, weather, skids and traction devices

Misconceptions: braking hard after running off the road is the wrong reaction, you slow gently first; swerving left into the other lane to avoid a drifting car is never the escape; high beams make fog worse, not better; parking lights alone are not legal driving lights at night; a light summer shower is not harmless, since rain mixed with oil and dirt makes the road slippery; cruise control is out on wet or icy roads.
Mnemonics: stop, turn or speed up are the three collision escapes; right, slow, warn for an oncoming drifter; low beams in fog, wipers on means lights on; slow, straight, gentle steer to climb back onto the road.
Glossary: defensive driving means adjusting your driving for conditions and other road users so you always have an escape route; hydroplaning means tires skimming on water so steering and braking fail; auxiliary lights means extra lamps such as fog lights; fog line means the painted line at the right edge of the road; child safety seat means an approved restraint used until a child is big enough for the adult belt.
Dynamic facts: headlight hours follow sunset and sunrise, which shift through the year, and the studded tire season and chain requirements in part C2 are seasonal and posted on the state road information site.
