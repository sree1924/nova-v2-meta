## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: defensive driving and road rage
Audience: every first-time Tennessee Class D or learner permit applicant; these habits apply on every trip after the test as well.
Why it matters to a first-timer: following distance and scanning [A03], the safety cushion and passing rules [A04] and road rage avoidance [A05] are the everyday habits that prevent the most common crashes.
Level: concept and habit recall with a few exact values such as following seconds, scanning distance, mirror checks, merging gaps and passing time; moderate load.
Prerequisites: the rules of the road chapters on following distance, passing and lane use, and the interstate chapter on merging.
