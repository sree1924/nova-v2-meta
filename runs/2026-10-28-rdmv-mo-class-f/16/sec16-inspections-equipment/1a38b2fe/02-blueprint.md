## A — Exam authority for the safety and emissions inspections and required equipment section (Missouri Class F operator license written test)
Issuer: the Missouri Department of Revenue publishes the Missouri Driver Guide [A01].
Source document: Missouri Driver Guide, with the front cover reading Revised August 2026 [A02].
Written test: 25 multiple-choice questions, all drawn from information in the guide [A03].
Passing standard: 20 of the 25 questions must be answered correctly, which is 80 percent [A03].
The driver examination has four parts (written, vision, road sign and driving skills) and is given by the Missouri State Highway Patrol; the Class F written test is not open book and leaves out the commercial vehicle chapter [A04].
Time limit: the guide does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MO-INSP-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: the two-year safety inspection schedule, fee caps and certificate windows, safety inspection exemptions, the equipment an inspection checks, plates, plate lights, projections, towlines, the slow moving vehicle sign, studded tires, window tint, and the emissions program areas, schedule and exemptions.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: safety and emissions inspections and required equipment
Audience: first-time Missouri Class F applicants, from teens on an instruction permit to adult newcomers who will register and maintain a vehicle.
