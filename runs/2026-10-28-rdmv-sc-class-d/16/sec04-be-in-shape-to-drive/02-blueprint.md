## A — Exam authority for the fitness to drive: vision, fatigue, distraction, alcohol and other drugs section (South Carolina Class D knowledge test)
Issuer: the South Carolina Department of Motor Vehicles (SCDMV) publishes the South Carolina Driver's Manual; the front cover names the agency and the manual [A01].
Source document: the front cover carries the edition line Revised 06/2024 [A01].
Required tests: an original license or an upgrade to a different class needs vision, knowledge and skills tests, and vision and knowledge tests can be taken at any SCDMV branch [A02].
Every first license test includes vision, knowledge and skills tests except the moped license, which has no skills test [A03].
Knowledge test content: traffic laws, road signs and driving safety rules [A04].
Test conduct: manuals, testing aids, written material and electronic devices are banned, and leaving the test area early or talking to anyone fails the test [A02].
Question count, passing score and time limit: the manual states none of them, so all three stay unknown and are never shown to learners (verify SC-FIT-A-COUNT, SC-FIT-A-PASS, SC-FIT-A-TIME).
Edition freshness: the issuer page links this Revised 06/2024 file, but a possibly newer unlinked upload exists on the issuer site and was not compared (verify SC-FIT-A-FRESH).
This section feeds the knowledge test: this section is a core knowledge-test source: the vision standard and restriction, the hands-free phone law, aggressive driving, the blood alcohol thresholds for adults and drivers under twenty-one, implied consent and refusal penalties, and drug effects.

## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: fitness to drive: vision, fatigue, distraction, alcohol and other drugs
Audience: every first-time South Carolina Class D applicant, with the under-twenty-one alcohol rules aimed at teen and young adult drivers.
Why it matters to a first-timer: the vision screening a new applicant must pass and the restriction that follows if lenses are needed are set here [A02], and so are the blood alcohol limits and penalties a new driver must know [A06].
Level: mixed recall and judgment; a few exact thresholds (vision standard, blood alcohol levels, suspension periods, sleep and break intervals) plus behavioral advice.
Prerequisites: the license application and renewal rules from the first section, where vision certificates also appear.

Glossary: orthorater means the branch device that tests distance vision; bioptic telescopic lenses means special low-vision lenses that need a yearly SCDMV form; BAC means blood alcohol concentration; DUAC means driving with an unlawful alcohol concentration; implied consent means the advance agreement to alcohol testing that comes with a license; zero tolerance means no alcohol allowed in the circulatory system of young drivers.
