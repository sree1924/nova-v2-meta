Misconceptions: you do not stop and wait at an empty roundabout; you never change lanes inside a roundabout; when an emergency vehicle comes you do not stop inside the circle but exit first; at a ramp meter you pull all the way to the white line, not short of it, so the pavement sensors detect you; the roadside is not the place to argue a ticket; seeing one deer does not mean the danger has passed.
Mnemonics: slow, yield left, find a gap, stay in lane, signal right to leave; at a stop think park, off, belt on, window down, hands up top; deer eyes glow, so slow, honk and be ready to stop.
Glossary: roundabout means a circular intersection where traffic flows counterclockwise around a center island; ramp meter means a signal on a freeway entrance ramp that spaces out vehicles merging onto the freeway; diverging diamond interchange means an interchange that guides traffic through crossing exit and entrance paths; funeral procession means a line of vehicles traveling with the deceased.
Dynamic facts: the list of diverging diamond interchanges in Wisconsin can grow as new ones are built, so the named locations reflect this handbook edition.
