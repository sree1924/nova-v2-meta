## A — Exam authority for the the point system section (Missouri Class F operator license written test)
Issuer: the Missouri Department of Revenue publishes the Missouri Driver Guide [A01].
Source document: Missouri Driver Guide, with the front cover reading Revised August 2026 [A02].
Written test: 25 multiple-choice questions, all drawn from information in the guide [A03].
Passing standard: 20 of the 25 questions must be answered correctly, which is 80 percent [A03].
The driver examination has four parts (written, vision, road sign and driving skills) and is given by the Missouri State Highway Patrol; the Class F written test is not open book and leaves out the commercial vehicle chapter [A04].
Time limit: the guide does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MO-PTS-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: how points are added after a moving violation conviction, sample point values, the advisory, suspension and revocation thresholds, reinstatement, yearly point reduction, driving while suspended or revoked, failure to appear in court, and the school bus suspension.

Misconceptions: the first point threshold brings only an advisory letter, not a suspension; points reaching zero does not erase every conviction from the record; paying the ticket alone does not lift a failure to appear suspension without the reinstatement fee; driving while revoked is treated more harshly than driving while suspended.
Mnemonics: advisory, suspension, revocation as the three rungs of the point ladder; one-third, one-half, zero as the three yearly steps of point reduction.
Glossary: point accumulation advisory means the warning letter sent when points start to build up; suspension means a temporary loss of the driving privilege; revocation means cancellation of the driving privilege for a set period; FACT means Failure to Appear in Court for Traffic Violations; DLB means the Driver License Bureau.
Dynamic facts: point values, thresholds, suspension lengths and reinstatement fees are statutory and can change, and the bureau's mailing address and office hours are agency contact details to refresh from the Department of Revenue.
