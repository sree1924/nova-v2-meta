## A — Exam authority for the before you drive: tires, seat and mirrors, safety belts, air bags, child passengers, loads and driver assistance section (South Carolina Class D knowledge test)
Issuer: the South Carolina Department of Motor Vehicles (SCDMV) publishes the South Carolina Driver's Manual; the front cover names the agency and the manual [A01].
Source document: the front cover carries the edition line Revised 06/2024 [A01].
Required tests: an original license or an upgrade to a different class needs vision, knowledge and skills tests, and vision and knowledge tests can be taken at any SCDMV branch [A02].
Every first license test includes vision, knowledge and skills tests except the moped license, which has no skills test [A03].
Knowledge test content: traffic laws, road signs and driving safety rules [A04].
Test conduct: manuals, testing aids, written material and electronic devices are banned, and leaving the test area early or talking to anyone fails the test [A02].
Question count, passing score and time limit: the manual states none of them, so all three stay unknown and are never shown to learners (verify SC-PRE-A-COUNT, SC-PRE-A-PASS, SC-PRE-A-TIME).
Edition freshness: the issuer page links this Revised 06/2024 file, but a possibly newer unlinked upload exists on the issuer site and was not compared (verify SC-PRE-A-FRESH).
This section feeds the knowledge test: this section supplies knowledge-test items on tire pressure and tread checks, seat and mirror setup, the primary safety belt law, correct belt fit, air bags, the child restraint stages by age and height, unsecured loads and driver assistance features, including the features not allowed on the driving test.

## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: before you drive: tires, seat and mirrors, safety belts, air bags, child passengers, loads and driver assistance
Audience: every first-time South Carolina Class D applicant, with the child restraint stages also aimed at drivers who carry young children.

Misconceptions: seeing all of Lincoln's head in the penny test means the tread is worn out, not that it is fine; air bags add to safety belts and never replace them; a gauge reading above the doorframe number means letting air out; enhanced mirror settings are meant to hide your own vehicle's sides; assistance features do not drive the car, and some are not allowed on the driving test.
Mnemonics: the child restraint ladder runs rear-facing, forward-facing, booster, then belt, at under two, two and up, four and up, and eight years or fifty-seven inches; for tires, think cold tires, door sticker, match the numbers.
Glossary: PSI means pounds per square inch, the unit of tire pressure; blind spot means an area around the vehicle your mirrors and windows do not show; head restraint means the seat part that limits whiplash; supplemental restraint means a device, such as an air bag, that works alongside the safety belt; primary enforcement means an officer may stop a driver for a belt violation alone; ADAS means advanced driver assistance systems; unsecured load means cargo that can slide, shift, fall, sift or blow off a vehicle.
Dynamic facts: child passenger seat rules, the cited code section and the list of driver assistance features can change; confirm them on the Buckle Up, South Carolina website and the SCDMV website.
