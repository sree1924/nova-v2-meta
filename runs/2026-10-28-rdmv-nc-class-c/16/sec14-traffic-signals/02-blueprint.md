Misconceptions: yellow does not mean speed up; a dark signal is not a free pass but an all-way stop; a flashing yellow arrow allows the turn only after yielding; right on red requires a full stop first and is barred where signed; a dark ramp meter means merge normally, not stop; a flashing red hybrid beacon still requires waiting until the crossing is clear.
Mnemonics: green go with care, yellow stop if you safely can, red stop; arrows protect, circles permit; flashing red equals stop sign, flashing yellow equals warning sign; dark signal, treat as all-way stop.
Glossary: protected turn means a turn on a green arrow with opposing traffic held; unprotected turn means a turn on a circular green or flashing yellow arrow that requires yielding; ramp meter means a signal that controls entry from an entrance ramp onto a freeway; hybrid beacon means a signal that stays dark until a pedestrian or responder activates it; combination signal means a head showing both arrows and circular lights.
Dynamic facts: the penalty range for failing to yield to a pedestrian on a right turn on red is set by statute and is flagged as dynamic.
