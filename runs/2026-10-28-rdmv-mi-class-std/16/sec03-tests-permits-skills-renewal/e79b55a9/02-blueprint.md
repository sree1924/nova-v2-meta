## A — Exam authority, Tests, permits, skills test and renewal

This dossier supports first-time, never-licensed applicants preparing for the regular passenger-car written knowledge and road sign tests in Michigan. The issuing authority is the Michigan Department of State, led by the Secretary of State, and the source is the handbook What Every Driver Must Know. This section covers the vision and knowledge tests, the temporary instruction permit, the motorcycle permit, the driving skills test, restricted and probationary licenses, changing license information, renewal, and the decision to stop driving. The handbook pages do not state an official question count, passing score, test format or time limit for the Secretary of State knowledge test, so this dossier asserts no value for any of them and records each as an explicit unknown. Practice scoring follows only the accepted LearningLoop product policy, never an official threshold.
## B — Audience, Tests, permits, skills test and renewal

The audience is first-time, never-licensed applicants for a Michigan operator's license, reading at a plain-English level, plus newcomers and parents of teen drivers who use the same handbook. This section covers the vision and knowledge tests, the temporary instruction permit, the motorcycle permit, the driving skills test, restricted and probationary licenses, changing license information, renewal, and the decision to stop driving. It teaches what happens at the Secretary of State office before and after the knowledge test, how the permit and skills test work, and what probation, renewal and address-change rules apply after licensing. It assumes no prior driving knowledge and no prerequisites beyond literacy, so wording stays concrete, procedural and free of legal jargon throughout.
