Fire, ambulance, police and authorized emergency responders showing red, red-blue or red-white flashes with possible sirens or air-horns, which police may skip, command an immediate safe pull to the right edge and stop from every direction, after clearing any intersection, including opposite-lane two-way approaches and sirens of unknown location. FACT_ID: sec05-F11 | anchor: A02
The Move Over law demands care, reduced speed and, on multilane parkways, interstates and controlled roads, a lane shift away from shoulder-stopped emergency and hazard vehicles flashing emergency, amber or blue-green lights unless unsafe, plus blue-green-light vehicles and any shoulder-parked vehicle on parkways and controlled highways, all punishable as a moving violation. [A02]

FACT_ID: sec05-F12 | anchor: A02
Volunteer firefighters show blue, volunteer ambulance crews green, and plows, tow rigs, tow-blue combos, rural mail carriers and school buses amber, yet none count as authorized emergency vehicles, their drivers obey every law, and others yield only by courtesy. [A02]

FACT_ID: sec05-F13 | anchor: A03

FACT_ID: sec05-F14 | anchor: A03
Turn preparation slows down, scans all sides with motorcycle paranoia since unseen bikes cause most car-bike crashes, holds wheels straight until committed, tracks the tighter rear-wheel path, and guards pedestrians, cyclists, moped riders and low-hidden wheelchair, stroller and suitcase users, especially turning right. [A03]
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=36]

FACT_ID: sec05-F15 | anchor: A03

FACT_ID: sec05-F16 | anchor: A03
One-way-to-one-way lefts stage in the left lane and land in the entered road's left lane where two exist. [A03]
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=36]

FACT_ID: sec05-F17 | anchor: A03
One-way-to-two-way lefts stage left and enter right of center hugging the line, watching left-road traffic and hard-to-judge motorcycles. [A03]
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=36]

FACT_ID: sec05-F18 | anchor: A04
Two-way-to-two-way lefts stage in the right half near center, favor the intersection's left side to clear opposing left turners, enter right of center hugging the line, and watch oncoming, cross-lane and easily-missed motorcycle traffic. [A04]
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=37]

FACT_ID: sec05-F19 | anchor: A04
Two-way-to-one-way lefts stage in the right half near center, cut before the intersection's middle, and land in the entered left lane. [A04]
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=37]

FACT_ID: sec05-F20 | anchor: A04
Two-way-to-four-lane lefts stage in the right half near center, enter the left lane right of center, and drift outward when traffic allows. [A04]
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=37]

FACT_ID: sec05-F21 | anchor: A04
U-turns reverse direction, belong in lots and driveways with forward re-entry rather than on highways, start only from the leftmost pavement near the centerline never the right lane, and need green-arrow permission with yielding where allowed. [A04]
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=37]

FACT_ID: sec05-F22 | anchor: A04
U-turns die without five-hundred-foot visibility both ways, in city business districts, at no-U-turn signs, on limited-access expressways even with cross-paths, and in school zones, while narrow two-way streets allow three-point turnarounds that road tests may demand. [A04]
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=37]

FACT_ID: sec05-F23 | anchor: A05
Three-point turns run right-signal pull-over and stop, left-signal all-direction check, left sweep to the far curb, full-right back-up with a rear watch stopping shy of the curb, then full-left forward exit when safe. [A05]
Source: [A05 -> src:rdmv-ny-d:new-york-state-department-of-motor-vehic:handbook#pdf_page=38]

Every left stages near its own center yet lands differently, hugging the entered center on two-way arrivals and the left lane on one-way arrivals, one staging rule with two landing rules [A03].
