Misconceptions: missing an exit never justifies stopping or backing up, you always continue to the next exit; the entering driver, not freeway traffic, is responsible for a safe merge; the fire hydrant, crosswalk and railroad parking distances are three different numbers; the HOV restriction applies at the posted times, not around the clock.
Mnemonics: Hydrant, crosswalk, railroad — small, middle, large; on the freeway, miss it and move on.
Glossary: gore area means the space between a through roadway and an entrance or exit ramp; deceleration lane means an exit lane where you slow down off the main road; controlled access means a highway entered and left only by ramps; HOV lane means a high-occupancy-vehicle lane marked by white diamonds.
Dynamic facts: HOV hours are set by posted signs that can change, and the manual does not state HOV fine amounts, so check the signs and current Arizona law.
