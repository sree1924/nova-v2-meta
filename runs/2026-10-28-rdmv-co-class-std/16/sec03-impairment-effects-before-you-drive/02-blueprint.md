FACT_ID: sec03-F05 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec03-F06 | anchor: A01
Polydrug impairment means using two or more drugs at once, in any mix of alcohol, marijuana, prescription, over-the-counter or other drugs, and the mix can strengthen the effects. [A01]

FACT_ID: sec03-F07 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec03-F08 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec03-F09 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec03-F10 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec03-F11 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec03-F12 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec03-F14 | anchor: A01

Misconception: any headset is fine if the music is quiet; covering both ears is prohibited whatever the volume. [A01]
Mnemonic: Refuse once, lose for one, blow for two, meaning revocation then interlock years. [A01]
Glossary: Express consent means agreeing in advance, by driving, to chemical testing. [A01]
Glossary: Ignition interlock means a breath-testing device that blocks the engine when alcohol is detected. [A01]
Glossary: Polydrug impairment means impairment from two or more substances used together. [A01]
Glossary: Permissible inference means a level from which a court may infer impairment unless other evidence shows otherwise. [A01]
