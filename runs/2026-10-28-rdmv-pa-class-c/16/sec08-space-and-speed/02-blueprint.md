## A — Pennsylvania Class C knowledge-test authority for Managing space and speed
Issuer: the cover identifies Pennsylvania Department of Transportation and its Bureau of Driver Licensing [A01].
Document edition: the cover carries PUB 95 (4-21) English Version [A01].
Scope: the handbook prepares applicants for a non-commercial Class C license, including ordinary passenger vehicles, pickups and vans [A02].
The knowledge examination contains 18 multiple-choice items [A03].
Passing requires correct answers to 15 of the questions [A03].
No passing percentage is asserted because the cited sentence gives a correct-answer count (verify PA-sec08-A-PERCENT).
No test duration is asserted because the selected evidence provides no time limit (verify PA-sec08-A-TIME).
The current issuer page links this handbook, but the edition is old and no effective date is stated; changes since its publication require evidence review (verify PA-sec08-A-FRESH, PA-sec08-A-EFFECTIVE).
Practice mastery requirements are product policy and are separate from the issuer examination standard.
## B — Audience and prerequisites for Managing space and speed
Audience: first-time Pennsylvania applicants studying managing space and speed for ordinary non-commercial passenger driving.
Scope: Class C passenger-car preparation is the handbook's stated focus [A01].
Teaching level: plain-language recall followed by scenario practice; topic emphasis is pedagogic policy because no official topic weights were supplied.
Prerequisites: read the cited handbook rules before using practice material; commercial and motorcycle licensing are outside this assignment.
