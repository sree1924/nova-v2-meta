## B — Sharing the road

Audience: first-time Kansas non-commercial Class C applicants studying Sharing the road.
The selected scope excludes motorcycle and commercial licensing; those require their own manuals [A11].
Prerequisites and sequence: Recognize other road users before applying user-specific yielding, space and passing decisions. This is an editorial teaching order, with no claimed official domain weights.
Coverage follows the mapped handbook pages and preserves restrictions, exceptions and decision conditions in separate fact blocks.
