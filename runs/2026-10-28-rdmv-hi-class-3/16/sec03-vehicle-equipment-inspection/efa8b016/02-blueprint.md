Misconceptions: the yearly safety inspection is the rule, but ambulances are inspected every six months and a new vehicle waits two years after its first inspection; air bags protect in the first impact only, so belts are still required; the steering free-play limit is two inches with power steering and three inches without, not the same for both; a booster seat is for a child under eight who is also under four feet and under forty pounds, not for every child under eight regardless of size.

Mnemonics: Once a year for cars, twice a year for ambulances, two years free for new; four under four in a restraint, under eight on a booster when small; two inches of play with power, three without; a penny upside down tells you when the tread is gone.

Glossary: periodic motor vehicle inspection, or PMVI (Hawaii's yearly safety inspection); muffler cut-out (a device that routes exhaust around the muffler); antilock brakes (a system that prevents wheel lock-up and must not be pumped); free play (steering wheel movement before the wheels respond); booster seat (a belt-positioning seat for a child too small for the adult belt).

Dynamic facts: the inspection web address and the national highway safety web addresses printed on these pages can change and are not stated as stable knowledge; the intervals, measurements and prohibited equipment are stable rules of this printing.
