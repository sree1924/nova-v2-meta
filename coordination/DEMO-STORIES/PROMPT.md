# Demo stories — the one prompt for every lane (paste as-is to Argon, Sol, Astra; run through `muse exec` for Muse)

Founder's demo plan (2026-11-03): the belt is the centerpiece; every LLM lane that executed it tells its own story in its
own words; the stories are cut together over screen captures of the live product and the receipts; no human on camera.
The founder reviews and edits every script line. Narration voice is decided later.

## Paste from here ↓

For the PassLearnersPermit hackathon demo video, write YOUR story of executing the belt, in your own words.

Write it to `coordination/DEMO-STORIES/<your-lane>.md` (Argon → `argon-gemini.md`; Sol → `sol-gpt.md`; Astra → `astra-gpt.md`;
Muse → `muse-meta.md`). Rules:

1. First person, past tense, no word cap (long is right; we cut later), plain English a judge can follow in one listen.
2. Say what you executed on the belt: which stages, which validators accepted or HOLD-ed you, how many attempts, what it cost,
   what refused you and how you recovered. Mechanics, not adjectives.
3. **Every number or claim points at a file you can see on disk** (ledger, receipt, report, manifest, validator output).
   No file, no claim. Write "(verify)" if unsure. Nothing made up.
4. Two lines of feeling at most. No praise of any model, including yourself. No digs at anyone.
5. Lanes, for the record: Muse = Meta (author of Utah, fact-checker of 51 states, picture inspector, sign painter);
   Claude = Fable coordinator + Opus dossier workers; Sol/Astra = GPT; Argon = Gemini (decks, Piper narration, 4,987 lesson
   videos, road-signs module, store frame, CDN drop-off); Terra = GPT image lane (stopped 11-01).
6. End with **3 bullet "shots"** the editor can put on screen while you speak: a file path or URL + one line on what it shows.

Do not edit anything else. The app and the store are frozen. Reply with the path when done.
