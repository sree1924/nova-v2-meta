Misconceptions: yellow means general warning, but school and pedestrian warnings use fluorescent yellow-green instead; the circle warns that rails lie ahead while the crossbuck marks the crossing itself; the pennant hangs on the left, never on the right.

Mnemonic: red octagon, eight sides, full stop; triangle yields; circle means rails ahead; pentagon protects pupils.

Glossary: regulatory (an enforceable rule sign, usually white); warning (a hazard alert, usually yellow or fluorescent); guide (direction or information); crossbuck (the crossed-boards rail crossing marker); pennant (the sideways triangle for no-passing zones).

Dynamic facts: none in these pages; every color and shape rule above is stable handbook knowledge with no date-variable or place-variable values.
