## A — Exam authority for the license eligibility, documents and the three tests section (Minnesota Class D written test)
Issuer: the Minnesota Department of Public Safety, whose Driver and Vehicle Services division licenses drivers, permits the printing of the Minnesota Driver’s Manual [A01].
Source document: Minnesota Driver’s Manual, with the edition line June 2026 printed on the front cover [A02].
Written test format: multiple choice and true-or-false questions on Minnesota traffic laws and road signs, taken on paper or on a computer, with English, several other languages and American Sign Language at computer test stations [A03].
Passing standard: a score of 80 percent, and only one written test may be taken per day [A03].
Retest fee: after two consecutive failed written tests, a fee of $10 is charged for the third and every later attempt [A03].
Vision screening: normal or corrected vision of at least 20/40 and peripheral vision of at least 105 degrees [A03].
Question count: the manual does not state how many questions the written test has, so the count stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MN-LICENSE-A-COUNT).
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MN-LICENSE-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: who must hold a license and who is exempt, the reasons a license is refused, the identification, REAL ID and enhanced card documents, and how the written test, vision screening and road test are given, scored and retaken.

## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: license eligibility, documents and the three tests
Audience: first-time Minnesota Class D applicants, teens under eighteen and their parents, adults who were never licensed, and newcomers moving in from other states or countries.
