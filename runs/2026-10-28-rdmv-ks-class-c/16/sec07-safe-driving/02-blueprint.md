## A — Safe driving

Issuer: Kansas Department of Revenue, Division of Vehicles, publishes this non-commercial driver handbook; the department is identified in the introductory message [A02].
Edition: the cover labels this Kansas Driving Handbook as Revised February 2022 [A01].
Exam: the handbook describes a knowledge test using multiple-choice questions about signs, markings, Kansas law and safe driving [A94].
The handbook states a question count of 25, an 80 percent passing standard, and an untimed test; the usual completion estimate is not a deadline [A94].
This dossier covers Safe driving; its learning sequence and pedagogic emphasis are product choices, not an official topic weighting.
Effective date and total question-bank size remain unconfirmed (verify sec07-EFFECTIVE, sec07-BANK).
## B — Safe driving

Audience: first-time Kansas non-commercial Class C applicants studying Safe driving.
The selected scope excludes motorcycle and commercial licensing; those require their own manuals [A11].
Prerequisites and sequence: Practice searching before combining speed, space and communication decisions. This is an editorial teaching order, with no claimed official domain weights.
Coverage follows the mapped handbook pages and preserves restrictions, exceptions and decision conditions in separate fact blocks.
