Glossary: resident (a person meeting any licensing trigger); permit (a credential to carry while driving, like a license); liability insurance (coverage every driver must prove with a card); vision screening (the required eyesight check at application); knowledge test (the rules test every applicant must pass); Graduated Driver's License Program (staged licensing for drivers under eighteen); Skip the Trip (knowledge testing at home or school).
