## A — Exam authority for the insurance proof, REAL ID and renewal section (North Carolina regular Class C)
Issuer: the North Carolina Department of Transportation, Division of Motor Vehicles (NCDMV) publishes the handbook and administers the license tests [A01].
Source document: North Carolina Driver Handbook, back cover revision note Revised May 2025 [A03].
Test structure for an original license: vision check, traffic signs test, knowledge test on traffic laws and safe driving, and an on-road test, each passed separately [A02].
This section feeds the knowledge test: who must prove liability insurance, what REAL ID means, new-resident deadlines, and renewal, duplicate, address and name-change rules.
Not stated in the handbook: the knowledge-test question count, passing score and time limit; they stay unknown and are never shown to learners (verify NC-INS-A-EXAM).
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: insurance proof, REAL ID and renewal
Audience: first-time North Carolina applicants and new residents preparing for the regular Class C knowledge test, in English.
Why it matters to a first-timer: an original license application needs printed liability insurance proof [A01], and new residents face a short deadline to switch licenses [A02].
Level: administrative recall of deadlines, documents and card features; low conceptual load but many look-alike time limits.
Prerequisites: the licensing and testing section (which tests exist) and the graduated licensing section (provisional license levels).

Misconceptions: a phone photo of an insurance card does not count as proof; a non-REAL ID card is still a valid license for driving; renewal by mail is not available for full provisional licenses; the address-change deadline applies even though no new test is needed.
Mnemonics: Star means REAL; the three S changes — Settling in as a new resident, Street address and Surname — all share the same deadline.
Glossary: REAL ID means a federally compliant license or ID marked with a star; financial responsibility means proof of liability insurance; duplicate means a replacement license with the same expiration; nonresident means a person whose legal home is outside North Carolina.
Dynamic facts: fees, appointment options, phone numbers and online-service availability can change and are isolated as dynamic; confirm them on the DMV website.
