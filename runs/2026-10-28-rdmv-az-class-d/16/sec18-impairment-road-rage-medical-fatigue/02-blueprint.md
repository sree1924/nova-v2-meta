## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: impaired driving, aggressive driving, emotions, medical conditions, fatigue and revocation
Audience: first-time Arizona drivers and permit holders preparing for the written test in English, including drivers under twenty-one who fall under zero tolerance.
Why it matters to a first-timer: medications and alcohol impair drivers and the open container law covers drivers and passengers [A01], under-twenty-one drivers face a long suspension for any impairing trace and aggressive driving carries escalating penalties [A02], and medical conditions and fatigue carry reporting and safety duties [A03].

Mnemonics: Under twenty-one, zero means zero; aggressive driving is speeding plus two; sleepy means you are already late to stop.
Glossary: blood alcohol concentration means the share of alcohol in the blood, written as BAC; open container means an opened container of spirituous liquor; revocation means the removal of the driving privilege; SR-22 means a Certificate of Insurance filed to prove coverage; Traffic Survival School means the driver course required after a first aggressive driving offense.
Dynamic facts: impaired-driving penalties, the aggressive driving penalties, the minor-supply suspensions and the reinstatement fee are statutory or set by MVD and can change.
