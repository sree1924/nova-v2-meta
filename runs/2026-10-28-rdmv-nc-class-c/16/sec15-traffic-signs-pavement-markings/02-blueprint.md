Misconceptions: the octagon is never used for anything but a stop sign; a yield sign does not always require a stop, only when cross traffic demands it; black on orange means a work area, not an ordinary warning; a broken yellow line on the other side does not let you pass; the two-way left-turn lane is not a passing lane; an officer's signal overrides a green light.
Mnemonics: eight sides stop, three sides yield, five sides school; yellow divides opposing traffic, white divides same-direction traffic; odd goes north and south, even goes east and west; red markers mean wrong way.
Glossary: regulatory sign means a sign stating a rule you must obey; warning sign means a sign alerting you to a hazard ahead; guide sign means a sign giving routes, distances or services; pennant means the long triangular yellow no-passing sign on the left; two-way left-turn lane means a shared center lane for left turns in both directions.
Dynamic facts: school zone speed reductions and their hours are set locally and are posted on site, so none are stated here.
