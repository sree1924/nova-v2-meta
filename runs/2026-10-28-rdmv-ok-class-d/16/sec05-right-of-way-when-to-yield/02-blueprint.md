FACT_ID: sec05-F03 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec05-F04 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec05-F05 | anchor: A02

FACT_ID: sec05-F06 | anchor: A02

FACT_ID: sec05-F07 | anchor: A02

FACT_ID: sec05-F08 | anchor: A02
Siren or flashing-light police cars, fire engines, and ambulances own the yield with drivers pulling to the right edge clear of intersections until they pass, clearing a path when traffic bars pulling over, and still reaching the right shoulder even from the left lane under an officer stop, while lightless sirenless emergency vehicles earn no yield and every roadside vehicle including hazard-flashers deserves a leftward move or a slowdown [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0029]

FACT_ID: sec05-F09 | anchor: A02
At four-way stops and unsigned unsignaled crossings the first vehicle in owns the right-of-way, and simultaneous arrivals make the left driver yield to the right [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0029]

FACT_ID: sec05-F10 | anchor: A03
Intersections injure most, so slow early, scan both ways plus left again, roll in slow then accelerate clear, never brake mid-turn or mid-crossing, creep blind views to a stoppable pace, and refuse to enter blocked crossings until traffic ahead moves [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0030]

FACT_ID: sec05-F11 | anchor: A03
Flashing red bus lights or a red stop paddle mean boarding children and order a held stop until the bus rolls, the driver waves you on, or lights die with the sign folded, excused only across a different roadway, at controlled-access loading zones barring foot crossing, or as approaching traffic on a divided highway, and ignoring school-bus reds costs a mandatory one-year license revocation [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0030]

FACT_ID: sec05-F12 | anchor: A03
The Move Over or Bernardo-Mills Law under Title forty-seven section eleven-three hundred fourteen orders shifting a lane from stationary flashing-light roadside vehicles when safe or slowing with care, with multi-lane drivers leaving a buffer lane and two-lane drivers simply slowing, all to prevent distraction-fed secondary crashes [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ok-d:service-oklahoma-sok:handbook:pdf-0030]

FACT_ID: sec05-F13 | anchor: A04
