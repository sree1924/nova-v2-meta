## A — Exam authority for the backing, parking, passenger restraints, crashes, insurance and phones section (Minnesota Class D written test)
Issuer: the Minnesota Department of Public Safety, whose Driver and Vehicle Services division licenses drivers, permits the printing of the Minnesota Driver’s Manual [A01].
Source document: Minnesota Driver’s Manual, with the edition line June 2026 printed on the front cover [A02].
Written test format: multiple choice and true-or-false questions on Minnesota traffic laws and road signs, taken on paper or on a computer, with English, several other languages and American Sign Language at computer test stations [A03].
Passing standard: a score of 80 percent, and only one written test may be taken per day [A03].
Retest fee: after two consecutive failed written tests, a fee of $10 is charged for the third and every later attempt [A03].
Vision screening: normal or corrected vision of at least 20/40 and peripheral vision of at least 105 degrees [A03].
Question count: the manual does not state how many questions the written test has, so the count stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MN-TRAFFICB-A-COUNT).
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify MN-TRAFFICB-A-TIME).
This section feeds the written test: safe backing, the curb distance and no-parking distances, hill and parallel parking, highway parking lights, the seat belt law and its exemptions, child restraint stages, airbag spacing and hand position, crash duties, insurance proof and penalties, vehicle registration and the wireless messaging ban.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: backing, parking, passenger restraints, crashes, insurance and phones
Audience: first-time Minnesota Class D applicants, including parents who will carry children, and new residents who must register and insure a vehicle.
Why it matters to a first-timer: the no-parking distances [A01], the child restraint stages [A04] and the crash duties [A05] are frequent written test items, and the road test includes parking.
Level: moderate; several look-alike distances and ages (ten, twenty, thirty and fifty feet; two, four, nine and thirteen years) make confusion the main risk.
Prerequisites: the backing rules at the end of traffic laws part one and the seat belt equipment rule in the vehicle chapter.
