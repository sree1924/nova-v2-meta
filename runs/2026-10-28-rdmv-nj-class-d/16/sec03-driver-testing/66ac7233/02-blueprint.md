## A — Exam authority for the driver testing and the road test section (New Jersey Class D basic license knowledge test)
Issuer: the New Jersey Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC) publishes the New Jersey Driver Manual, which prepares applicants for the knowledge portion of the driver test [A01].
Source document: New Jersey Driver Manual, with 2026 printed on the front cover [A02].
Knowledge test: 50 questions, plus a survey question about organ donation [A03].
Passing standard: 80 percent, which means 40 of the 50 questions must be answered correctly [A04].
Vision screening is required for every motorist, and basic license applicants need 20/50 vision with or without corrective lenses [A04].
Time limit: the manual does not state one, so it stays unknown and is never shown to learners (verify NJ-TEST-A-TIME).
This section feeds the knowledge test: this is the testing chapter itself: ID and REAL ID requirements at the test, vision rules, the knowledge test's length, languages, interpreters and passing standard, transfer rules, the road test checklist and the vehicle problems that cause rejection.
Practice quizzes follow the product mastery policy from the learning loop, which is not an official exam threshold.
## B — Audience, level and prerequisites: driver testing and the road test
Audience: every first-time New Jersey Class D applicant about to sit the knowledge test and book a road test, plus transfer and out-of-country applicants.
Why it matters to a first-timer: this chapter states what the knowledge test looks like and what must be brought to it [A01], and what must be in place before a road test appointment [A05].
Level: procedural recall with a few exact thresholds (question count, passing percentage, vision standards, practice minimums); low conceptual load.
Prerequisites: the license-law, identity-document and GDL sections of the first chapter.
