## C — Passing limits, hills, night driving and weather

FACT_ID: sec10-F01 | anchor: A01
Do not pass on a hill or curve that blocks your view, within 100 feet of an intersection or railroad crossing, or within 100 feet of a bridge, viaduct or tunnel when your view is blocked. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F02 | anchor: A01
Do not pass a bicyclist unless you can keep a buffer of at least three feet, counting mirrors and other projections; a brief crossing of a solid yellow line to do so is allowed when no one is oncoming and the view ahead is clear. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F03 | anchor: A01
It is illegal to pass in a marked no-passing zone or to go over the posted speed limit while passing. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F04 | anchor: A01
It is illegal to pass a school bus whose red lights are flashing and whose stop arm is out. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F05 | anchor: A01
Passing is illegal closer than 100 feet to an intersection or a railroad crossing, and on a bridge, curve or hill that limits what you can see. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F06 | anchor: A01
Hills and curves can hide hazards even on roads you know, so be ready to stop when one blocks your view of the road. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F07 | anchor: A01
If you cannot see past the top of a hill, slow to a suitable speed until you crest it and can see the road again. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F08 | anchor: A01
Slow down before you enter a curve, because taking it too fast lets the tires lose grip and skid; start speeding up only after the middle of the curve. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F09 | anchor: A01
Dawn and dusk can be riskier than daytime because of poor visibility, the small area your headlights light up and glare from headlights combined with fog lights. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F10 | anchor: A01
Colorado law requires headlights from sunset to sunrise and whenever you cannot see 1,000 feet ahead; keeping low beams or daytime running lights on all the time lowers risk. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F11 | anchor: A01
A vehicle parked beside the road, attended or not, must show parking lights from sunset to sunrise or when visibility is under 1,000 feet, but never drive on parking lights alone. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F12 | anchor: A01
Use high beams in rural areas and on open highways away from cities. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F13 | anchor: A01
Dim high beams, or low beams used with fog lights, before you come within 500 feet of an oncoming vehicle so its driver is not blinded. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F14 | anchor: A01
When you are within 200 feet behind another vehicle, you must use low beams with fog lights turned off. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F15 | anchor: A01
Do not stare into oncoming headlights; lower your eyes below the glare and steer by the painted edge line until the vehicle passes, then look up again. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F16 | anchor: A01
In fog at night use low beams or fog lights, because high beams reflect back at you as if shining on a mirror. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F17 | anchor: A01
Leave more following distance at night and on roads you do not know. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F18 | anchor: A01
At night watch for vehicles and bicyclists without lights, slow down in dark areas where people or animals may be moving, and look for the single headlight of a bicycle or motorcycle. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F19 | anchor: A01
Bad weather affects every vehicle, whether front, rear, four-wheel or all-wheel drive, and the two key responses to rain, fog, ice, high wind or dust are slowing down and leaving more following distance. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F20 | anchor: A01
In bad weather turn on headlights when visibility is poor, and never use cruise control on a wet or icy highway. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F21 | anchor: A01
Do not drive through large pools of standing water; if the road is flooded, take another route. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F22 | anchor: A01
Hydroplaning happens when tires ride on a film of water on the pavement, which can wipe out traction and steering; worn tires raise the risk, so check tread depth regularly. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F23 | anchor: A01
If you start to hydroplane, lift your foot off the gas and steer slowly in the direction the car is sliding until control returns, which helps line the tires up with the vehicle. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F24 | anchor: A01
During a hydroplane, never jerk the wheel, since overcorrecting can flip the car, and do not brake or turn until the tires grip again. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-F25 | anchor: A01
Roads covered with snow, packed snow or ice are extremely hazardous. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

Headlight distances differ by purpose: lights on when visibility is under 1,000 feet, dim for oncoming traffic within 500 feet, low beams when following within 200 feet. [A01]

FACT_ID: sec10-R03 | anchor: A01

Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-R04 | anchor: A01
In a hydroplane you steer gently toward the direction of the slide, not away from it, and you ease off the gas instead of braking. [A01]
Why it matters: group weather; difficulty three; prereq sec10-F23 and sec10-F24. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-R05 | anchor: A01
In a curve you slow down before entry and accelerate only after the middle, never while entering. [A01]
Why it matters: group hills and curves; difficulty two; prereq sec10-F08. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-R06 | anchor: A01
The no-passing distance near intersections and railroad crossings is 100 feet, the same figure used for bridges, viaducts and tunnels with a blocked view. [A01]
Why it matters: group passing; difficulty two; prereq sec10-F01 and sec10-F05. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

FACT_ID: sec10-R07 | anchor: A01
Four-wheel or all-wheel drive does not exempt you from slowing down and adding following distance in bad weather. [A01]
Why it matters: group weather; difficulty two; prereq sec10-F19. [A01]
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-co-s:colorado-department-of-revenue-division-:handbook:pdf-0019]

Mnemonic: Slow down, back off: the two weather rules. [A01]
