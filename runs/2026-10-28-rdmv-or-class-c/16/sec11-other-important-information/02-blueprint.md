Misconceptions: a hospital birth certificate is not accepted proof of birth date; an out-of-state license works as identity proof for a much shorter time after expiry than an Oregon card; personal mail does not prove your address; a police report does not take the place of your own DMV collision report; a crash caused by distraction or fatigue is a preventable collision, not an accident; fifty practice hours are enough only with an approved traffic safety course.
Mnemonics: permit at fifteen, license at sixteen; for teens, six months on a permit and one hundred hours, or half the hours plus a course; supervisor twenty-one with three years licensed; stop, help, swap, stay, report.
Glossary: Class C means the standard non-commercial privilege for cars, vans, pickups, mopeds and autocycles; instruction permit means the learner permit held before a license; Personal Injury Protection means coverage for medical and related costs after a crash; uninsured motorist coverage means coverage when the other driver has no insurance; preventable collision means a crash caused by driver error rather than chance.
Dynamic facts: insurance minimums, document expiry windows, collision report thresholds and fees can change with law and DMV policy, so they are shown as printed in the current manual and re-checked each edition.
