Misconceptions: refusing a breath test does not avoid penalties, it carries its own surcharge and interlock requirement; an underage driver does not need to reach the adult limit to be charged; having no license yet does not cancel an underage suspension, it simply starts later; clearing snow is required even if nothing would have flown off.
Mnemonics: interlock ladder after a first DUI is three months, then seven to twelve months, then twelve to fifteen months as BAC climbs; the interlock trips above point-oh-five; three suspensions in three years makes a habitual offender; doubled fines in work zones and sixty-five zones.
Glossary: implied consent means agreeing to a breath test just by driving on New Jersey roads; ignition interlock device means a breathalyzer wired to the ignition that blocks starting after drinking; IDRC means Intoxicated Driver Resource Center, where alcohol offenders are detained and evaluated; habitual offender means a driver suspended three times in three years; surcharge means a yearly MVC charge on top of court fines.
Dynamic facts: fines, fees, surcharges and interlock terms in this chapter are set by statute and can change; confirm current amounts on the official MVC website.
