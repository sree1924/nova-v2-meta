FACT_ID: sec10-F022 | anchor: P41
After an engine power loss, use steady brake pressure rather than hard braking, reduce speed and pull off the road.

FACT_ID: sec10-F023 | anchor: P41
Once stopped after power loss, try restarting; if unsuccessful, raise the hood, activate warning flashers and request help.

FACT_ID: sec10-F024 | anchor: P41

FACT_ID: sec10-F025 | anchor: P41

FACT_ID: sec10-F026 | anchor: P41

FACT_ID: sec10-F027 | anchor: P41

FACT_ID: sec10-F029 | anchor: P41
