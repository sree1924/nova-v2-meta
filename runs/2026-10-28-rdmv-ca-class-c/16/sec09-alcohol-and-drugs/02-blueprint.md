FACT_ID: sec09-F03 | anchor: A01

FACT_ID: sec09-F04 | anchor: A01
Nobody may drink alcohol or use cannabis while driving or riding; carried containers stay sealed and unopened, open ones ride in trunks or non-passenger spots never glove boxes, with bus, taxi, camper, and motorhome passengers exempt [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-ca-c:california-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0078]

FACT_ID: sec09-F05 | anchor: A02
Illegal BAC starts at 0.08% for drivers over 21, 0.01% for drivers under 21, 0.01% at any age on DUI probation, and 0.04% for commercial licenses and paid passenger hauling; below-limit driving can still be unsafe, and DUI convictions need no BAC reading at all [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ca-c:california-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0079]

FACT_ID: sec09-F06 | anchor: A02
BAC tables estimate levels by drink count, gender, and weight with 0.01% subtracted per 40 drink-free minutes, one drink equaling 1.5 ounces of 80-proof liquor, 12 ounces of 5% beer, or 5 ounces of 12% wine, fewer than 5 in 100 exceeding the table, and even a single drink impairing [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ca-c:california-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0079]

FACT_ID: sec09-F07 | anchor: A02

Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-ca-c:california-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0079]

FACT_ID: sec09-F08 | anchor: A03
California drivers consent to breath, blood, or urine testing on DUI suspicion, PAS or breath tests never excusing drug bloodwork, refusal suspending or revoking driving, arrests triggering Admin Per Se suspension with seized licenses swapped for 30-day temps, and hearings requestable within 10 days of arrest [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ca-c:california-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0080]

FACT_ID: sec09-F09 | anchor: A03
DUI convictions suspend or revoke driving for a year plus a DUI program, SR 22 or SR 1P insurance proof, reissue fees, and possible ignition interlocks, with up to 6 months of jail, fines, impound and storage bills, and civil suits for injury or death on top, convictions haunting records for 10 years with harsher repeats [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-ca-c:california-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0080]

FACT_ID: sec09-F10 | anchor: A04
Drivers under 21 carry no alcohol without a companion 21 or older, containers full sealed and unopened with opened ones in trunks or non-passenger spots, except workers for off-site liquor licensees with closed containers [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ca-c:california-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0081]

FACT_ID: sec09-F11 | anchor: A04
Under-21 drivers caught with alcohol face impounds up to 30 days plus court fines and year-long suspensions, or year-long licensing delays for the unlicensed [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ca-c:california-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0081]

FACT_ID: sec09-F12 | anchor: A04
Officers suspecting under-21 drinking can demand handheld, PAS, or chemical tests; DUI convictions at 0.01% or higher revoke driving for a year plus a licensed DUI program, PAS readings at 0.05% or higher force breath or blood tests, and confirmed 0.05% or higher means arrest and suspension [A04].
Source: [A04 -> src:rdmv-ca-c:california-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0081]
