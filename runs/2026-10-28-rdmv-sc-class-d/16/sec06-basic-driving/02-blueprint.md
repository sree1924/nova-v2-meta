Misconceptions: the two and ten o'clock grip is not recommended in air bag vehicles; mirrors do not show the area right behind the vehicle, so you turn and look through the rear window when backing; the steering wheel turns the way you want to go even in reverse; one-hand steering is not for normal forward driving.
Mnemonics: push/pull for normal speeds, hand-over-hand for slow turns, one hand only for backing or a quick reach; for starting, think brake, park, key.
Glossary: gear selector means the lever that chooses park, drive or reverse; push/pull steering means hand-to-hand steering in which the hands never cross the wheel; hand-over-hand steering means crossing the hands over the top of the wheel for low-speed turns; blind spot means an area around the vehicle where your view is blocked.
