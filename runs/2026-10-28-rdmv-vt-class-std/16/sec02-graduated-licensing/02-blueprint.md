Misconceptions:
- A junior driver with a supervising adult aboard may fill every seat and more: false, a supervisor removes the passenger count limit, but passengers may never exceed the number of safety belts.
- Any relative counts as immediate family in the second passenger stage: false, the law names siblings and parents only.
- A recall is a suspension with a reinstatement fee: false, a recalled permit or junior license is restored when the term ends, after any required re-examination, with no reinstatement fee.
- Texting as a junior operator only costs points: false, it brings a thirty day recall, a fine of at least one hundred dollars and at least two points.
- A teen who turns eighteen still must wait a full year to drive for work: false, the employment ban ends at one year or at age eighteen, whichever comes first.

Mnemonics:
- Two for the permit, one on the permit, six before the junior: two clean years to apply, one year holding the permit, six clean months before the junior license.
- Hire and hired, ninety; board and texting, thirty: the recall terms for passengers for hire or employer driving versus a board recommendation or texting.
- Three alone, three with family, then anyone belted: the three stages of junior license passengers.

Glossary:
- Graduated driver's license law: the Vermont law that moves new drivers through supervised permit, restricted junior license and full license stages
- Provisional license: a learner's permit or junior driver's license, which can be recalled for the listed junior operator violations
- Recall: a temporary withdrawal of a provisional license for a set term, restored without a reinstatement fee when the term ends
- Immediate family members: for the second passenger stage, siblings and parents only
- Nighttime driving: driving from thirty minutes after sunset to thirty minutes before sunrise, the period that counts toward the ten night practice hours
