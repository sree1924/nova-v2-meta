## C — Sharing the road, blind spots

FACT_ID: sec09-F01 | anchor: A01
Set the inside mirror to frame the whole back window and the side mirrors to barely show your vehicle; clear blind spots with a quick head turn and shoulder glance before lane changes and passes [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F02 | anchor: A01
Never linger in another driver's blind spot: speed up or drop back instead [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F03 | anchor: A01
Vulnerable users cover pedestrians, bicyclists, wheelchair, skateboard, skate, and scooter users, animals, and animal-drawn vehicles; distracted or careless driving around them is illegal, and you must drive to cover their mistakes [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F04 | anchor: A01
Guard pedestrians near schools, playgrounds, and homes; let them fully cross before you turn; never pass at a crosswalk; stop and stay stopped until they clear your stopped lane [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F05 | anchor: A01
Come to a full stop for cane or guide-dog users and for any pedestrian lawfully crossing; failing to stop and causing serious injury or death brings a Class 1 misdemeanor [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F06 | anchor: A01
Bicyclists share motor-vehicle right-of-way, may hold the lane center when needed for left turns or narrow lanes, ride all public roads except interstates and most freeways, and may approach from either direction on sidewalks, paths, and two-way bike lanes [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F07 | anchor: A01
Pass cyclists with at least three feet of clearance, changing lanes where the lane cannot spare it; motor vehicles may never use marked bike lanes, even to pass [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F08 | anchor: A01
Bicycles slip easily into blind spots: check for them before pull-outs, lane changes, turns, backing, door openings, and intersections, give room, and expect sudden stops [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F09 | anchor: A01
Look twice for motorcycles, since drivers expect four-wheelers and miss bikes until too late in over half of car-motorcycle crashes; check blind spots before every maneuver [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F10 | anchor: A01
Never tailgate or lane-share with a motorcycle: add an extra second of following distance, double it in bad weather, expect sudden in-lane moves, and yield with double looks since bike speed and distance deceive [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec09-F11 | anchor: A02
Around Norfolk light rail The Tide, with 27 crossings over 7.4 miles, expect trains on any track at any time, never circle lowered gates or stop, pass, or shift on tracks, look both ways before turning across, and cross only with room to clear fully [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0023]

FACT_ID: sec09-F12 | anchor: A02
Low-speed vehicles top out at 21 to 25 MPH and may run only on roads posted 35 MPH or less for licensed drivers or supervised permit holders, meeting federal safety plus Virginia registration and insurance rules; golf carts do not qualify [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0023]

FACT_ID: sec09-F13 | anchor: A02
Trucks, tractor-trailers, buses, and RVs run longer, higher, and wider with slow acceleration and long stopping and turning needs; their danger areas are called No-Zones on the sides, front, and rear [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0023]

FACT_ID: sec09-F14 | anchor: A02
Side No-Zones on trucks, buses, and RVs dwarf car blind spots because of wide turns: if you cannot see the driver's face in his mirror, he cannot see you [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0023]

FACT_ID: sec09-F15 | anchor: A02
Rear No-Zones hide your car from the truck driver and hide ahead traffic from you, leaving no escape at sudden stops; always hold a safe following distance [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0023]

FACT_ID: sec09-F16 | anchor: A02
Front No-Zones punish cutting in too soon or slowing after passing, since trucks need nearly twice car stopping time and room; combinations run up to 65 feet and may need over half a mile to pass, so return only with the whole truck front in your mirror and hold speed [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0023]

FACT_ID: sec09-F17 | anchor: A02
Trucks, buses, and RVs swing wide to turn with blind spots up to 20 feet ahead of the cab and about 200 feet behind; never squeeze between them and the curb or another vehicle [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0023]

FACT_ID: sec09-F18 | anchor: A02
Light and medium trailers ride on safety chains and hitches with large mirrors needed; towing doubles passing, stopping, accelerating, and turning effort, and No-Zones apply [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-va-s:virginia-department-of-motor-vehicles:handbook:pdf-0023]
