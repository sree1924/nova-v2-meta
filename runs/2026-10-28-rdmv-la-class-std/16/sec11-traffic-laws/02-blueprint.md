# Audience and boundaries

This section supports first-time Louisiana passenger-vehicle applicants studying traffic laws, speed, stopping, turning and right of way. The study scope follows the registered Class E route; the guide also contains other vehicle classes, which are outside this product scope. # Accuracy and teaching notes

School-bus exceptions must retain the difference between one-lane-each-way center-turn roads and wider four- or five-lane roads; do not flatten the rule to all medians. Current statute and traffic-stop procedure remain legal/currentness review debt. Memory cue, authored product policy: “Signal early, choose the lane, yield safely; bus rules depend on the roadway”. The cue retrieves complete cited rules and does not replace their conditions.
Terminology: An all-way stop applies a stop requirement to every approach. A shared center turn lane serves left turns from both directions and is distinct from a physical median.
Misconception to correct: Every center turn lane exempts opposite traffic from stopping for a school bus, or legal priority guarantees a safe path. The roadway configuration and the presence of other users still matter.
