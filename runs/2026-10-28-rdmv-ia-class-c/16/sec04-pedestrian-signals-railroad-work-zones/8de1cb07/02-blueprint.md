Misconceptions: flashing red is a stop sign while flashing yellow only asks caution; a steady green arrow still requires giving way to traffic already inside; passive crossings carry no lights or gates at all, so looking is the only protection.

Mnemonic: solid red stops, flashing red stops too; yellow means decide, green means go when clear; arrows add turning rules to the same colors.

Glossary: conveyance (a human-powered device counting as a pedestrian, from wheelchairs to scooters); active crossing (rails guarded by lights or gates); passive crossing (rails marked only by a crossbuck with a stop or yield sign); channelizing device (a cone, drum, or barrier steering traffic through work); flagger (a vest-wearing worker directing traffic with paddles).
