## C — Vehicle Inspection and Registration

FACT_ID: sec02-F01 | anchor: A01
Every motor vehicle registered in Texas passes a yearly check at an official inspection station, motorcycles, mopeds, scooters, and autocycles included, with insurance proof for that vehicle shown at inspection time [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0021]

FACT_ID: sec02-F02 | anchor: A01
A single valid windshield sticker now proves both registration and inspection together, the station prints a passing Vehicle Inspection Report into an electronic database the motor vehicle department confirms at registration, and that report stays valid ninety days from the inspection month for registration purposes [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0021]

FACT_ID: sec02-F03 | anchor: A01
Safe cars need a foot brake that halts within twenty-five feet from twenty miles per hour plus a parking brake strong enough to stop and hold the car [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0021]

FACT_ID: sec02-F04 | anchor: A01
Lighting rules demand two headlights with a high-beam indicator, two taillights or one on pre-nineteen-sixty cars, two working brake lights or one on pre-nineteen-sixty cars with all three lit where a center high lamp is fitted, a white rear plate light, white-or-amber front and red rear parking lights, two red side reflectors set fifteen to sixty inches high and seen six hundred feet out or three hundred fifty on pre-nineteen-sixty cars, and electric turn signals on most trailers and vehicles except motorcycles and narrow pre-nineteen-sixty cars under eighty inches wide [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0021]

FACT_ID: sec02-F05 | anchor: A01
Further required gear includes a horn heard two hundred feet away, emission controls on nineteen-sixty-eight and later models, safety glass for new and replacement panes, front and rear plates except single-plate dealer and commercial issues, wipers, a mirror reaching two hundred feet behind, slow-moving emblems on rigs capped at twenty-five miles per hour, front belts where anchorages came factory-fitted, tires holding two thirty-seconds of an inch, and a sound fuel cap on gasoline vehicles aged two to twenty-four years outside antique, circus, slow, motorcycle, and alternate-fuel exceptions [A01].
Source: [A01 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0021]

FACT_ID: sec02-F06 | anchor: A02
Banned gear covers front red lights, bells, sirens, exhaust whistles, muffler cutouts, loads jutting over three inches left or six inches right, front flashing red lights, and radar-jamming devices, with emergency vehicles and school or church buses keeping their narrow allowances [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec02-F07 | anchor: A02
No car may ride with its body below the lowest wheel rims, while optional spotlights dim for oncoming traffic or cast fifty feet in headlight failure, twin cowl lights and single side courtesy lights glow amber or white, twin backup lights never burn moving forward, hazard flashers blink amber-white ahead and amber-red behind in unison, up to three extra driving lamps sit twelve to forty-two inches high, and any tinting obeys state rules [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec02-F08 | anchor: A02
Vehicles register in the owner's home county with the sticker on the windshield or the rear plate of a bike or moped, fresh purchases register within thirty days, nonresident farm trucks may draw thirty-day harvest permits, buyer tags last sixty days, dealer and manufacturer plates still need inspection, and cardboard dealer tags cover demos, hauls, and service without inspection yet never driving exams [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec02-F09 | anchor: A02
Farm-registered vehicles may also ferry family to church, school, doctors, and household needs but never serve gainful employment, and registration questions go to the county tax assessor-collector or the motor vehicle department [A02].
Source: [A02 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0022]

FACT_ID: sec02-F10 | anchor: A03
Temporary highway use needs no registration, inspection, or plates for farm tractors, light farm and fertilizer or cottonseed trailers at four thousand pounds or less, farm implements, power sweepers, certain golf carts, electric bicycles, and personal mobility devices [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0023]

FACT_ID: sec02-F11 | anchor: A03
Well-drilling and construction machinery plus heavier farm, cotton, and fertilizer trailers between four thousand and thirty-four thousand pounds run on an annual five-dollar distinguishing plate with the law's extra conditions [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0023]

FACT_ID: sec02-F12 | anchor: A03
New Texans register every owned vehicle before seeking a license, fresh residents and new jobholders get thirty days of operation first, registration demands a Texas inspection report plus VIN verification and insurance proof with no insurance meaning no registration, and the county receipt doubles as registration proof at licensing [A03].
Source: [A03 -> src:rdmv-tx-c:texas-department-of-public-safety-driver:handbook:pdf-0023]
