Misconceptions: an unposted curb is not automatically legal parking since colored curbs and signs also ban it; hazard lights on a stopped car trigger the move-over duty just like an emergency rig; clearing drivable cars after a no-injury crash is the law, not a courtesy.

Mnemonic: hydrant five, sign ten, station twenty, rails fifty; see flashers, change lanes or slow; no hurt, move it.

Glossary: double parking (halting alongside another stopped or parked car); move-over law (the duty to change lanes or slow for stopped flashing-light vehicles); steer-it-clear-it (the duty to clear operable cars from lanes after an injury-free crash); hydrant red zone (a marked no-park area at a fire hydrant).
